FAQ
What is a yieldcoin?
A yieldcoin is a token backed by cash-equivalent real-world assets like US Treasuries that also accrues yield daily. USDY is a yieldcoin, but not a stablecoin. Like stablecoins, USDY operates as a permissionless bearer asset that can be transferred to without needing to be onboarded first. Unlike stablecoins, however, USDY accrues yield every day.
Learn more
about yieldcoins
Who is eligible to buy USDY?
USDY is currently accessible to non-US individuals and institutional investors. Additional eligibility restrictions apply. You can learn more about eligibility and availability requirements
here
.
Who can mint and redeem USDY?
USDY is offered under a "Regulation S" exemption from the SEC. This exemption requires that Ondo only offer this product to non-US individuals or entities. Likewise, USDY can only be redeemed by non-US persons individuals or entities. Additional restrictions apply.
Learn more
about who can mint and redeem USDY
How can USDY be lower risk than stablecoins?
We have worked closely with our attorneys, among other specialists, to design the structure to comply with securities laws, tax laws, anti-money laundering laws, and other regulatory requirements. USDY is a type of bearer note that is distributed to non-US investors in accordance with Regulation S under the US Securities Act of 1933, as amended.
Creating a product that meets these requirements does impose some limitations, however—for example, we can only redeem to USD via bank wire to non-US bank accounts.

about how USDY is lower risk than stablecoins
