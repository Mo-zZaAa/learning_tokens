# Flux Finance

A fork of Compound v2, Flux Finance is a protocol we developed as an onchain Treasury repo marketplace, allowing users to borrow and lend stablecoins backed by high quality collateral such as OUSG (Ondo's US-Treasuries-linked token). We have since sold the protocol to the Ondo Foundation.

Find out more at [https://fluxfinance.com](https://fluxfinance.com)
