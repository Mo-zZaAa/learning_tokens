FAQ
Who is eligible to buy OUSG?
Organizations or individuals in eligible countries - including the US - who have onboarded to our Qualified-Access Funds can invest in, redeem, or transfer, or receive OUSG. See all the ways you can qualify
here
.
How is OUSG backed?
The significant majority of this portfolio is currently in the BlackRock USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund (BUIDL), with the remainder in BlackRock's FedFund (TFDXX), bank deposits, and USDC for liquidity purposes. The portfolio may, in the future, include other US Treasury funds and/or direct investments in US Treasuries.
How do I invest?
To invest, you must first onboard to our Qualified-Access Funds. Once you complete onboarding, you can invest either USD via wire transfer or via any supported stablecoin.
What is the minimum I can invest in OUSG?
