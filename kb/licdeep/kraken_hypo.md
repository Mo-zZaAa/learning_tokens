## 가설 1. 인가를 가진 회사는 주인을 바꿔 가며 돈다

### 받치는 사실
- Small Exchange(2019 출범, 2020-03-10 DCM 지정) [LDKR-009](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/ig-group-acquires-the-small-exchange-from-foris-dax-markets/) [F-2050](https://mondovisione.com/news/cftc-designates-small-exchange-inc-as-a-contract-market-2020310)
  - IG가 지분 39% 보유 → 2021-12 매각 합의, 2022-03 Crypto.com 모회사 Foris DAX Markets에 넘김 [LDKR-010](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/ig-group-acquires-small-exchange-from-cryptocoms-parent/)
  - 2023-04-03 IG가 Foris DAX에서 다시 삼 [LDKR-009](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/ig-group-acquires-the-small-exchange-from-foris-dax-markets/)
  - 2025-10-16 IG가 Payward에 1억 달러에 팖 [F-2028](https://financialreports.eu/filings/8026393/content)
- NinjaTrader의 선물 중개 인가(FCM)
  - 2021-01-13 NinjaTrader가 비청산 FCM TransAct Futures를 사서 얻음 [LDKR-003](https://ninjatrader.com/News/NinjaTrader-Acquires-TransAct-Futures)
  - NinjaTrader 자체는 2020-01-06 사모펀드 Long Ridge가 다수 지분 투자 [LDKR-002](https://ninjatrader.com/News/NinjaTrader-Announces-Majority-Growth-Investment-led-by-Long-Ridge-Equity-Partners), 2025-03-20 Kraken에 팖 [LDKR-001](https://ninjatrader.com/News/Kraken-to-Acquire-NinjaTrader-Introducing-the-Next-Era-of-Professional-Trading) [F-1117](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-to-acquire-ninjatrader)
  - 2022-06 NinjaTrader는 Coinbase 첫 선물의 외부 판매 중개사였음 [F-2112](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/trading/coinbase-to-target-retail-traders-with-first-bitcoin-derivatives/)
- 키프로스 투자회사(인가 342/17, 2017-11-13 인가): 옛 이름 Pacific Union Group, BrightFX Capital, Greenfields Wealth, 법적 이름 변경 2023-12-18과 2025-01-24 [F-1810](https://www.cysec.gov.cy/en-GB/entities/investment-firms/cypriot/45914/) [F-1811](https://api.gleif.org/api/v1/lei-records/2138006O8D7SV36PWU16)
- (비교) Robinhood 선물 중개사는 옛 Marex North America(2010 FCM 등록) [F-1236](https://www.nfa.futures.org/BasicNet/#0424278). Coinbase 선물 거래소는 FairX(LMX Labs) 인수 [F-1122](https://www.cftc.gov/IndustryOversight/IndustryFilings/TradingOrganizations)
- Bitnomial은 2025-01 자체 청산소를 가동하기 전 MIAX Futures에 청산을 맡김(판단) [F-2047](https://www.marketsmedia.com/bitnomial-to-launch-cftc-regulated-digital-asset-derivatives-ccp/)

### 반대 사실
- Crypto Facilities: 2015 창업자가 세워 FCA 인가를 받은 회사를 2019 Kraken이 처음 삼(창업자가 판 첫 매각) [F-1779](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-acquires-crypto-derivatives-trading-platform-and-index-provider-crypto-facilities-in-nine-figure-deal-official-press-release) [LDKR-044](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-acquires-crypto-derivatives-trading-platform-and-index-provider-crypto-facilities-in-nine-figure-deal-official-press-release)
- Bitnomial: 창업자가 거래소(첫 제출 2016-09-30, 지정 2020-04-17)와 청산소(신청 2022-04, 등록 2023-12-15)를 직접 받은 뒤 2026 Payward에 팖 [F-2048](https://bitnomial.com/exchange/regulatory/cftc/orders/bitnomial-exchange-cftc-order.pdf) [F-2045](https://bitnomial.com/exchange/regulatory/cftc/orders/bitnomial-clearinghouse-cftc-order.pdf) [LDKR-013](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-to-acquire-bitnomial)
- BCM: 2017 공동 창업자가 세운 회사를 2023 Kraken에 넘김 [LDKR-042](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-to-acquire-dutch-crypto-broker-bcm-in-commitment-to-grow-european-business)
- Kraken이 직접 받은 인가: 와이오밍 SPDI(2020), 브로커딜러(2024), 버뮤다 Class F(2024), 아일랜드 MiCA(2025), 두바이 VARA(2026) [F-1719](https://blog.kraken.com/post/6241/kraken-wyoming-first-digital-asset-bank) [LDKR-036](https://api.brokercheck.finra.org/search/firm/324343) [F-1416](https://www.bma.bm/regulated-entities) [F-0250](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-12/CASPS.csv) [LDKR-028](https://www.payward.com/press-release/kraken-plans-uae-launch)
- Kraken이 갖던 아부다비 허가는 팔지 않고 철회 [LDKR-024](https://www.adgm.com/public-registers/fsra/firms/financial-firms/payward-mena-holdings-limited-190008)

## 가설 2. 제휴는 인수의 예고편이다

### 받치는 사실
- Backed: 2025-04-28 버뮤다 법인이 발행사와 지정참가자(AP) 계약 F-0919(로컬 저장 문서), 2025-06-30 Backed 발행 xStocks를 Kraken이 팜 [F-1249](https://blog.kraken.com/product/xstocks/tokenized-equities-now-available), 2025-12-02 인수 합의 [F-0139](https://www.payward.com/press-release/kraken-acquires-backed-xstocks-expansion)
- Crypto Facilities: 2016-05-04 CME 비트코인 기준가 일에서 Kraken은 시세 공급, Crypto Facilities는 지수 계산 [F-1758](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-to-provide-pricing-data-for-new-cme-group), 2019-02-04 인수(협상 약 10개월) [F-1778](https://blog.kraken.com/product/unlock-futures-trading-with-our-crypto-facilities-acquisition)

### 반대 사실
- 독일 Fidor Bank(2013-10 유로 입출금 제휴) [F-1731](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-forms-exclusive-partnership-with-fidor-bank): 인수 없음. 은행 기능은 2020 와이오밍 SPDI로 직접 [F-1719](https://blog.kraken.com/post/6241/kraken-wyoming-first-digital-asset-bank)
- 결제회사 SynapsePay(2016-01 미국 달러 입출금 제휴) [F-1752](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-acquiring-coinsetter-and-cavirtex-to-expand): 인수 기록 없음(빈칸)
- Silvergate(2019~2023 ACH), MVB Bank(Fedwire) 은행 제휴 [LDKR-015](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/news/kraken-to-suspend-ach-deposits-and-withdrawals/): 인수 없음
- Alpaca(2025-04 미국 주식 청산 제휴, 2025-12 xStocks 기초 주식 우선 거래처) [F-1801](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/kraken-adds-over-us-stocks-and-etfs-in-collaboration-with-broker-dealer-alpaca/) [F-1524](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-momentum-with-alpaca-and-kraken-partnership): 2026-11 기준 인수 없음

- 독일거래소 그룹(2025-12 제휴, 2026-04 Payward 구주 매입), Nasdaq(2026-03 제휴, 2026-09 1억 달러 투자): 상대가 Payward에 투자한 방향 [F-0175](https://www.payward.com/press-release/xstocks-dax-40) [F-2026](https://deutsche-boerse.com/dbg-en/media/news-stories/press-releases/Deutsche-B-rse-Group-Acquires-a-Stake-in-Kraken-for-200-Million-5077740) [F-1255](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-nasdaq-tokenized-equities)
- NinjaTrader, Small Exchange, Bitnomial: 인수 전 Kraken과 제휴한 기록 빈칸(kraken.md B17, B20, B22)
- 공동 CEO는 사지 못하는 신뢰는 기존 기관과 제휴한다고 말함(보도) [F-1349](https://www.coindesk.com/ko/business/2026/09/26/kraken-s-parent-payward-is-betting-billions-on-becoming-financial-infrastructure-not-just-a-crypto-exchange)

### 받치는 사실
- 미국
  - 2021-09-28 CFTC가 FCM 미등록 미국 개인 신용거래를 제재 [F-1781](https://mondovisione.com/media-and-resources/news/cftc-imposes-a-125-million-penalty-against-kraken-for-offering-illegal-off-exc-2021928/)
  - 2023-11-20 SEC 소송 [F-1788](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-continues-to-fight-for-its-mission-and-crypto-innovation-in-the-united-states), 2025-03-03 취하 원칙 합의 [F-1790](https://blog.kraken.com/news/sec-lawsuit-dismissal), 2025-03-27 공식 취하 [F-1791](https://decrypt.co/312072/sec-drops-cases-kraken-consensys-cumberland-drw)
  - 2025-03-20 NinjaTrader(FCM) 발표 [F-1117](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-to-acquire-ninjatrader), 2025-10-16 Small Exchange(DCM) [F-1116](https://blog.kraken.com/news/acquires-small-exchange), 2026-04-17 Bitnomial(DCM, DCO, FCM) [F-2029](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-to-acquire-bitnomial)
  - 2026-03-04 연준 마스터 계정 [F-1532](https://www.payward.com/press-release/kraken-first-digital-asset-bank-federal-reserve-master-account), 2026-05-08 OCC 신탁 신청 [LDKR-018](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-files-application-for-occ-national-trust-company)
- EU
  - 2023-10-05 회사가 MiCA 체계 성립을 BCM 인수와 유럽 계획을 앞당긴 이유로 듦 [F-1807](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-to-acquire-dutch-crypto-broker-bcm-in-commitment-to-grow-european-business)

  - 2025-02-03 키프로스 MiFID 투자회사 인수 [F-1809](https://blog.kraken.com/news/eu-mifid-license), 2025-06-25 아일랜드 MiCA 인가 [F-0250](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-12/CASPS.csv)
- UAE: 2026-05-21 두바이 VARA 예비, 2026-10-12 최종 [LDKR-027](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-vara-authorization-uae) [LDKR-028](https://www.payward.com/press-release/kraken-plans-uae-launch)

- 2019 Crypto Facilities: 새 제도가 열린 것이 아니라 FCA가 2018-04 기존 MiFID II가 가상자산 선물에 적용된다고 밝힌 상태 [F-2034](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/statements/cryptocurrency-derivatives)

- 일본: 2020-09-08 재등록 뒤 2022-12-28 철수 발표, 이유는 시장 상황 [LDKR-031](https://blog.kraken.com/post/6363/launch-in-japan) [LDKR-032](https://cointelegraph.com/news/kraken-quits-japan-for-second-time-blaming-a-weak-crypto-market)
- 2026 Reap(결제), Saldo Bank(은행), Tribe Capital(자산운용): 같은 시기 규제 개방 사건과 짝지을 회사 문장 없음 [F-2030](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-acquires-reap) [F-1194](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-acquires-saldo-bank) [LDKR-019](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-to-acquire-tribe-capital)
- NinjaTrader 발표와 SEC 취하 사이를 잇는 회사 발언은 없음 [LDKR-007](https://cointelegraph.com/news/kraken-reportedly-nears-billion-dollar-deal-allowing-futures-trading-us-report)

## 가설 4. 출신이 사는 것을 정한다(증권사 출신은 가상자산 거래소를, 거래소 출신은 증권사와 선물 중개사를 삼)

### 받치는 사실
- Kraken(2013 가상자산 거래소로 출발)이 산 규제 금융회사: 선물 거래장(Crypto Facilities 2019), EU 투자회사(키프로스 2025), 미국 선물 중개사(NinjaTrader 2025), 미국 선물 거래소(Small Exchange 2025), 거래소, 청산소, 중개사 묶음(Bitnomial 2026), 호주 금융서비스 인가 보유사(Beaufort), EU 은행(Saldo 2026 계약) [F-1803](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-acquires-crypto-derivatives-trading-platform-and-index-provider-crypto-facilities-in-nine-figure-deal-official-press-release) [F-1809](https://blog.kraken.com/news/eu-mifid-license) [F-1117](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-to-acquire-ninjatrader) [F-1116](https://blog.kraken.com/news/acquires-small-exchange) [F-2029](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-to-acquire-bitnomial) [LDKR-040](https://download.asic.gov.au/media/h5mfmwo3/kraken.pdf) [F-1194](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-acquires-saldo-bank)
- 판 쪽 IG(온라인 증권사)는 Small Exchange를 판 같은 공시에서 호주 가상자산 거래소 Independent Reserve 인수(2025-09 발표)를 다른 가상자산 행보로 듦 [LDKR-011](https://financialreports.eu/filings/8026393/content)
- (비교) Coinbase(거래소 출신)는 2018 증권사 Keystone 등 인수 [F-2115](https://altswire.com/crypto-exchange-coinbase-buys-broker-dealers-move-offer-regulated-securities/)

### 반대 사실
- Kraken의 미국 증권사는 인수가 아니라 직접 등록(2022-11-02 법인 설립, 2024-06-10 FINRA 승인) [LDKR-036](https://api.brokercheck.finra.org/search/firm/324343)
- Kraken은 거래소도 여러 번 삼: Coinsetter와 Cavirtex(2016), CleverCoin(2016), Bit Trade(2020), 중개사 BCM(2023) [F-1752](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-acquiring-coinsetter-and-cavirtex-to-expand) [F-1761](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-acquires-dutch-bitcoin-exchange-clevercoin) [F-1806](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-acquires-australias-longest-running-crypto-service-provider-bit-trade) [F-1807](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-to-acquire-dutch-crypto-broker-bcm-in-commitment-to-grow-european-business)
- 규제 금융회사가 아닌 것도 삼: 발행사 Backed, 카드 결제 Reap, 매매 도구 Capitalise.ai, 프롭 트레이딩 Breakout, 지갑 Magic Labs [F-0139](https://www.payward.com/press-release/kraken-acquires-backed-xstocks-expansion) [F-2030](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-acquires-reap) [LDKR-020](https://www.payward.com/press-release/kraken-acquires-capitalise-ai-trading-automation) [F-1252](https://blog.kraken.com/news/kraken-acquires-breakout) [F-1514](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-to-acquire-magic-labs-wallet-infrastructure)
- 선물 중개사 출신 NinjaTrader도 FCM을 사서 청산 쪽으로 내려감(앱 출신이 인가를 삼) [LDKR-003](https://ninjatrader.com/News/NinjaTrader-Acquires-TransAct-Futures)

## 가설 5. 돈의 형태가 인수 방식을 정한다(상장사 주식 대금, 현금, 비상장 자기 주식)

### 받치는 사실
- Small Exchange 1억 달러 가운데 Payward 비상장 주식 6,750만 달러(67.5%), 현금 3,250만 달러. IG는 주식 대가를 규제 자본에서 뺌 [F-2028](https://financialreports.eu/filings/8026393/content)
- Bitnomial(최대 5.5억 달러), Reap(최대 6억 달러) 모두 현금과 Payward 주식, Payward 주식가치 200억 달러 기준 [F-2029](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-to-acquire-bitnomial) [F-2030](https://www.payward.com/press-release/payward-acquires-reap)
