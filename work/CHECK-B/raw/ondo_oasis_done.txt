This acquisition provides the Ondo Finance group with the most comprehensive set of SEC registrations to provide digital asset services in the United States. With the acquisition completed, Ondo Finance can now develop and provide regulated markets for tokenized securities, laying the foundation for a new era of blockchain-based financial products for U.S. investors. Unlocking the Next Chapter of Tokenized Finance Oasis Pro brings a full-stack digital asset infrastructure to Ondo, enabling a wide range of tokenized securities functions. Through this acquisition, Ondo gains: An engine for the tokenization of real-world assets (RWAs) A digital asset transfer agent and capitalization management solution A primary offering marketplace A multi-asset secondary trading system for both public and private tokenized securities, utilizing digital currency or fiat Regulatory approval to conduct certain secondary market activities for a broad spectrum of assets, including traditional NMS securities, tokenized or traditional OTC equities, corporate debt, closed-end mutual funds, REITs, structured products, MBS, ABS, and privately placed securities Licenses to support capital markets activities, including private placements, underwriting, and M&A advisory functions This comprehensive framework positions Ondo to deliver a robust, regulated ecosystem for tokenized securities in the U.S., bridging traditional financial markets with blockchain-based infrastructure. "We now have the most comprehensive suite of licenses and infrastructure necessary to develop compliant and regulated tokenized securities markets in the U.S. This acquisition makes Ondo's offering one of the strongest commitments to building a transparent, accessible, and compliant financial system onchain." — Nathan Allman, CEO & Founder of Ondo Finance "Ondo is defining the future of tokenization and positioning itself as the global leader in the transformation of finance. This strategic combination unites an incredibly talented team, infrastructure, and expertise to build the leading trusted, regulatory-compliant platform in digital assets. We are excited to be part of this journey." — Pat LaVecchia, CEO of Oasis Pro Oasis Pro's Institutional-Grade Infrastructure, Proven Regulatory Track Record Founded in 2019, Oasis Pro operates, through its subsidiaries, an SEC-registered and FINRA member broker-dealer and Alternative Trading System (ATS), and an SEC-registered Transfer Agent (TA)—providing compliant infrastructure to support issuing and trading tokenized securities in the United States. Backed by leading investors such as Mirae Asset Ventures, Oasis Pro was one of the first US-regulated ATS authorized to support settlement of digital securities in both fiat and stablecoins like USDC and DAI. As a FINRA member since 2020 and an SEC-registered broker-dealer, Oasis Pro has paved the way for compliant innovation in digital asset markets, acting as a contributing member of FINRA's Crypto Working Group to shape the evolving regulatory framework for tokenized assets in the U.S. Building the Future of Tokenized Securities The acquisition significantly accelerates Ondo's roadmap. With over $1.6 billion in assets under management across its existing tokenized products, Ondo has already established itself as a global leader in real-world asset (RWA) tokenization. Bringing Oasis Pro's licenses in-house enables Ondo to expand access, particularly as the tokenized securities market rapidly accelerates, predicted to exceed $18 trillion by 2033.
