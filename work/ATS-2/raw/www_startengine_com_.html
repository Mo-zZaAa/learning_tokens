Frequently Asked Questions

What is equity crowdfunding?

Equity crowdfunding lets everyday investors buy ownership shares in private startups and growth-stage companies through SEC-regulated platforms like StartEngine.

How much money do I need to invest in a startup?

On StartEngine, most offerings accept investments starting at $100 to $500, depending on the company's minimum investment requirement.

Is investing in startups risky?

Yes. Startup investments are speculative and illiquid. Most startups fail, and you should only invest money you can afford to lose entirely.

What is Regulation Crowdfunding (Reg CF)?

Reg CF is an SEC exemption that allows non-accredited investors to invest in private companies online through registered platforms, subject to annual investment limits.
