# Ondo and LayerZero Launch Bridge for Onchain Securities, Starting With Ethereum and BNB Chain

Ondo Finance

Official Blog

[그림: Ondo and LayerZero launch tokenized securities bridge.]

* **100+ Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs can now be transferred seamlessly across Ethereum and BNB Chain with more chains coming soon**
* **2,600+ apps currently integrated with LayerZero can easily add Ondo assets**
* **Ondo Bridge launches as the largest bridge for tokenized securities**

We’re excited to introduce the [Ondo Bridge](https://app.ondo.finance/bridge) for tokenized stocks and ETFs. Launched in collaboration with [LayerZero](https://layerzero.network/), the bridge is now live across Ethereum and BNB Chain, with more EVM chains coming soon. Users can seamlessly transfer Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs cross-chain, while any protocol, wallet, or application already integrated with LayerZero can easily add [Ondo Stocks](https://ondo.finance/ondo-stocks) assets. **With 100+ assets live from day one, it launches as the largest bridge for tokenized securities.**

### Transfer Tokenized Stocks and ETFs Across Ethereum and BNB Chain Today

Since its [launch on Ethereum](https://ondo.finance/ondo-stocks) in September, Ondo Stocks (previously known as Ondo Global Markets) has become the largest tokenized securities platform worldwide, surpassing $350 million in total value locked (TVL) and $2 billion in trading volume. Ondo Stocks recently [launched on BNB Chain](https://ondo.finance/blog/global-markets-live-on-bnb-chain), providing its 3.4 million daily active users and expansive DeFi ecosystem access to onchain equities.

Now, 100+ Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs can be seamlessly transferred cross-chain between Ethereum and BNB Chain with 1:1 asset parity via the Ondo Bridge, all with the reliability and predictability expected from institutional-grade RWAs.

### Ondo Bridge Can Be Rapidly Deployed on EVM Chains in Weeks

The new unified bridging system powered by LayerZero replaces the previous model of isolated per-token bridges with a single framework. Through the custom configuration, all Ondo Stocks assets now use one consolidated architecture for cross-chain movement, eliminating the need for separate bridge contracts for each asset on each network.

Key unlocks:

* **Fast deployment to new chains:** Ondo Bridge can be connected to additional EVM chains in just weeks.
* **One pathway for all assets:** All Ondo Stocks assets can be bridged across EVM networks through LayerZero’s standardized framework.
* **Built for scale:** This architecture streamlines operations and is designed to support hundreds of assets moving between many chains.

### Any Protocol, Wallet, or App Integrated With LayerZero Can Easily Add Ondo Tokenized Stocks and ETFs

With this infrastructure now live, over 2,600 apps already integrated with LayerZero across Ethereum and BNB Chain can quickly add Ondo Stocks assets, all with minimal integration work. [Stargate](https://stargate.finance/) has already added Ondo assets to its protocol. Ultimately, Ondo Bridge for tokenized equities unlocks seamless integrations across the multi-chain economy and further embeds Ondo assets as the core RWA primitive across DeFi.

Ondo Finance President Ian De Bode:

> “The stage is set for the rapid expansion of Ondo Stocks across the multi-chain economy. Launching this Ondo Bridge with LayerZero empowers users to seamlessly transfer 100+ tokenized stocks and ETFs between Ethereum and BNB Chain, with many more chains coming soon. Moreover, with any wallet, protocol, or app in LayerZero’s ecosystem now able to easily integrate Ondo assets out of the box, we’re accelerating the creation of advanced cross-chain use cases and embedding Ondo tokenized stocks as foundational DeFi assets.”

LayerZero Labs Co-Founder and CEO Bryan Pellegrino:

> “Tokenized assets are quickly becoming the better version of everything that already exists in traditional markets: more transparent, more programmable, and able to move with the speed and finality of the internet. As the world shifts toward digitized stocks, ETFs, and other financial instruments, users will expect these assets to function like stablecoins — becoming instant, portable, and available everywhere. LayerZero is building the foundation for that future. We’re excited to support Ondo as they bring the next generation of financial assets onchain and make them accessible across the global crypto economy.”

### Future Ondo Bridge Expansion

With this architecture in place, Ondo Bridge can be extended to new EVM networks with unprecedented speed, further democratizing access to U.S. markets while powering a new generation of cross-chain DeFi use cases for Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs.
