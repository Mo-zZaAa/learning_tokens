What is Ondo Stocks (previously known as Ondo Global Markets)?

The following information is provided for general informational purposes only. Ondo Stocks are not available in the United States and are not available to U.S. persons. Nothing on this page constitutes an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy Ondo Stocks or any other product in the United States or to U.S. persons.

Ondo Stocks is a tokenization platform that provides eligible investors outside the United States with onchain economic exposure to publicly traded securities. The platform supports hundreds of U.S. publicly traded securities, including stocks, ETFs, and certain ETFs based on commodities. By providing tokenized exposure to these assets, Ondo Stocks brings the transparency, efficiency, and accessibility enabled by blockchain technology to widely held securities.

Ondo Stocks are designated by the “on” suffix at the end of the applicable stock ticker. For example, the stock ticker for Tesla is TSLA, and the corresponding Ondo tokenized stock is called TSLAon. The “on” suffix can be understood as standing for Ondo.

Ondo Stocks are fully backed by underlying shares and are structured to provide institutional-grade investor protections. Ondo Stocks also provide eligible users outside the United States with access to liquidity through the just-in-time minting-and-redemption model pioneered by Ondo Stocks.

Ondo Stocks are not available in the United States, and are not available to U.S. persons.

What are tokenized stocks and ETFs?

Tokenized stocks and ETFs are blockchain-based tokens that track the value of publicly traded securities, giving tokenholders economic exposure to traditional equity markets onchain. They are also known as tokenized equities and form part of the broader category of real-world assets, or RWAs.

Each token is designed to represent the economic value of an underlying security. A holder of a tokenized stock receives economic exposure designed to track the underlying stock, with dividends reinvested net of applicable withholding tax. Outside the United States and subject to applicable eligibility and jurisdictional restrictions, tokens may be minted and redeemed onchain and transferred or traded using blockchain infrastructure.

Examples of Ondo tokenized stocks include tokenized Nvidia (NVDAon), tokenized Tesla (TSLAon), tokenized Alphabet (GOOGLon), tokenized SpaceX (SPCXon), and tokenized Circle (CRCLon). Examples of Ondo tokenized ETFs include SPDR S&P 500 ETF (SPYon) and Invesco QQQ (QQQon).

These examples are provided for informational purposes only. Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs are not available in the United States.

Are Ondo Stocks available in the United States?

Information about Ondo Stocks that is accessible from the United States or by U.S. persons is provided for general informational purposes and should not be understood as an offer or solicitation.

Note that any mention of “U.S. stocks” in connection with Ondo Stocks refers to the underlying U.S.-listed stocks and ETFs whose economic performance Ondo Stocks are designed to track. It does not mean that Ondo Stocks themselves are offered or available in the United States or to U.S. persons.

Similarly, the fact that underlying assets may trade on U.S. exchanges or be held with U.S.-registered service providers does not make Ondo Stocks available in the United States or to U.S. persons.

Does temporarily traveling outside the United States make me eligible?

No. Eligibility does not depend solely on the location from which a person accesses the platform. A person must satisfy all applicable eligibility, jurisdictional, and onboarding requirements, including not being in the United States and not being a U.S. person.

Can developers build with tokenized stocks?

Ondo Stocks provides APIs and SDKs through which third-party protocols and developers can build financial applications involving tokenized assets. Potential applications include lending markets, structured products, index vaults, portfolio tools, and other blockchain-based financial services.

Developers may also be able to review Ondo’s publicly available technical documentation. However, access to documentation, APIs, SDKs, or other developer resources does not make Ondo Stocks available in the United States.

Any developer integration must maintain appropriate eligibility and geographic restrictions and must not offer, distribute, or facilitate transactions in Ondo Stocks in the United States. Developers should contact Ondo before launching an integration that could involve U.S. users.

What should I do if I am unsure whether I am eligible?
