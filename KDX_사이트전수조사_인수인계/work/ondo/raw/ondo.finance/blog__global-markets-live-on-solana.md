# Ondo Finance Brings Hundreds of Tokenized Stocks to Solana For the First Time

Ondo Finance

Official Blog

[그림: Ondo Finance Brings Hundreds of Tokenized Stocks to Solana]

*Ondo Stocks expansion to Solana brings onchain equities with the liquidity of traditional finance to its 3.2 million daily active users for the first time.*

Ondo Finance today announced the expansion of Ondo Stocks, the world’s largest tokenized securities platform by total value locked (TVL), to [Solana](https://solana.com/). The launch brings 200+ tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs with the liquidity of traditional finance to Solana for the first time. This makes Ondo Stocks the largest, broadest, and most liquid catalog of tokenized equities available on Solana today.

[Ondo Stocks](https://ondo.finance/ondo-stocks) (previously known as Ondo Global Markets) is now the largest real-world asset issuer on Solana by asset count. The expansion significantly broadens the scope of tokenized assets available to the Solana ecosystem, spanning tokenized technology and growth stocks, broad-market and sector ETFs, leveraged long and short ETFs, blue-chip equities, gold, silver, and other commodities-linked ETFs, alongside an array of other sectors such as AI and EV.

By tapping into the liquidity of the world’s largest exchanges such as NASDAQ and NYSE rather than relying on onchain liquidity pools that cannot scale efficiently for more than a few assets, Ondo Stocks unlocks million-dollar trade sizes with near-zero slippage for hundreds of assets. Now with Wall Street liquidity for onchain equities, Solana’s internet capital markets are set to scale rapidly.

3.2 million plus daily active users on Solana’s high-performance blockchain can access Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs today, with seamless discovery and execution via leading ecosystem infrastructure.

Ian De Bode, President of Ondo Finance, said:

> “We’re excited to bring hundreds of onchain securities with Wall Street liquidity to Solana’s thriving ecosystem. For the first time, Solana users can rest assured that they can buy tokenized stocks in size at brokerage prices, giving them peace of mind when trading onchain. Ondo Stocks’ liquidity model enables it to launch with hundreds of assets live on day one, with thousands more to come.”

Nick Ducoff, Head of Institutional Growth, Solana Foundation, added:

> “Solana is designed to support high-performance financial applications at global scale. As Solana powers internet capital markets, real-world assets are an important part of that future. Ondo Stocks’ launch brings a broad range of tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs into the Solana ecosystem, expanding what’s possible for onchain finance.”

Since launching on Ethereum in September 2025 and expanding to BNB Chain in October, Ondo Stocks has rapidly become the largest tokenized securities platform worldwide, surpassing $460 million in total value locked and driving more than $6.8 billion in cumulative trading volume.
