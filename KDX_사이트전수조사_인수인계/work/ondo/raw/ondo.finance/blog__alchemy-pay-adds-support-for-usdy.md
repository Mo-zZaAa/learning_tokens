# Alchemy Pay Adds Global Support for Fiat On-Ramp to USDY

[Ondo Finance

Official Blog](https://ondo.finance/blog)

[그림: Alchemy Pay Adds Global Support for Fiat On-Ramp to USDY]

Today, Ondo Finance announced that Alchemy Pay, a leading crypto payment solution provider, integrated Ondo Finance’s US Dollar Yield (USDY) token on Solana into Alchemy Pay’s extensive fiat on-ramp, making it easier for non-US users to acquire USDY using their local fiat currencies.

Through this collaboration, users across 173 countries can now seamlessly purchase USDY on Solana using local fiat currencies and preferred payment methods such as Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, regional mobile wallets, and bank transfers. Enabled by Alchemy Pay’s global infrastructure of over 300 payment channels and 50+ fiat currencies, this collaboration significantly lowers the entry barrier for non-US investors and institutions seeking high-quality yieldcoins.

USDY combines the accessibility of stablecoins with the financial strength of US Treasuries, offering 24/7 availability, transparent collateralization, and compatibility with a range of use cases, including treasury management, cross-border payments, and wealth preservation. USDY on Solana also benefits from near-instant transactions and minimal associated costs, making it practical for both institutions and individual investors seeking capital efficiency and secure dollar exposure.

As the industry accelerates toward the adoption of tokenized assets, Ondo Finance remains at the forefront with market-leading assets that can seamlessly integrate with platforms such as Alchemy Pay. Together, Ondo Finance and Alchemy Pay are advancing the vision of an open economy, while empowering users with tools for everyday financial utility and long-term wealth management.

**Learn More**

Try Alchemy Pay’s On & Off-Ramp to buy and sell crypto and local fiat currencies: <https://ramp.alchemypay.org/>

**About Alchemy Pay**

Founded in 2017, Alchemy Pay is a payment gateway that seamlessly connects crypto with traditional fiat currencies for businesses, developers, and end users. With its offerings including On & Off-Ramp, Web3 Digital Bank, NFT Checkout and its newly launched RWA platform, Alchemy Pay supports fiat payments in 173 countries.

The Ramp is a one-stop solution to buy and sell crypto and fiat, easily integrated by platforms and dApps according to requirements. The RWA platform allows global users to invest in tokenized real-world assets using local fiat currencies, lowering entry barriers and democratizing access to traditional financial instruments.
