# Ledger Wallet Now Supports In-App Swaps for Ondo Tokenized Stocks

Ondo Finance

Official Blog

[그림: Ledger Wallet Supports Ondo Tokenized Stocks]

We’re excited to announce that [Ondo Stocks](https://ondo.finance/ondo-stocks), the world’s largest tokenized securities platform with over $1 billion in TVL and tens of thousands of asset holders, is now accessible in [Ledger Wallet](https://www.ledger.com/) via 1inch intent-based swaps. Millions of Ledger users can now swap hundreds of Ondo tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs directly within one of the world’s leading hardware wallets.

Swaps of Ondo tokenized securities are powered by 1inch intent-based infrastructure and verified via Ledger’s hardware signing model.

### Self-Custody 260+ Tokenized U.S. Stocks & ETFs In-App

Ledger has long been synonymous with digital asset security, enabling millions of users to self-custody Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other crypto assets with hardware-enforced protection and clear signing. For crypto-native users, self-custody is foundational. By integrating Ondo Stocks into Ledger Wallet, tokenized U.S. stocks can live inside the same hardware-secured environment.

Eligible Ledger Wallet users in supported jurisdictions can now swap a rapidly expanding catalog of 260+ tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs directly in-app. This provides tokenized exposure to leading companies such as Tesla, NVIDIA, Apple, Microsoft, and Amazon, as well as broad-market ETFs like QQQ. Minting and redemption of Ondo Stocks assets is available 24 hours a day, five days a week, while permissionless token transfers remain available 24/7.

### Expanding Global Access to Tokenized Stocks

Since launching in September 2025, Ondo Stocks has surpassed $1 billion in total value across 260+ tokenized stocks, with tens of thousands of asset holders and more than $20 billion in cumulative trading volume. Ondo tokenized stocks are now live across Ethereum, Solana, and BNB Chain, and integrated into leading wallets, exchanges, and DeFi protocols.
