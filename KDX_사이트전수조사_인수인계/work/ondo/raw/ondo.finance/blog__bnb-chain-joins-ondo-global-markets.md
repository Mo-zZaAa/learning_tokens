# BNB Chain Joins Ondo Stocks Alliance With Plans to Expand Access to Tokenized Equities

[Ondo Finance

Official Blog](https://ondo.finance/blog)

[그림: BNB Chain Joins Ondo Stocks Alliance With Plans to Expand Access to Tokenized Equities]

We are excited to announce that BNB Chain, one of the world’s most widely used blockchain networks, will soon expand access to Ondo's suite of tokenized assets—including over 100 U.S. stocks, ETFs, and funds onchain with the liquidity of traditional finance.

As part of this collaboration, BNB Chain has also joins [Ondo Stocks Alliance](https://ondo.finance/blog/https:/blog.ondo.finance/ondo-finance-global-markets-alliance). BNB Chain joins a growing group of industry leaders, including wallets, exchanges, protocols, infrastructure providers, and custodians, committed to establishing shared standards for tokenized securities. By focusing on interoperability, investor protections, and liquidity, the alliance aims to streamline user access to tokenized real-world assets (RWAs) and set the highest standards for the industry.

*“Bringing Ondo Finance's tokenized equities to BNB Chain is an advancement in our ongoing efforts to expand real-world asset (RWA) accessibility. This collaboration accelerates our mission to bridge traditional finance with onchain ecosystems, empowering our global community with diversified investment opportunities while reinforcing BNB Chain’s role as a foundational layer for RWA adoption." -**Sarah S, the Head of Business Development at BNB Chain.***

*“Expanding Ondo’s tokenized stocks and ETFs to BNB Chain will mark a major milestone in our mission of bringing financial markets onchain. BNB Chain’s vibrant ecosystem and global reach make it a natural fit as we continue building the infrastructure for institutional-grade onchain capital markets.”* **- Nathan Allman, CEO & Founder, Ondo Finance**

BNB Chain's participation underscores the growing momentum behind the tokenization of traditional assets and the industry's commitment to building a more inclusive and efficient financial ecosystem.

*Stay tuned for more updates as we continue to expand access to global markets onchain.*
