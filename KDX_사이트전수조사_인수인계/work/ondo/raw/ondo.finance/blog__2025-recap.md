# 2025 Recap: Wall Street 2.0 Goes Global

Ondo Finance

Official Blog

[그림: 2025 Ondo Finance Recap]

At the start of the year we [highlighted](https://ondo.finance/blog/towards-the-open-economy) the $867 trillion in traditional financial assets that can [benefit](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2021/05/07/world-economic-forum-blockchain-could-disrupt-867t-in-traditional-markets) from tokenization and that “now is the time to bring a broader array of institutional-grade RWAs onchain.” That’s exactly what we did.

In 2025, Ondo has successfully brought over 100 U.S. stocks and ETFs onchain and collaborated with premier Wall Street institutions like BlackRock, Fidelity, and Kinexys by J.P. Morgan. We have significantly expanded the RWA stack as part of “the largest shift in infrastructure since electronic trading,” and cleared the way for regulated tokenized assets across the U.S. and EU.

This recap highlights Ondo’s major 2025 milestones that are shaping not only the next phase of tokenization, but the next era of global finance.

### Ondo Stocks Launches and Quickly Becomes Largest Tokenized Stocks Platform

Ondo Stocks (previously known as Ondo Global Markets) brings U.S. stocks and ETFs onchain, issuing tokens that are freely transferable and usable across DeFi, fully backed 1:1 by offchain securities, and inherit the liquidity of major exchanges like Nasdaq and NYSE.

Key Ondo Stocks highlights:

* **Ondo Stocks [launches](https://ondo.finance/blog/global-markets-is-live)** on Ethereum, giving investors 24/7 access to 100+ tokenized stocks and ETFs.
* **3.4 million daily active users on [BNB Chain](https://ondo.finance/blog/global-markets-live-on-bnb-chain)**, one of the most widely used blockchain networks globally, gained access to Ondo tokenized securities.
* **Ondo Bridge [launches](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-bridge-for-onchain-securities) as the largest bridge** for tokenized securities, connecting Ondo Stocks assets on Ethereum and BNB Chain, with more chains to come.
* **Solana is on track to get [access](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/2000567522580697128)** to hundreds of tokenized stocks and ETFs in early 2026.
* **Ondo established itself as the largest tokenized stock platform** with [more](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/2001363367995543671) market share than all other platforms and the most RWAs available.
* **$2 billion in total volume and $370 million in TVL** for Ondo Stocks [achieved](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/1999489821442716138), with over $1 billion traded in a single month.
* **~$10m of NVDAon redemptions were [processed](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/2001412167266877574)** in minutes and with near-zero slippage, demonstrating how tapping into Nasdaq and NYSE liquidity enables institutional-grade execution onchain.

> “Ondo is set to bring trillions of RWAs onchain and usher in the institutional era of blockchain, ultimately democratizing access to the world’s most in-demand capital markets.” — Ondo President Ian De Bode

### Ondo Clears The Way For Tokenization Across U.S. and EU

Ondo Finance is actively helping shape the tokenization policy landscape in the U.S. as it develops compliant solutions for the U.S., EU, and globally.

2025 policy takeaways:

* **Ondo [gains](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-acquires-oasis-pro) the most comprehensive set of SEC registrations** to provide digital asset services in the United States with its acquisition of Oasis Pro, including its SEC-registered digital assets broker-dealer, alternative trading system (ATS), and transfer agent (TA) licenses. This provides Ondo with the infrastructure necessary to develop compliant and regulated tokenized securities markets in the U.S.
* **500+ million investors in 30 European countries** can soon access regulated exposure to U.S. markets directly onchain, with Ondo [receiving](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-eu-regulatory-approval) regulatory approval to bring tokenized stocks and ETFs to investors across the European Union (EU) and the broader European Economic Area (EEA).
* **SEC [ends](https://ondo.finance/blog/tokenized-securities-step-forward) Biden-era investigation into Ondo without charges**. Ondo remained steadfast in its conviction that regulated, transparent tokenization models like Ondo’s are not only compatible with investor-protection principles, but can strengthen them. The path is now clearer than ever for tokenized Treasuries and tokenized equities to become core components of U.S. capital markets.
* **Ondo submitted multiple open letters to the SEC**, calling for [transparency](https://ondo.finance/blog/call-for-transparency-sec), submitting a [roadmap](https://ondo.finance/blog/tokenized-securities-roadmap) for tokenized securities, and [withdrawing](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/2002021633775546570) a previous objection given the SEC helped address key issues we had previously raised.
* **Ondo steps up its leadership in D.C.**, joining the [Blockchain Association](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/1998075618878271767), the leading group driving pro-innovation U.S. digital-asset policy.

> "We now have the most comprehensive suite of licenses and infrastructure necessary to develop compliant and regulated tokenized securities markets in the U.S. This acquisition makes Ondo's offering one of the strongest commitments to building a transparent, accessible, and compliant financial system onchain." — Nathan Allman, CEO & Founder of Ondo Finance

### Wall Street’s Premier Institutions Partner With Ondo

As the industry leader for tokenized RWAs, Ondo is working with the world’s largest institutions to bring traditional assets onchain.

Key collaborations with leading institutions:

* **Fidelity’s new tokenized fund** was [anchored](https://ondo.finance/blog/fidelity-unveils-onchain-money-market-fund-anchored-by-ondo-finance) by OUSG, Ondo Finance’s tokenized U.S. treasury fund. OUSG’s portfolio also now spans funds from the world’s most trusted asset managers, including BlackRock, Fidelity, Franklin Templeton, State Street, WisdomTree, and Wellington Management.
* **State Street Investment Management, Galaxy Digital, and Ondo** [plan](https://ondo.finance/blog/state-street-galaxy-ondo-tokenized-sweep-fund) to launch a tokenized money market fund. OUSG, Ondo’s flagship tokenized fund, plans to serve as the lead anchor investor when it launches in early 2026.
* **Kinexys by J.P. Morgan and Ondo achieve major RWA [milestone](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-chain-jpmorgan-kinexys-chainlink)** together with Chainlink. The successful completion demonstrates a scalable approach to cross-chain, atomic settlement of tokenized assets.
* **PayPal’s stablecoin, PYUSD, and Ondo’s OUSG fund** can now be [converted](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-finance-launches-24-7-conversions-between-ousg-and-paypal-usd-pyusd) onchain 24/7. The $25 million facility makes it one of the largest such facilities for stablecoins and tokenized money market funds.
* **BX Digital, part of the Boerse Stuttgart Group**, Europe's sixth-largest exchange group with €90 billion in annual trading volume, and Ondo [partner](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-partners-with-bx-digital) to launch regulated trading of Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs in Europe.
* **Mastercard to [onboard](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-finance-brings-tokenized-real-world-assets-to-mastercards-multi-token-network) Ondo to its Multi-Token Network**, one of the first live instances of direct connectivity between a private payments network and public blockchain networks.
* **Franklin Templeton, Wellington Management, WisdomTree, Google Cloud, ABN Amro, Aon, and McKinsey**, became design [advisors](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/1887522597480337672) for [Ondo Chain](https://ondo.finance/blog/introducing-ondo-chain), joining other notable industry leaders such as BlackRock, PayPal and Morgan Stanley already in the Ondo ecosystem.
* **Ondo Nexus is introduced**, leveraging assets from BlackRock, Franklin Templeton, Wellington Management, and WisdomTree to deliver instant liquidity for third-party tokenized treasuries.

> “The demonstrated cross-chain solution is a testament to what can be achieved through strong collaboration across diverse segments of the Web3 ecosystem, and we are pleased to have worked with Ondo.” — Nelli Zaltsman, Head of Platform Settlement Solutions, Kinexys Digital Payments at Kinexys by J.P. Morgan.

### Unparalleled Ondo Expansion Across RWA Stack

Ondo is rapidly expanding across the tokenization stack to provide end-users and partners with the best assets and infrastructure available.

Key Ondo expansions include:

* **$250 million [Ondo Catalyst](https://ondo.finance/catalyst) initiative** focused on developing the next generation of tokenization launches with support from Pantera Capital.
* **[Ondo Chain](https://ondo.finance/ondo-chain) was announced**, a layer 1 blockchain purpose-built for institutional-grade RWAs.
* **Ondo acquires Strangelove** to [accelerate](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-finance-acquires-strangelove) our full-stack RWA platform development. This significantly strengthened our position as the only full-stack tokenized asset platform with omnichain asset issuance, protocol design and blockchain development capabilities.
* **USDY adoption continues** across Stellar, Sei, Alchemy Pay, ICRYPEX, Zodia, TruBit, and more, while OUSG is deployed on Ripple’s XRP Ledger.

> "Tokenization of traditional financial assets is the largest shift in infrastructure since electronic trading, and Ondo is at the forefront of it with the right traction and partners." — Paul Veradittakit, Managing Partner, Pantera Capital
