## Welcome to the Monthly Spotlight from Ondo Finance

October marked another transformative month for Ondo and the broader digital asset ecosystem.

---

### October Highlights

* **Ondo Finance Finalizes Oasis Pro Acquisition to Expand U.S. Operations**
* **Ondo Summit Returns February 2026**
* **Open Letter to the SEC on Tokenized Securities Regulation**
* **Ondo Brings Tokenized U.S. Stocks and ETFs to Blockchain․com Users**
* **PancakeSwap Joins Ondo Stocks Alliance**
* **Ondo Global Markets Expands to BNB Chain**
* **Ondo and Chainlink Announce Landmark Strategic Partnership**

---

#### Ondo Finance Finalizes Oasis Pro Acquisition to Expand U.S. Operations

The acquisition provides the Ondo Finance group with one of the most comprehensive sets of SEC registrations for digital asset services in the United States. [Read more](https://ondo.finance/blog/ondo-acquires-oasis-pro)

---

#### Ondo Summit

**The event that put tokenization on the map returns.**  
On **February 3, 2026**, the world’s leading executives, investors, and policymakers will reconvene in **New York** for the **Ondo Summit**, defining the next era of global capital markets. [Read more](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/1977751385425821944)

---

#### An Open Letter to the SEC on Transparency in Tokenized Securities Regulation

Ondo submitted an open letter to the **U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission** in response to **Nasdaq’s proposed rule changes** regarding the trading of tokenized securities.

As a leader in real-world asset tokenization, Ondo called for greater transparency before such proposals advance, emphasizing the importance of public access to critical market information. [Read more](https://ondo.finance/blog/call-for-transparency-sec)

---

#### Ondo Brings Tokenized U.S. Stocks and ETFs to Blockchain․com Users

Over **90 million wallets** now have access to tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs.

**Blockchain․com** integrated **Ondo Stocks**, enabling global users to access more than 100 tokenized assets. [Read more](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/1980239182137004438)

---

#### PancakeSwap Joins Ondo Stocks Alliance

**PancakeSwap (PancakeSwap)**, the leading DEX on **BNB Chain**, joins the **Ondo Stocks Alliance**, a coalition of **30+ industry leaders** working to align standards for tokenized stocks and ETFs.

Together, the Alliance is building interoperability and standards for tokenized financial assets. [Read more](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/1980469620508356760)

---

#### Ondo Stocks Expands to BNB Chain

**Ondo Stocks** has expanded to **BNB Chain**, bringing **U.S. markets** to millions of users worldwide.

Now, over **100 tokenized stocks and ETFs** are live within one of the world’s most active blockchain ecosystems, supported by **PancakeSwap**.  
**Wall Street, now on BNB Chain. Powered by Ondo.** [Read more](https://ondo.finance/blog/global-markets-live-on-bnb-chain)

---

#### Ondo and Chainlink Announce Landmark Strategic Partnership

The two firms announced a **landmark strategic partnership** to bring financial institutions onchain and build infrastructure to tokenize trillions.

Together, **Ondo** and **Chainlink** will collaborate with leading institutions to move assets and operations onchain using Ondo’s tokenization infrastructure and Chainlink’s **CCIP** for cross-chain connectivity. [Read more](https://x.com/OndoFinance/status/1983897891602153853)
