# Ondo Stocks Is Now Live on BNB Chain

[Ondo Finance

Official Blog](https://ondo.finance/blog)

[그림: Ondo Stocks Is Now Live on BNB Chain]

### BNB Chain users can now access 100+ tokenized stocks and ETFs

We’re excited to announce the expansion of Ondo Stocks, the world’s largest tokenized stock and ETF platform by total value locked (TVL), to BNB Chain, one of the most widely used blockchain networks globally. The move marks another milestone in Ondo’s mission to democratize access to high-quality U.S. financial assets through blockchain technology, making it the first tokenization platform to bring tokenized stocks & ETFs to BNB Chain at scale.

The integration provides BNB Chain — with its 3.4 million daily active users and expansive DeFi ecosystem — access to over 100 tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs, supported by leading ecosystem projects such as PancakeSwap.

By joining BNB Chain’s extensive ecosystem of wallets, exchanges, and DeFi protocols, Ondo tokenized stocks will integrate with key infrastructure partners to expand global access and liquidity for tokenized securities. The expansion builds on Ondo’s cross-chain strategy, with support already live on Ethereum and additional deployments planned, ensuring that tokenized assets can move seamlessly across major blockchains.

Sarah Song, Head of Business Development at BNB Chain:

*“Real-world assets are one of the fastest-growing segments on BNB Chain, and having Ondo Finance join our ecosystem is another strong validation of that momentum. Together, we’re expanding access to high-quality financial assets and driving the next wave of adoption that connects traditional markets with blockchain technology.”*

Nathan Allman, Founder and CEO of Ondo Finance:

*“BNB Chain is home to one of the largest and most engaged global user bases in Web3. Expanding Ondo Stocks to BNB Chain allows us to bring tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs to millions of users across Asia, Latin America, and other geographies, in an environment that is fast, cost-efficient, and highly interoperable. This is a major step toward making U.S. markets globally accessible through blockchain technology.”*

Since its launch in September, Ondo Stocks has become the largest tokenized securities platform worldwide, surpassing $350 million in total value locked (TVL) within weeks. It is more than two times larger than all other platforms combined, driving over $669 million in total onchain volume, and offering one of the largest selections of more than 100 tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs.
