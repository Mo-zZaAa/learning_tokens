### What Are Stablecoins?

Stablecoins are digital tokens designed to maintain a stable value, most commonly pegged to the U.S. dollar.

Explore what tokenized real-world assets (RWAs) are and how they’re bringing stocks, ETFs, and other financial assets to the blockchain ecosystem.

### What Is a Decentralized Exchange (DEX)?

Decentralized exchanges (DEXs) are blockchain-based trading platforms that enable peer-to-peer cryptocurrency and tokenized asset trading, using smart contracts instead of centralized order books.

4 min read](https://ondo.finance/learn/defi/decentralized-exchange-dex)[[그림: Tokenized Treasuries]

### What Are Tokenized Treasuries?

Tokenized U.S. Treasuries are blockchain-based representations of U.S. government debt instruments. These tokens provide holders with yield from Treasury bills, notes, or Treasury-focused ETFs.

5 min read](https://ondo.finance/learn/tokenized-rwas/what-are-tokenized-treasuries)[[그림: Tokenized stocks and ETFs ]

### What Are Tokenized Stocks and ETFs?

Tokenized stocks and ETFs are blockchain-based representations of publicly traded securities that provide exposure to traditional equity markets.

5 min read](https://ondo.finance/learn/tokenized-rwas/tokenized-stocks-and-etfs)[[그림: Decentralized Lending and Borrowing]

### What Is Decentralized Lending and Borrowing?

Explore decentralized lending and borrowing protocols, how they work, key benefits like permissionless access, and how tokenized assets serve as collateral.

### What Is Decentralized Finance (DeFi)?

Decentralized finance (DeFi) refers to financial products and services built on blockchain infrastructure that operate without traditional intermediaries like banks, brokerages, or clearinghouses.
