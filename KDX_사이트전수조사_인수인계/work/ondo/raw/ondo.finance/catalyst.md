# Ondo Catalyst

A $250M initiative pursuing strategic investments to accelerate the Ondo ecosystem and transformation of capital markets.

[Apply Now](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdzQUkCHCsrFdufFoyMGLTizwDYPbt9AJH3yklD_zaD2H9t_Q/viewform)

### What We Are Looking For

Ondo Catalyst backs projects building across the Ondo ecosystem, with a focus on next-generation technologies that drive utility, scalability, and adoption of tokenized assets.

### Protocols and Platforms

Core financial applications, DeFi protocols, and developer platforms that integrate with or extend the utility of tokenized RWAs.

### Infrastructure

Foundational technologies enabling tokenized finance, including custody, compliance, identity, and data interoperability.
