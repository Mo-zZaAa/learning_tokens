The following table highlights the maximum theoretical single trade size for each asset for the given trading session. For example if the maximum trade size for NVDAon is $3M in the regular session, you may submit 3 subsequent trades of $3M to acquire $9M worth of NVDAon. Please note these limits are not guaranteed and other transient factors may temporarily prevent a successful trade at the indicated size. The Off-Hours value is a maximum net-notional position (buys and sells offset) enforced while the US market is closed, rather than the active notional used during regular sessions.
