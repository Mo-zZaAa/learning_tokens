# Transferability

How portfolio token transfers and DeFi use work.

Copy pageCopy page

### Are Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens transferable?

Yes. Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens are freely transferable onchain outside the US across supported blockchains like [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org/), [BNB Chain](https://www.bnbchain.org/), and [Solana](https://solana.com/), subject to [certain jurisdictional and other restrictions](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/eligibility).

### Can I use portfolio tokens in DeFi?

Yes. Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens are designed to be usable in DeFi protocols that support them — for example, as collateral for lending — subject to [applicable restrictions](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/eligibility).
