## What is Ondo Private Client?

The Ondo Private Client Program is a loyalty program offered by the Ondo Foundation to our ecosystem’s greatest supporters. Private Clients are entitled to personalized support channels, gifts, early access to new features, and events.Private Client tiers are separated by account activity levels, which the program tracks directly from your qualifying wallet.**[Check your status →](https://app.ondo.finance/private-client)**

## Program Tiers & Qualification

Ondo Private Client has three tiers defined by **assets held** or **cumulative spot volume** thresholds. Tier status is based on verified qualifying activity. There is no application to submit and Private Client status cannot be purchased.

| Tier | Eligible assets held | Cumulative spot volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Platinum** | $1M-$5M | $1M-$10M |
| **Diamond** | $5M-$25M | $10M-$50M |
| **Obsidian** | More than $25M | More than $50M |

Qualifying assets may include [Ondo Stocks](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/overview), [USDY](https://docs.ondo.finance/general-access-products/usdy/basics), and other eligible Ondo products. Assets held are measured over a 90-day rolling period. Cumulative spot volume is measured over a 30-day rolling period. Because eligibility is evaluated using rolling periods, maintaining status requires continuing to meet the applicable asset or volume thresholds.Qualification and tier status may be subject to verification, applicable terms, and [jurisdictional restrictions](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/eligibility).

## Benefits

Ondo Private Client members may receive a combination of enhanced support, early product access, invitations, and other member benefits. Benefits expand by tier and are subject to availability, account status, eligibility, and applicable terms.

| Benefit | Description | Platinum\* | Diamond\* | Obsidian\* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Personalized Support** | A dedicated relationship manager, with around-the-clock support availability where offered. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Early Access to New Features** | Product updates and early access to selected new features, where available. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Invitations to Select Events** | Invitations to selected Ondo events, subject to availability and capacity. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Member Gifts** | Select member gifts, where available. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Shape What’s Next** | Opportunities to share feedback with Ondo product teams to help inform future products and experiences. | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Hosted Experiences** | Curated gatherings with the Ondo Private Client team and fellow members. | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Signature Experiences** | One-of-a-kind experiences created for Obsidian members. | — | — | ✓ |

\* A checkmark indicates that the benefit may be available to members of that tier. Benefits are not guaranteed and may vary by user, tier, account status, jurisdiction, capacity, and availability.An active, verified [Ondo Stocks account](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/onboarding-and-kyc) may be required to unlock select rewards or benefits.

## Check your status

You can check your eligibility directly through the official Ondo Private Client page.

1. **Navigate directly to Ondo Private Client.** Visit the [official Ondo website](https://app.ondo.finance/private-client).
2. **Connect your wallet.** Connect the wallet associated with your qualifying activity.
3. **Confirm eligibility.** If you qualify, the dashboard displays your Private Client tier and the benefits currently available to you.

**[Check your status →](https://app.ondo.finance/private-client)**

### What happens next

If you qualify for Ondo Private Client:

1. **Connect with the Ondo Private Client Team.** Share your Telegram handle or email address to set up a direct line of communication with the Ondo Private Client Team.
2. **Access eligible benefits.** Once applicable requirements are complete, you may receive access to relevant support, product features, events, experiences, or other member benefits based on your tier.
3. **Activate an Ondo Stocks account where required.** Select rewards or benefits may require an active, verified [Ondo Stocks account](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/onboarding-and-kyc).
4. **Maintain your status.** Continue to meet the applicable assets-held or trading-volume thresholds to maintain Private Client status.

Your dashboard is the primary place to confirm your current tier and available benefits.

## Security & Verification

**Do not use a DM link to claim your status.** Ondo will never ask you to click a link in a direct message to claim Ondo Private Client status. Always navigate to the official Ondo website and connect your wallet there. When in doubt, reference our [Official Domains](https://docs.ondo.finance/official-domains) page.

**Verify authenticity.** Any onchain communications will be sent from the [official Ondo DeBank account](https://debank.com/official/ondo). Messages will never ask you to click a link.

**Review every signature request.** Wallet ownership may be verified using an onchain signature that does **not require gas or spending permissions**. Review every wallet request carefully before signing.

## Eligibility & Legal Notice

Access to Ondo Private Client, [Ondo Stocks](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/overview), [USDY](https://docs.ondo.finance/general-access-products/usdy/basics), and related benefits is subject to eligibility, onboarding, jurisdictional restrictions, and applicable terms. Availability of assets, features, benefits, and services may vary by user, tier, and jurisdiction.
