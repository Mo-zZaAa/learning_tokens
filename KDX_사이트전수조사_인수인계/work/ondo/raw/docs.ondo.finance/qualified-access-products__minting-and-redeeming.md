# Minting & Redeeming

Copy pageCopy page

Copy pageCopy page

### **What currency or stablecoins can I use to invest in your Qualified Access Funds?**

We currently support investing via USDC, PYUSD, RLUSD, or USD bank wire.

### **What currency or stablecoins can I redeem OUSG for?**

We currently support redeeming to USDC, PYUSD, RLUSD, or USD bank wire.
