### On which chains is Ondo Stocks available?

Ondo Stocks is available on Ethereum mainnet, BNB Chain, and Solana, with more chains coming soon. Select Ondo Stocks can also be bridged to HyperEVM and Robinhood Chain.

### Are the Ondo Stocks smart contracts audited?

Yes. You can see the results of our audits [here](https://docs.ondo.finance/audits).
