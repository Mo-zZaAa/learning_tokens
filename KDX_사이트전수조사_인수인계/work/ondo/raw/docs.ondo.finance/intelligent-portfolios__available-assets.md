| YLD5on | Ondo 5 Targeted Income TR Portfolio | Income | Ondo |
| YLD8on | Ondo 8 Targeted Income TR Portfolio | Income | Ondo |
| BLKHIon | Ondo High Income Powered by BlackRock | Income | BlackRock |

For current holdings, weights, yields, and fees for each portfolio, please check the individual product page at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/).

### What assets do the portfolios hold?

Each portfolio is fully backed by a weighted basket of Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs — the same assets available individually on the [Ondo Stocks](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/overview) platform. Depending on the portfolio, constituents include tokenized individual stocks, equity and fixed income ETFs, and crypto ETFs (such as the iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF, IBITon). Asset selection and base weights are set by each portfolio’s [model provider](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/model-providers).

### Who sets the strategies?

Each portfolio follows a portfolio strategy developed by a model provider — either Ondo or an institutional partner working with Ondo. [Learn More](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/model-providers)

### Do you intend to offer more portfolios in the future?

Yes. The platform was designed to be highly scalable, and we intend to add more portfolios and model providers over time.
