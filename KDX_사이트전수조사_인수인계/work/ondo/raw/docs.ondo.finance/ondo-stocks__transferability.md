### Are Ondo stock tokens transferable?

Yes. All Ondo stocks are **transferable outside the US** across supported blockchains like [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org/), [BNB Chain](https://www.bnbchain.org/), [Solana](https://solana.com/), [HyperEVM](https://hyperliquid.xyz/), and [Robinhood Chain](https://robinhood.com/us/en/crypto/chain) (with other chains coming soon), subject to [certain jurisdictional and other restrictions](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/eligibility).
