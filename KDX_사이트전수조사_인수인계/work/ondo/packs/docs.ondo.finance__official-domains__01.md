# Official Domains Operated by Ondo Finance or Ondo-Branded Ecosystem Participants

Copy pageCopy page

Verify you’re interacting with official Ondo Finance and Ondo-branded ecosystem properties

Copy pageCopy page

To protect the Ondo community from phishing and impersonation scams, this page lists all official domains, subdomains, and social accounts operated by (i) Ondo Finance or its affiliates or (ii) Ondo ecosystem participants that are Ondo-branded but unaffiliated with Ondo Finance. **If a URL or social handle is not listed here, treat it as untrusted** — verify with the team before connecting a wallet, signing a transaction, or sharing any personal information.

Ondo team members will never DM you first, ask for your seed phrase or private keys, or request that you send funds to “verify” your wallet, unlock an allocation, or claim rewards.

### Spotting Scams

Scam and phishing sites frequently mimic Ondo branding using lookalike domains, typos, or appended words (e.g., “premarket-”, “claim-”, “-airdrop”). **Do not connect a wallet, send funds, or submit information to any such site.**
When in doubt:

1. Start from one of the domains listed above — don’t follow links from search engines, ads, X/Twitter replies, or DMs.
2. Bookmark [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/) and [docs.ondo.finance](https://docs.ondo.finance/) for direct access.
3. Only trust posts from the official X accounts listed above — impersonator accounts using similar handles (e.g., extra underscores, swapped letters) are common.

### Reporting

If you believe you’ve encountered a phishing site, impersonator account, or any suspicious activity claiming to be (i) Ondo Finance or its affiliates or (ii) any Ondo-branded ecosystem participant (Ondo Foundation, Flux Finance, or Ondo Perps), please report it immediately to (i) [support@ondo.finance](mailto:support@ondo.finance) or (ii) [admin@ondo.foundation](mailto:admin@ondo.foundation), respectively.
