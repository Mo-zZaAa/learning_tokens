## FAQ

What are Ondo Intelligent Portfolios?

Ondo Intelligent Portfolios are professionally curated investment portfolios delivered as single onchain tokens. Each portfolio token gives eligible investors outside the U.S. exposure to a weighted basket of tokenized assets that tracks a defined investment portfolio, without needing to buy, weight, or rebalance individual positions themselves.

The first three portfolios are based on portfolios built by BlackRock for Ondo, marking the first time eligible onchain investors can access exposure to such portfolios through a single token. Each portfolio is managed, sponsored, and administered by Ondo. Constituents, weights, and every rebalance are fully visible onchain, and the tokens are freely transferable peer-to-peer across wallets, exchanges, and DeFi protocols (subject to certain jurisdictional and other restrictions).

What is a portfolio token?

A portfolio token is a blockchain-based token that provides economic exposure to a basket of underlying assets managed according to a defined portfolio. Instead of opening a brokerage account and buying, weighting, and rebalancing positions yourself, investors can hold a single onchain token that automatically rebalances in line with an institutional-grade portfolio.

Just as the ETF made diversified portfolios easier to hold and trade than the mutual fund, the portfolio token takes the next step with a curated portfolio that can be held in a wallet, moved peer-to-peer 24/7, and composable across the broader onchain economy.

Which portfolios are available?

Our current portfolio lineup includes portfolios across income, allocation, and thematic categories:

* Ondo 8 Targeted Income TR Portfolio (YLD8on)
* Ondo 5 Targeted Income TR Portfolio (YLD5on)
* Ondo High Income Powered by BlackRock (BLKHIon)
* Ondo Diversified Growth Powered by BlackRock (BLKDIGon)
* Ondo High Growth Powered by BlackRock (BLKGRWon)
* Ondo Big 5 Core Allocation Portfolio (BIG5on)
* Ondo Magnificent 7 and Crypto Portfolio (MAG7Xon)
* Ondo AI Leaders Portfolio (BRAINon)

More Ondo Intelligent Portfolios are launching soon.

How do Ondo Intelligent Portfolios work?

Investors mint or redeem a single token to gain or exit exposure to a professionally curated basket of tokenized assets. The portfolio rebalances back to its target weights programmatically, executed at the smart contract level, so holders always maintain exposure to the intended portfolio without needing to take any action themselves. Every constituent, weight, and rebalance is visible onchain in real time.

How is this different from buying an ETF or model portfolio?

Traditional diversified portfolios require brokerage accounts and fund structures, are only tradable during market hours, and are out of reach for much of the world’s population. Ondo Intelligent Portfolios deliver the same institutional portfolio construction as a single token that is:

* **Accessible:** Available globally (outside restricted jurisdictions), without minimums, lockups, or intermediaries
* **Transparent:** Every constituent, weight, and rebalance is visible onchain in real time, rather than in periodic disclosures
* **Flexible:** Freely transferable peer-to-peer and composable in DeFi
* **Programmable:** Rebalancing executes automatically at the smart contract level, with the potential to combine asset classes within a single token in ways conventional vehicles were never built to support

Just as the ETF was a step change over the mutual fund, the portfolio token is positioned to be a step change in how curated portfolios are held, moved, and built upon.

What are Ondo Stocks?

[Ondo Stocks](https://ondo.finance/ondo-stocks) provide onchain exposure to publicly traded securities, giving eligible investors exposure to hundreds of U.S.-listed stocks and ETFs. Each Ondo Stock is designated by the “on” suffix at the end of the stock ticker. For example, tokenized Tesla is TSLAon.

All Ondo Stocks are fully backed by underlying shares (together with cash in transit), track the total return of the underlying security (with dividends reinvested, net of applicable withholding tax), provide institutional-grade investor protections, and offer best-in-class liquidity through the just-in-time liquidity model Ondo Stocks pioneered.

How are Ondo Intelligent Portfolios different from Ondo Stocks?

Ondo Stocks are the building blocks, while Ondo Intelligent Portfolios are portfolios built from them.

Ondo Stocks, such as NVDAon or SPYon, give you tokenized exposure to a single security. Investors select what to buy, how much, and when to rebalance their portfolio. An Ondo Intelligent Portfolio delivers a professionally curated, multi-asset portfolio in a single token. The selection, weighting, and rebalancing are handled for you and executed programmatically at the smart contract level.

Do Ondo Intelligent Portfolios track the total return of the underlying securities?

Yes. Ondo Intelligent Portfolios are designed to be total-return trackers of their underlying tokenized stocks. This means they provide exposure to both price movements and reinvested dividends and/or interest (net of applicable withholding tax), giving users exposure to the economic performance of the underlying basket over time, not just the price appreciation.

How does rebalancing work?

Rebalancing is programmatic. Smart contracts automatically rebalance the portfolio back to its target weights at periodic intervals. No action is required from holders and every rebalance is visible onchain.

Are Ondo Intelligent Portfolios transferable 24/7?

Portfolio tokens are freely transferable peer-to-peer 24/7, including on applicable exchanges and DeFi protocols (subject to the operation of those platforms).

Can Ondo Intelligent Portfolios be used in DeFi?

Yes. Ondo Intelligent Portfolios are freely transferable onchain outside the U.S. (subject to certain jurisdictional and other restrictions) and are fully composable with DeFi protocols that support them.
