# Comparison to ETFs & Tokenized Assets

Copy pageCopy page

How Ondo Intelligent Portfolios compare to ETFs and individual tokenized assets.

Copy pageCopy page

### How is an Ondo Intelligent Portfolio different from an ETF?

An ETF is a fund. When you buy one, you buy shares in a pooled investment vehicle that is registered, sponsored, and administered under a fund regime, and that trades on a listed exchange. An Ondo Intelligent Portfolio is not a fund and is not exchange-listed. You are not buying shares in a fund, and there is no fund manager, fund administrator, or fund custodian standing behind your position in that capacity.An Ondo Intelligent Portfolio token is not an ETF. The token is fully backed by a weighted basket of Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs (together with any cash in transit). You mint and redeem it directly with the Issuer rather than buying it from another investor on an exchange, and you can transfer it peer-to-peer onchain, subject to certain transfer restrictions.A few practical consequences of that difference:

* **There is no primary-market intermediary.** ETF creation and redemption runs through authorized participants in large fixed-size blocks. With portfolio tokens, any eligible investor can mint or redeem directly with the issuer.
* **You can hold fractional amounts.** ETF shares trade in whole units on exchange; Portfolio tokens can be held and transacted in fractional amounts.
* **The fee is charged differently.** ETFs charge an expense ratio at the fund level. Each Ondo Intelligent Portfolio charges an [annual service fee](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/fees-taxes) that accrues daily and is reflected in the token’s value. There is no separate charge and nothing to pay directly.
* **Distributions are handled differently.** Many ETFs pay dividends out to shareholders. Ondo Intelligent Portfolios are total return trackers: dividends on the portfolio’s holdings are reinvested back into the portfolio assets (net of applicable [withholding taxes](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/fees-and-taxes)) and reflected in the token’s value over time.

Some Ondo Intelligent Portfolios hold tokenized ETFs among their underlying assets. That does not make the portfolio itself an ETF.

### How is it different from holding Ondo tokenized stocks individually?

Portfolio tokens are built on the Ondo Stocks platform, and each constituent is itself an Ondo tokenized stock/ETF that can be minted and redeemed against traditional market liquidity. Every Ondo Intelligent Portfolio is backed by the same tokenized stocks and ETFs that are available individually on the Ondo Stocks platform, invested according to a professionally-constructed target allocation. The difference is the packaging and the maintenance.Building the same exposure yourself would mean sizing and minting each position individually, tracking how the weights drift as prices move, and trading back to target on your own schedule. With a portfolio token, one mint gives you the whole basket, and the portfolio is designed to [rebalance back to its base weights](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/rebalancing-reconstitution) approximately every quarter without any action from you.Two things do work differently from a single-asset token, and they follow from the fact that a portfolio is a basket rather than one security:

* **Availability depends on the whole basket.** If any constituent asset is closed for trading, minting and redemption are paused for that portfolio until the full basket is open again. Mint/redeem availability for single-asset Ondo stock tokens only depends on its own underlying asset.
* **The token price will not match the price of any one underlying asset.** The displayed price is the per-token value of the portfolio, calculated from the [weighted basket value](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/pricing) of the underlying portfolio assets, not the price of any single stock in it.

### How else are Ondo Intelligent Portfolios different?

A few other key differences between Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens and some other tokenized basket or index token implementations are that our assets are:

* [Transferable 24/7](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/transferability), subject to certain transfer restrictions
* [Compatible with DeFi](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/transferability)
* Structured to provide [strong investor protections](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/trust-and-transparency)

# Corporate Actions

Copy pageCopy page

How corporate actions on underlying assets affect portfolio tokens.

Copy pageCopy page

### How do corporate actions affect Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens?

A corporate action is any event initiated by a company that affects its securities — such as dividends, stock splits, mergers or acquisitions, spin-offs, and rights offerings.Because each portfolio is backed by a basket of Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs, corporate actions are handled at the constituent level. See [corporate actions handling](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/corporate-actions) for Ondo tokenized stocks.Price changes from corporate actions at the constituent level flow through to the portfolio token’s price. There is nothing you need to do, and your portfolio token balance does not change.

### Is minting and redemption paused during corporate actions?

It can be. If a corporate action pauses trading in any constituent asset — for example, around an ex-dividend date, or while an ETF distribution amount is being finalized — minting and redemption are paused for every portfolio holding that asset, until the constituent is trading again.These pauses affect minting and redemption only. Your tokens remain transferable and tradable on secondary markets. You can always check the current status of each portfolio and its constituents at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/).

### What happens if an asset in a portfolio is delisted, acquired, or otherwise ceases to trade?

It depends on whether the portfolio has a fixed or dynamic asset set:

* **Fixed portfolios** (reconstitution shown as “Fixed”): if a constituent is affected by a terminal corporate action — such as a delisting, cash acquisition, or liquidation — the asset is removed and its weight will be rebalanced among the remaining constituents.
* **Dynamic portfolios**: the portfolio model may be adjusted at the model provider’s discretion — including whether the affected asset is replaced or its weight redistributed.

Any resulting change to a portfolio’s composition is reflected on its product page at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/) and visible onchain. Minting and redemption for the affected portfolio may be paused while the change is processed.

### Where can I learn more about how specific corporate actions work?

For detailed treatment of dividends, ex-dividend dates, trading pauses, and other corporate action mechanics at the asset level, see [Corporate Actions](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/corporate-actions) under Ondo Stocks.

# Fees & Taxes

Copy pageCopy page

The annual service fee and how portfolio tokens are taxed.

Copy pageCopy page

### What fees do Ondo Intelligent Portfolios charge?

Each portfolio charges an annual service fee. The service fee covers the ongoing operations and maintenance of the portfolio, including rebalancing and investing and divesting the underlying assets.Minting or redeeming portfolio tokens will also incur a mint/redemption fee at the constituent level, in accordance with individual Ondo Stock fees. [Learn more](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/fees-and-taxes).In addition, when a quote is generated for an investor to buy (or sell) a portfolio token, the price may be slightly more (or less) than the price at which the platform buys (or sells) the underlying assets, with any difference and any fees being retained by the platform. Investors are responsible for their own gas costs.(Note that if portfolio tokens are acquired in the secondary market, fees may be charged to you by other parties.)

### How does the service fee work?

The service fee is an annual rate that accrues daily and is reflected in the portfolio token’s value — there is no separate charge and nothing to pay directly. Your token balance does not change; the fee is already accounted for in the token’s price. Fee rates vary by portfolio and are shown on each portfolio’s product page on [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/).

### How are Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens taxed?

Because each portfolio is backed by a basket of Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs, withholding taxes apply at the constituent level. Dividends or other income paid by the underlying securities are subject to withholding taxes before reinvestment at the constituent level, and the after-withholding total return flows through to the portfolio token’s price.The Ondo entity that issues the tokens is based in the British Virgin Islands (BVI), so dividends paid by US securities to the entity are subject to the standard rates for BVI entities. Withholding rates vary by the type of underlying asset — for example, dividends from US companies are generally withheld at 30% for the BVI-based issuer, portions of US fixed-income ETF distributions may be exempt, and non-US companies listed as ADRs are typically subject to their home country’s withholding instead. See detailed [Fees & Taxes](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/fees-and-taxes) for Ondo tokenized stocks. Because each portfolio holds a mix of assets, the portfolio’s effective withholding rate is a blend of its constituents’ rates. Each portfolio’s product page at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/) shows its dividend yield both before and after estimated withholding.No other taxes (e.g., capital gains, income) are withheld, nor are any taxes withheld when tokens are redeemed. However, investors may be subject to tax in connection with redeeming, purchasing or holding tokens, depending on the laws of the jurisdictions in which they are subject to tax, as well as their own particular tax circumstances. Each investor is solely responsible for understanding, reporting and fulfilling his, her, or its respective tax obligations. We strongly suggest that each investor consult his, her, or its own tax advisor to determine their tax obligations associated with redeeming, purchasing or holding Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens.

# Investing & Redeeming

Copy pageCopy page

How investing and redeeming work, and how base and current weights differ.

Copy pageCopy page

Investing in and redeeming Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens generally works the same way as Ondo tokenized stocks. See [Investing & Redeeming](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/investing-and-redeeming) under Ondo Stocks for details.

### What are base weights and current weights?

Base weights are the model’s target allocations: the percentage of the portfolio that each asset is intended to represent, as defined by the model provider. Current weights are the percentage each asset actually represents at a given moment, based on the current market value of the portfolio’s holdings.At each portfolio’s inception, the underlying assets are calibrated to the model’s base weights. As market prices move, the actual weights may naturally drift away from base weights — assets that outperform grow to a larger share of the portfolio, and assets that underperform shrink. This drift is normal and expected. Each portfolio is designed to [rebalance](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/rebalancing-reconstitution) back to its base weights approximately every quarter.Portfolio weights are shown on each portfolio’s product page at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/).

### Are mints and redemptions processed at base weights or current weights?

They differ.When you mint, your investment is put to work according to the model’s target allocations — Ondo invests your funds into the portfolio’s assets at the model’s base weights.When you redeem, your redemption is processed against the portfolio’s current weights — redemptions return your share of the existing portfolio, which reflects how weights have drifted since the last rebalance.

# Market Hours & Trading Availability

Copy pageCopy page

When portfolio tokens can be minted and redeemed, and when they can’t.

Copy pageCopy page

### When is the platform open for minting and redeeming Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens?

Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens are generally available to mint and redeem during regular US market hours from 9:30:00am ET to 3:45:00pm ET whenever [US markets are open](https://www.nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars) — that is, during the Core Trading Session only, with the window closing 15 minutes before the 4:00pm regular market close.For platform and security reasons, minting and redemption may be briefly unavailable around the market open and close.That said, there are some times when specific portfolios (and/or the platform generally) may not be available. You can always check the exact status of what is and isn’t available on the product page at app.ondo.finance.

### Why are the mint/redeem hours narrower than for Ondo tokenized stocks?

Minting or redeeming a portfolio token involves buying or selling every asset in the portfolio’s basket. To provide the best pricing and execution, these basket trades are executed during the Core Trading Session, when liquidity in the underlying assets is deepest. The window closes 15 minutes before the 4:00pm close so that all underlying trades can be completed while the market is still open.

### Are all portfolios always available during the mint and redemption window?

In general, yes, but there are exceptions. See the next question.

### Under what conditions is minting and redemption halted?

**All Portfolios**

* Outside of the mint and redemption window described above
* On all [NYSE Market Holidays](https://www.nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars)
* If certain proprietary platform-level risk control thresholds are met
* During scheduled or emergency platform maintenance

**Specific Portfolios**

* If any constituent asset in the portfolio is halted or unavailable for trading — for example, during a corporate action affecting that asset — minting and redemption are paused for the whole portfolio until the full basket is available again
* If certain proprietary asset-level risk control thresholds are met for any constituent
* During scheduled or emergency maintenance affecting the portfolio or any constituent

Note: You can always check portfolio-level availability on each portfolio’s product page at app.ondo.finance.

### Are Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens available for Off-Hours trading?

No. A select set of single-asset Ondo tokenized stocks are available to trade during an Off-Hours session when the conventional sessions are closed, but Off-Hours availability is enabled on a per-asset basis and does not currently extend to portfolio tokens. See [Off-Hours Trading](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/off-hours-trading) for how that session works for single-asset Ondo Tokenized Stocks.

### Can I still transfer or trade portfolio tokens when minting and redemption are closed?

Yes. The mint and redemption window applies only to minting/redeeming directly with the Issuer. The tokens themselves are freely transferable onchain 24/7 (subject to certain jurisdictional and other restrictions) and can be traded on secondary markets at any time. Note that when the traditional US markets are closed, liquidity in the underlying constituent assets are limited, so spreads may widen and a portfolio token’s price may diverge more significantly from the underlying asset value in the portfolio.

# Model Providers

Copy pageCopy page

What model providers do and who the current providers are.

Copy pageCopy page

### What is a model provider?

A model provider develops the portfolio strategy, selecting the assets and setting their base weights. Every Ondo Intelligent Portfolio follows target allocations defined by its model provider, which may be Ondo or an institutional partner working with Ondo.

### What are the roles of the model provider vs the token issuer?

The model provider develops the portfolio strategy while Ondo implements and manages each portfolio onchain — executing the underlying basket trades, running the [rebalancing process](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/rebalancing-reconstitution) back to base weights, and operating [minting and redemption](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/investing-redeeming).The model provider does not issue, sponsor, manage, or administer the portfolio tokens, does not hold or custody any assets backing them, and does not have any relationship with, or obligations to, tokenholders.

### Who are the current model providers?

Our current portfolio lineup includes portfolios constructed by:

* **BlackRock** — the BLKHIon, BLKDIGon, and BLKGRWon are portfolio strategies developed by BlackRock for Ondo.
* **Ondo** — the MAG7Xon, BRAINon, YLD5on, YLD8on, BIG5on, and SPYSHIELDon are portfolio strategies developed by Ondo.

We intend to add more portfolios and model providers over time. Visit the portfolio product page at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/) to see each portfolio’s model provider.

### What are Ondo Intelligent Portfolios?

Ondo Intelligent Portfolios are curated investment portfolios, built on the Ondo Stocks platform. Each portfolio is a professionally constructed basket of tokenized stocks and ETFs, delivered as a single onchain token.Instead of buying, weighting, and rebalancing many individual positions, investors outside the US can hold one token to gain exposure to a diversified investment strategy. Each portfolio’s target asset allocations are set by a model provider — either Ondo or an institutional partner working with Ondo — and implemented and managed onchain by Ondo. Like Ondo tokenized stocks, portfolio tokens carry the `on` suffix. For example, MAG7Xon is the Ondo Magnificent 7 and Crypto Portfolio.Ondo Intelligent Portfolios provide economic exposure only. Holders of portfolio tokens do not own the underlying stocks or ETFs directly. Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens are issued by Ondo Global Markets (BVI) Limited (the Issuer), the same issuer as Ondo tokenized stocks. Ondo supports the Issuer, including by powering the Ondo Stocks platform. For further information, please see the offering documents associated with Ondo tokenized stocks.

### What are the most important things I should know?

# Token Quote & Pricing

Copy pageCopy page

How a portfolio token’s price is determined and how quotes work.

Copy pageCopy page

### How is the price of an Ondo Intelligent Portfolio token determined?

The price of a portfolio token reflects the weighted value of its constituent assets. Each token is backed by a basket of Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs, plus any cash in transit, shown as a per-token value:Price per token = (sum (constituent token price × quantity held) + cash in transit) ÷ total portfolio tokens outstandingBecause each constituent is itself an Ondo tokenized stock and a total-return tracker, dividends on the underlying assets are reinvested at the constituent token level (net of any applicable [withholding taxes](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/fees-and-taxes)) and reflected in each constituent’s price. That total return flows through to the portfolio token’s price automatically. The portfolio token therefore reflects both price movements *and* reinvested income across every asset in the basket.

### Can you give me an example?

Let’s take a simplified portfolio, SAMPLEon, holding two Ondo tokenized stocks at 50% each. At inception, assume the basket backing one portfolio token is worth $100 — $50 of Token A and $50 of Token B.If Token A rises 10% and Token B is unchanged, the basket is now worth $105, so the portfolio token’s price is $105.Dividends work the same way, because each constituent is itself a total-return tracker. When the stock underlying Token A pays a dividend, that dividend is reinvested at the constituent level (net of applicable withholding taxes) and reflected in Token A’s price — the same way all Ondo tokenized stocks work. The portfolio simply holds Token A, so the increase flows through to the portfolio token’s price automatically. There is no separate dividend handling at the portfolio level, and there is never anything for you to claim or reinvest.Over time, this means the price of a portfolio token reflects the total return of the full basket — including price movements and reinvested income across every constituent. Current holdings and per-token value are shown on each portfolio’s product page at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/).

### What is a total-return tracker?

Total-return trackers are financial assets that are designed to mirror the complete performance of an index or asset. Unlike *price* return trackers, which only capture an asset’s market value changes, *total* return trackers account for all cash flows — dividends, interest, or capital gains distributions — assuming these are reinvested to compound returns (net of applicable withholding taxes).A total-return tracker includes:

* Price movements – gains or losses in the assets’ market value
* Income distributions – such as dividends (for stocks) or interest (for bonds)
* Corporate actions – like mergers that may affect total value

By capturing both capital gains and income, total-return trackers offer a more comprehensive reflection of performance than price-only trackers.

### When I am actually buying or selling a portfolio token, the quoted price seems to be slightly different from the chart price on the product page. Why?

When you indicate that you want to buy or sell a portfolio token, we provide you a quote for a price that’s guaranteed for some amount of time. To generate that quote, the platform automatically looks at existing inventory, market conditions, and other factors and then does a little bit of (proprietary) math to figure out what an appropriate quote price should be for that order.For a portfolio token, that quote accounts for the cost of buying or selling *every asset in the portfolio’s basket*, each of which has its own bid-ask spread in the underlying market. The “main” price you see on the price chart of the product page is our estimate of the per-token value of the portfolio’s holdings, whereas the quote price is the result of our proprietary method for determining at what price we can guarantee we can buy or sell that number of tokens across the full basket. Those numbers should never be *too* different from one another, but that’s where the discrepancy comes from.(Please note that if you are buying a portfolio token on the secondary market, whomever you are transacting with may be charging their own fees. You are also responsible for your own gas costs.)

### Is there price history from before a portfolio’s launch?

Each portfolio’s live price data starts from its inception date. Where a portfolio token’s pre-inception price performance is shown, it is hypothetical, based on backtesting, and clearly labeled as such — it does not represent actual results. Performance after inception reflects actual results. Past performance, whether actual or hypothetical, does not guarantee future results.

# Rebalancing & Reconstitution

Copy pageCopy page

How portfolios rebalance to base weights and how reconstitution works.

Copy pageCopy page

### What is rebalancing?

At each portfolio’s inception, the underlying assets are calibrated to the model’s base weights. As market prices move, the actual weights may naturally drift away from base weights — assets that outperform grow to a larger share of the portfolio, and assets that underperform shrink. This drift is normal and expected. Rebalancing adjusts the portfolio’s holdings back to the model’s base weights. It changes how much of each asset the portfolio holds, not the asset mix in the portfolio.

### How often does a portfolio rebalance?

Portfolios generally follow a quarterly rebalance cadence, returning to their base weights each quarter. Each portfolio’s rebalance approach and frequency are different and set by its model provider.Each portfolio’s rebalance frequency and most recent rebalance date are shown on its product page at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/).

### Do I need to do anything when a portfolio rebalances?

No. Rebalancing is executed by Ondo on behalf of all holders and requires no action from you. Your token balance does not change when a portfolio rebalances — the composition of the basket backing each token is adjusted, and every rebalance is visible onchain.

### What is reconstitution?

Reconstitution changes which assets are in the model — adding or removing constituents — rather than adjusting the weights of existing ones. Not every portfolio reconstitutes: some portfolio models define a fixed set of assets, while others are dynamic. Any changes to a portfolio’s composition are visible onchain.

### How often does reconstitution happen?

There is no fixed schedule. For dynamic portfolios, reconstitution happens at the model provider’s discretion, when the provider updates its model. Portfolios with a fixed asset set generally do not reconstitute and show a reconstitution of ‘Fixed’ on their product page. The exception is a terminal corporate action, such as a delisting — see [Corporate Actions](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/corporate-actions) for how these are handled. Check each portfolio’s product page at [app.ondo.finance](https://app.ondo.finance/) for its reconstitution approach.

### What is the difference between rebalancing and reconstitution?

Rebalancing adjusts how much of each asset the portfolio holds, returning portfolios to the model’s base weights — the assets stay the same.Reconstitution changes which assets are in the model, adding or removing constituents. All portfolios rebalance; only some reconstitute.

# Troubleshooting

Copy pageCopy page

Common minting and redemption issues and what to check first.

Copy pageCopy page

### I’m having trouble minting or redeeming a portfolio token. What should I check first?

First check whether the portfolio you are trying to mint or redeem is currently available. For portfolio tokens, keep in mind:

* **Minting and redemption are only available from 9:30 a.m. to 3:45 p.m. ET Monday to Friday when US markets are open.** Outside this window, including on US market holidays, minting and redemption are closed — this is expected, not an error. See [Market Hours & Trading Availability](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/market-hours).
* **A portfolio pauses if any of its constituent assets is paused.** If a single asset in the basket is halted — for example, around an ex-dividend date or a corporate action — minting and redemption are paused for every portfolio holding that asset, even if the rest of the basket is trading normally. The pause lifts when the constituent resumes. Visit app.ondo.finance and navigate to each portfolio token’s product page to check its current status.

If you are still having issues, you can contact us at [support@ondo.finance](mailto:support@ondo.finance).

### The portfolio’s current weights don’t match its base weights. Is something wrong?

Each portfolio’s product page shows both its base weights and its estimated current weights. Current weights naturally drift from base weights between rebalances as market prices move: assets that outperform grow to a larger share of the portfolio, and assets that underperform shrink. The asset breakdown chart on the product page reflects the portfolio’s estimated current weights, so some difference from base weights is expected. Each portfolio is designed to rebalance back to its base weights approximately every quarter. See [Rebalancing & Reconstitution](https://docs.ondo.finance/intelligent-portfolios/rebalancing-reconstitution).

### Something else?

For all other issues see [Troubleshooting](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/troubleshooting) under Ondo Stocks.**Please note that we may only be able to support users minting or redeeming via our API or web app directly**. If you are trying to buy or sell portfolio tokens on the secondary market, please contact the market directly.
