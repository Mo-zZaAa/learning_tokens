## FAQ

What is the Ondo Private Client Program?

The Ondo Private Client Program is a loyalty program offered by the Ondo Foundation to our ecosystem's greatest supporters. Private Clients are entitled to personalized support channels, gifts, early access to new features, and events. Private Client tiers are separated by account activity levels, which the program tracks directly from your qualifying wallet.

Who is eligible?

Eligible non-U.S. wallets may qualify for Private Client status by meeting either an assets-held threshold or a cumulative spot trading-volume threshold. For Platinum status, users must hold at least $1M in qualifying assets, such as Ondo Stocks and USDY, or reach at least $1M in rolling 30-day spot trading volume. Diamond and Obsidian unlock at higher qualifying thresholds.

How do I check if I qualify?

Connect your wallet directly at https://app.ondo.finance/private-client. If you qualify, your tier and benefits display immediately.

Is there a cost to join?

No. Private Client status is earned through onchain activity. It cannot be purchased and doesn’t require an application.

What if I don't qualify yet?

Keep building your position on Ondo Stocks. Status as an Ondo Private Client unlocks automatically the moment eligible users cross the qualifying threshold.

How do I know an offer is really from Ondo?

We will never ask you to click a link in a direct message to claim status. Always navigate directly to https://app.ondo.finance/private-client and connect your wallet there to verify.
