# Ondo Finance Docs: Tokenized Stocks, ETFs and Treasuries

Copy pageCopy page

Institutional-grade financial products and services available to everyone.

Copy pageCopy page

Ondo Finance is a blockchain-based technology company focused on tokenizing real-world assets and bringing institutional-quality financial products onchain, from U.S. stocks, ETFs, and Treasuries to curated portfolios and exposure to leading private companies. By bridging traditional finance and decentralized infrastructure, Ondo Finance aims to make capital markets more accessible, transparent, and efficient. These docs explain how each Ondo-tokenized product works, its key features, who can access it, and how to build with it.

## Products

## Ondo Stocks

450+ tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs with 24/7 minting and redemption\*

## Ondo Intelligent Portfolios

Curated portfolios of tokenized stocks and ETFs, delivered as a single token\*

## Ondo Private Markets

Tokenized notes designed to provide economic exposure to leading private companies\*

## USDY

US Dollar Yield Token, a tokenized note secured by U.S. Treasuries funds\*

## OUSG

Ondo Short-Term US Government Treasuries fund, tokenized for institutions and high net-worth individuals

\* for eligible investors outside the U.S.
