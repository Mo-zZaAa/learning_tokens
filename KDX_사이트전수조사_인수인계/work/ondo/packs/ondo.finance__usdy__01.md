## FAQ

What is a yieldcoin?

A yieldcoin is a token backed by cash-equivalent real-world assets like US Treasuries that also accrues yield daily. USDY is a yieldcoin, but not a stablecoin. Like stablecoins, USDY operates as a permissionless bearer asset that can be transferred to without needing to be onboarded first. Unlike stablecoins, however, USDY accrues yield every day. [Learn more about yieldcoins](https://docs.ondo.finance/general-access-products/usdy/comparison-stablecoins)

Who is eligible to buy USDY?

USDY is currently accessible to non-US individuals and institutional investors. Additional eligibility restrictions apply. You can learn more about eligibility and availability requirements [here](https://docs.ondo.finance/general-access-products/usdy/eligibility).

Who can mint and redeem USDY?

USDY is offered under a "Regulation S" exemption from the SEC. This exemption requires that Ondo only offer this product to non-US individuals or entities. Likewise, USDY can only be redeemed by non-US persons individuals or entities. Additional restrictions apply. [Learn more about who can mint and redeem USDY](https://docs.ondo.finance/general-access-products/usdy/eligibility)

How can USDY be lower risk than stablecoins?

We have worked closely with our attorneys, among other specialists, to design the structure to comply with securities laws, tax laws, anti-money laundering laws, and other regulatory requirements. USDY is a type of bearer note that is distributed to non-US investors in accordance with Regulation S under the US Securities Act of 1933, as amended.

Creating a product that meets these requirements does impose some limitations, however—for example, we can only redeem to USD via bank wire to non-US bank accounts. [Learn more about how USDY is lower risk than stablecoins](https://docs.ondo.finance/general-access-products/usdy/basics)

Where is USDY available?

You can buy directly from Ondo once you complete onboarding. [Begin Onboarding](https://app.ondo.finance/assets/usdy)

USDY can also be traded peer-to-peer on the blockchains on which it is issued.

[See our full FAQ](https://docs.ondo.finance/general-access-products/usdy)
