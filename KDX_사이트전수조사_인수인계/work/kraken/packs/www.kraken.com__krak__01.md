FAQs

Is Krak a bank?

Krak operates under Kraken, a leading financial technology company. While not a bank, Krak offers innovative, technology-driven financial services.

Are there any hidden fees?

Most of our services are free to use. Whenever a fee applies, the exact amount will always be shown clearly within the app before you proceed. You can find full details about applicable fees in our [fee schedule](https://www.kraken.com/features/fee-schedule).

How do I deposit money in my Krak Account?

To [buy or trade crypto on Krak,](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360051144472-buying-crypto-on-kraken) first deposit funds using a supported currency below. Choose your local currency to see available deposit options. The most common method is bank transfer.

How do you open an account online through Krak?

To open a Krak app account, download the app from the iOS App Store or Google Play, tap "Create an account”, and enter your email, a secure password, and your country of residence. Alternatively, you can log in directly using existing Kraken exchange credentials if you already have one.
