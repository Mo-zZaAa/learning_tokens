## What is Kraken Borrow?

Kraken Borrow lets the crypto you hold become additional buying power, without needing to sell from your portfolio. Kraken shows how much you can purchase based on your cash balance and the value of your eligible crypto, up to 3 times the value of your eligible assets. Your cash is spent first and your buying power covers the rest when you need it, with the full terms shown before you confirm your order.

## Kraken Borrow FAQs

Can I buy more crypto without selling what I already own?

Yes. Kraken lets you use crypto you already hold as collateral to raise your buying power and buy more, in a single flow, without selling.

How much can I borrow against my crypto?

Up to 3 times the value of your eligible assets, including the crypto you're buying. For example, $10,000 in eligible collateral at a 5% haircut gives up to $28,500 in buying power. Check the Borrow Center for your personal figure.

Do I keep ownership of my crypto when I borrow against it?

Yes. The crypto you buy is yours and you keep full exposure to its price. The portion bought with your buying power is held as security until you repay, so it can't be withdrawn or sent during that time — but you can sell it whenever you want.

What happens if my collateral value drops?

The Borrow Center shows your borrow health as Healthy, Caution, or At risk. At Caution you may get a margin call asking you to add eligible assets or repay part of what you owe. At At risk, some or all of your crypto may be sold automatically to cover what you owe. This can happen quickly and without additional notice, so don't rely on getting a warning first.

Is there a fixed term, and can I repay early?

There's no repayment deadline and no minimum payment. Repay partially or in full at any time — with USD for free, or by selling any asset you hold, where a conversion fee applies.

Where is Kraken Borrow available?

Available to US residents in 48 states, excluding New York and Maine.

Which crypto assets can I use as collateral?

Eligible crypto you already hold on Kraken, including BTC and ETH, each with its own collateral weighting.
