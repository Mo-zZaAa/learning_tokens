# Price Predictor — Daily Prediction Game

*Official Rules — Ink Points Reward*

## Opening / Acceptance

Price Predictor is a free daily prediction game. No purchase, deposit, trade, or other financial action of any kind is required to play or to receive an Ink Points reward. By playing Price Predictor, you agree to these Official Rules (the "Rules") and to the Kraken Terms of Service referenced in Section 13 below.

*For clients in the European Economic Area, trading services are separately provided by Payward Europe Solutions Ltd. ("PESL"); for clients in Australia, by BitTrade Pty Limited; in each case as the regulated entity clients face for underlying Kraken services, distinct from Sponsor's role as administrator of this promotion.*

## Section 2 — Eligible Areas

The Promotion is open to eligible customers residing in the European Economic Area ("EEA"), the United States (excluding Maine and New York), Canada (excluding Quebec), Australia, and other jurisdictions where Kraken services are available and Price Predictor has been enabled. Residents of the United Kingdom are not eligible to participate. Void where prohibited.

## Section 3 — Promotion Period

The Promotion is ongoing, commencing upon launch and continuing on a rolling daily basis until suspended or terminated by Sponsor. Each calendar day (00:00:00–23:59:59 UTC) constitutes one "Play Period."

## Section 4 — How to Enter / How to Participate

To participate, an eligible individual must: (a) hold an individual Kraken Pro account in good standing; (b) access the Price Predictor feature within the Kraken Pro application; and (c) select "Higher" or "Lower" as their prediction for the direction of the Reference Asset's price over the applicable ten (10) second window. Each eligible account may play once per Play Period. "Reference Asset" means BTC, or such other digital asset as Sponsor may designate for a given Play Period.

No purchase, deposit, trade, or other financial transaction of any kind is required to play. Participation is fully decoupled from trading volume and financial activity.

## Section 5 — Prize Distribution

A correct prediction results in an automatic, guaranteed award of Ink Points, drawn from the following fixed distribution: 5 Ink Points (70% of correct predictions); 10 Ink Points (20%); 20 Ink Points (8%); 50 Ink Points (2%). An incorrect prediction results in no Ink Points award for that Play Period. The specific award amount for a correct prediction is determined automatically and immediately upon result determination; Sponsor exercises no discretion over the outcome.

## Section 6 — Prize / Reward Details

Reward: Ink Points as described in Section 5. Ink Points have no cash value, are not redeemable for cash, cryptocurrency, or any other item of value, and are non-transferable, consistent with the Kraken Ink Points Program Terms (incorporated by reference).

## Section 7 — Kraken Account and KYC Requirement

Participation requires an individual Kraken Pro account in good standing. No minimum KYC tier is required to play or to receive an Ink Points award, as Ink Points carry no monetary value. Sponsor may require additional verification where necessary to comply with applicable law or to administer the Promotion.

## Section 8 — Eligibility

Open only to individual accountholders described in Section 2 who are of legal age in their jurisdiction of residence. Not open to: (a) accounts flagged for fraud; (b) closed accounts; (c) API-only, institutional, corporate, or account-managed accounts. By participating you agree to these Rules and to the applicable Kraken Terms of Service referenced in Section 13.

## Section 9 — Disqualification

Sponsor reserves the right to disqualify any participant who: submits fraudulent information; uses bots, scripts, or automated means to play; circumvents the one-play-per-day limit through multiple accounts or otherwise; or engages in any conduct Sponsor deems, in its sole discretion, to be a threat to the integrity of the Promotion.

## Section 10 — Notification and Fulfilment

Ink Points are credited automatically to the participant's account immediately following each Play Period. No separate winner notification process applies, as every correct prediction results in an automatic award.

The Promotion runs in recurring monthly cycles (each, a "Draw Period"). ## Section 4 — How to Enter

Entry is automatic and free. Every time an eligible participant plays the daily prediction game (governed by separate Official Rules) during a Draw Period, they receive one entry ("ticket") into that month's draw, regardless of whether their prediction is correct. There is no separate or additional step required to enter the draw beyond playing. No payment, deposit, trade, or fee of any kind unlocks additional entries; entries cannot be purchased at any price.

## Section 5 — Winner Selection

One winner is selected per Draw Period by random drawing from all eligible tickets issued during that Draw Period, weighted only by number of plays (more plays = proportionally more tickets = proportionally higher odds; no bonus weighting for correct predictions, streaks, or any other factor). Sponsor exercises no discretion over the outcome of the draw. If the initially selected participant does not meet the eligibility criteria in Section 8, the next eligible candidate is considered, and so on, until a winner is confirmed.

## Section 6 — Prize / Reward Details

One (1) winner per Draw Period receives one (1) BTC, credited directly to the winner's Kraken account.

Approximate retail value (ARV) as of August 14, 2026: approximately USD 63,500, based on the market price of BTC on Kraken as of that date. Actual value at time of award will differ due to BTC price volatility.

The prize is non-transferable and may not be exchanged, redeemed for cash, or substituted, except that Sponsor reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to substitute a prize of equal or greater value in the event BTC cannot be awarded for any reason.

This requirement applies to prize fulfilment only and is not a condition of entry or participation.
