## What is tokenized American Airlines Group (AALx)?

Tokenized American Airlines Group (AALx) is a tokenized stock—a type of blockchain-based asset that represents American Airlines Group Inc. Common Stock shares. Each AALx digital token is backed 1-1 by real American Airlines Group Inc. Common Stock shares, held by a third-party custodian.

Trading xStocks like AALx provides indirect exposure to publicly-traded company shares, but it does not constitute ownership of the underlying American Airlines Group Inc. Common Stock stock. As such, holding tokenized equities does not convey any shareholder rights.
