# Kraken Market Participation Program

# Empowering institutional partners with aligned growth

[Join the program](https://www.kraken.com/institutions/market-participation-program)

Kraken’s new Market Participation Program is designed to strengthen long-term collaboration with our most engaged trading partners.

By offering a unique equity-linked rewards model, we align our success with yours — creating shared value as you help drive liquidity, market depth and growth across Kraken’s spot and futures markets.

## How the program works

## Clients trading at significant volume levels are eligible for the following benefits.

Reduced fees

Enjoy access to a new, ultra-competitive 5 bps spot trading tier.

Equity-linked rewards

Earn equity-linked incentives based on your trading activity — a first-of-its-kind program among major exchanges.

Performance transparency

Rewards are calculated weekly, allowing for clear, measurable results.

## Program highlights

Open participation

The program is available to all Kraken institutional clients (some geo restrictions apply) who meet trading volume thresholds and regulatory requirements

Aligned incentives

Unlike traditional fee rebates, participants can earn rewards linked to Kraken’s long-term growth

Built for performance

Participants benefit from Kraken’s latest infrastructure improvements, including enhanced FIX API, upgraded throughput and expanded co-location options for ultra-low-latency trading

Fair and transparent

All terms are standardized, ensuring equitable access and clear qualification criteria.

Why choose Krakens Market Participation program

Institutional trading firms are familiar with liquidity-based incentives — but Kraken’s program goes further. Our next-generation model aligns partners with Kraken’s growth through equity-linked rewards and clear, enforceable performance standards.

Program overview

Reward type

Equity-linked incentive warrants

Reward frequency

Allocated weekly and distributed at the end of the program

Eligibility

Qualified institutions reaching Kraken aggregate trading volume minimum thresholds (1% spot or 2% futures)

Market coverage

Crypto Spot and Futures only (excluding stablecoin pairs)

FAQs

Are stablecoin volumes included?

No, stablecoin trading volume is excluded from the program’s eligibility criteria.

How are equity incentives delivered?

Rewards will be distributed in the form of warrants, subject to applicable regulatory and legal conditions.
