What is the Kraken OTC Desk?

Kraken’s over-the-counter (OTC) desk offers a premium service that allows traders to execute orders off the open Kraken exchange. We offer deeper liquidity for tighter spreads as well as a more private, personalized service for institutional clients and high net-worth individuals needing to fill large orders of Bitcoin. Kraken’s OTC desk provides execution and settlement services that are discreet, secure and ultra-competitive.

### About Bitcoin

Bitcoin is the world's largest cryptocurrency project. The bitcoin (BTC) cryptocurrency was the first of its kind to be built on blockchain technology. Launched in 2009 by an anonymous developer under the pseudonym Satoshi Nakamoto, Bitcoin remains the most widely accepted and traded cryptocurrency today. Like the cryptocurrencies that followed it, Bitcoin was created to provide a decentralized electronic cash system that operates without the intervention of a centralized authority. A global team of developers constantly works on improving Bitcoin and its underlying technology.

OTC FAQs

What is the OTC Portal?

The [OTC Portal](https://pro.kraken.com/app/otc/quote) is a self-service system that enables OTC clients to get executable, automated quotes for supported cryptoassets, including:

* Customized price charts based on your trading habits
* Position exposure information
* Recent transactions and OTC trading history
* RFQ access

What cryptocurrencies do we support?

**Trade over chat:**

* All [asset pairs listed](https://support.kraken.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000678446-Cryptocurrencies-available-on-Kraken) on the Kraken exchange (subject to geographic [restrictions](https://support.kraken.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001368823-Geographic-Restrictions-Can-I-use-Kraken-if-I-m-from-))

**RFQ**:

* Over [20 assets, including stablecoins](https://support.kraken.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021967711) (subject to geographic [restrictions](https://support.kraken.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001368823-Geographic-Restrictions-Can-I-use-Kraken-if-I-m-from-))

**OTC Derivatives:**

* BTC and ETH are supported for options trading (subject to geographic [restrictions](https://support.kraken.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001368823-Geographic-Restrictions-Can-I-use-Kraken-if-I-m-from-)).
* OTC Derivatives services are only available to OTC clients in certain jurisdictions. [Learn more](https://support.kraken.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021967711-Introducing-the-Kraken-OTC-Desk) about OTC Derivatives.

What is the minimum trade size?

The minimum trade amount is an equivalent value of $100,000 USD. There is no maximum size for trading over chat.

Is there a fee?

Kraken OTC Desk does not charge any fees. The bid or offer price we show is the 'all-inclusive' price.

How do I get started?

You must be verified with a Pro level Kraken exchange account to access the OTC desk.

*Please find our verification level [requirements](https://support.kraken.com/hc/en-us/articles/201352206-Verification-level-requirements) here.*

**Personal accounts:**

**Business accounts:**

<https://support.kraken.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004862007-Pro-accounts-for-Corporations-and-Institutions>

How does settlement work?

* Trade obligations must be settled within 24 hours
* Crypto can be settled to/from your Kraken exchange wallet or sent to the external wallet of your choice
* USD can be settled to/from your Kraken account or wired directly to your bank account
* AUD can be settled directly to/from your domestic bank account
* Other fiat currencies available for trading on Kraken must be settled to your Kraken account

Your settlement options:

* Flexible settlement arrangements allow you to settle trades with your Kraken account, your bank, or an external wallet of your choice within 24 hours
* [RFQ](https://pro.kraken.com/app/otc/quote) offers a fully automated trade and settlement feature, in addition to flexible settlement (subject to eligibility):
  + **Automated settlement** allows you to trade and settle with your Kraken balance. Your Kraken account must be funded, and once your trade is filled your assets will be settled into your Kraken account within seconds.
  + **Flexible settlement**, available to eligible clients, allows you to settle trades within 24 hours via your Kraken account, bank or an external wallet.

### Terms and eligibility

* Minimum order size is $100,000, however exceptions can be discussed on a case-by-case basis.
* Kraken OTC does not custody assets on behalf of trading counterparties.
* Eligibility for OTC trading is subject to AML/KYC and other requirements.
