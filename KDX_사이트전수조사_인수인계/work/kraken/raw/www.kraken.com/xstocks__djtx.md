## What is tokenized Trump Media & Tech Gr (DJTx)?

Tokenized Trump Media & Tech Gr (DJTx) is a tokenized stock—a type of blockchain-based asset that represents Trump Media & Technology Group Corp. Common Stock shares. Each DJTx digital token is backed 1-1 by real Trump Media & Technology Group Corp. Common Stock shares, held by a third-party custodian.

Trading xStocks like DJTx provides indirect exposure to publicly-traded company shares, but it does not constitute ownership of the underlying Trump Media & Technology Group Corp. Common Stock stock. As such, holding tokenized equities does not convey any shareholder rights.
