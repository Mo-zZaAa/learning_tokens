# How to buy, sell and convert xStocks on Kraken

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Only Kraken clients in [eligible regions](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-availability) can buy and sell US-listed xStocks instantly, 24/5. UK-listed and EU-listed xStocks can be bought, sold and converted only while their exchange is open. See [Market hours explained](https://support.kraken.com/articles/market-hours-explained).

Buy xStocks

1. 1

   Log into your Kraken account [here](https://www.kraken.com/).
2. 2

   On the right hand side, click **Buy**.
3. 3

   Search for or browse through the list of xStocks, then click the xStock you want to buy.
4. 4

   Enter the amount you want to buy using your account balance or any other method. In this example we’ll be using EUR, but you can also choose to buy using your debit or credit card or Apple Google Pay.
5. 5

   Review the transaction details and click Confirm.
6. 6

   That’s it! You can view your completed transaction by clicking View transaction or find them under the **Activity** tab on the left hand side.

Sell xStocks

1. 1

   Log into your Kraken account [here](https://www.kraken.com/session-expired).
2. 2

   On the right hand side, click **Sell**.
3. 3

   Search for or click the xStock you want to sell.
4. 4

   Enter the amount you want to sell.
5. 5

   Review the transaction details and click **Confirm**.
6. 6

   That’s it! You have successfully sold your xStock.

Convert xStocks

1. 1

   Log into your Kraken account [here](https://www.kraken.com/session-expired).
2. 2

   On the right hand side, click **Convert**.
3. 3

   Search for or click the xStock you want to convert from and which xStock you want to convert to. Enter the amount. You can’t convert between US-listed and UK-listed or EU-listed xStocks at launch.
4. 4

   Review the transaction details and click **Confirm**.
5. 5

   That’s it! You have successfully converted your xStock.
