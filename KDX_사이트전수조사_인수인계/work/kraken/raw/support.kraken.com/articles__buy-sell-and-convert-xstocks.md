# How to buy, sell and convert xStocks on the Kraken app

Last updated: August 27, 2025

On the Kraken app clients in [eligible regions](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-availability) can buy, sell and convert xStocks instantly, 24/5.

## To buy xStocks:

1. 1

   Open the Kraken app.
2. 2

   Tap the **Trade** button, then select **Buy**.
3. 3

   Search for or browse through the list of xStocks, then tap the xStock you want to buy.
4. 4

   Enter the amount you want to buy using your account balance. In this example we’ll be using EUR, but you can also choose to buy using your debit or credit card or apple google buy.
5. 5

   Review the important details and tap **Confirm**.
6. 6

   That’s it! You can view your completed transaction by tapping **View transaction** or find them under the **Activity > Transactions** tab.

## To sell xStocks:

1. 1

   Open the Kraken app.
2. 2

   Tap the **Trade** button, then select **Sell**.
3. 3

   Search for or tap the xStock you want to sell.
4. 4

   Enter the amount you want to sell.
5. 5

   Review the important details and tap **Confirm**.
6. 6

   That’s it! You have successfully sold your xStock.

## To convert xStocks:

1. 1

   Open the Kraken app.
2. 2

   Tap the **Trade** button, then select **Convert**.
3. 3

   The first field is the asset or xStock you want to **Convert from.** The second field is for the asset or xStock you want **Convert to.**
4. 4

   Next, input the amount you would like to convert. If everything looks right, tap **Continue** to review.
5. 5

   Tap **Confirm** to complete your conversion.

## Important reminder

[xStocks are available for trading on Kraken 24 hours a day, five days a week.](https://support.kraken.com/articles/market-hours-explained)
