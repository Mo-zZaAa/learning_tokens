# xStocks Perps on Kraken Pro

Last updated: February 24, 2026

[영상]

xStocks Perps (Perpetual Futures) let you trade select traditional-market exposures as **perpetual futures**, with crypto-native features like **24/7 trading** and **leverage**. Click [here](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-perps-kraken) for the full list of xStocks available as xStocks Perps.

What are xStocks Perps?

xStocks Perps are **perpetual futures contracts** that track the price of certain major market benchmarks, such as:

* •

  **AAPLx** (Apple)
* •

  **CRCLx** (Circle Internet Group)
* •

  **GLDx** (Gold exposure)
* •

  **GOOGLx** (Alphabet Inc)
* •

  **HOODx** (Robinhood Markets Inc)
* •

  **MSTRx** (Strategy Inc)
* •

  **NVDAx** (Nvidia Corp)
* •

  **QQQx** (Nasdaq 100 exposure)
* •

  **SPYx** (S&P 500 exposure)
* •

  **TSLAx** (Tesla Inc)

Because they are perpetuals, they can be traded **24/7**, without traditional market open/close constraints.

Key features

### 24/7 trading

Trade several stocks, the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, and Gold exposure around the clock, including weekends and holidays.

### Leverage (up to 20x)

xStocks Perps support **up to 20x leverage**, allowing you to take larger positions with less upfront capital (which also increases risk).

### Flexible collateral

Use the same collateral you already use for [margin](https://support.kraken.com/articles/204585998-collateral-currency) or [Derivatives](https://support.kraken.com/articles/4843323030164-derivatives-collateral-currencies), including **stablecoins and supported crypto assets**.

### Long or short

Like other perpetual futures, you can take positions in either direction, depending on your market view.

Note:

xStocks Perps is only available in certain regions, with more coming soon!

## Trading xStocks Perps

How to trade xStocks Perps on the Kraken Pro website

1. 1

   Go to [pro.kraken.com](https://pro.kraken.com/) and sign in to your Kraken account.
2. 2

   After signing in, you can immediately find xStocks Perps using the Market selector in the top left corner.
3. 3

   After clicking the market selector, click the **Futures filter** to see only Futures markets.

   You may also use the search bar to search for a specific xStock Perps market. **Remember, at this time, only [certain xStocks](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-perps-kraken) are available as xStocks Perps.**
4. 4

   Then, use the **Categories** filter to only display xStocks.
5. 5

   On the Trade page, you can then use the **Order Form** widget to place a buy or sell order. First, you’ll want to select whether you’d like to buy or sell, then select the order type.
6. 6

   Next, follow the [instructions in this article](https://support.kraken.com/articles/13614490585620-placing-your-first-derivatives-trade-on-kraken-pro) for placing an order on a futures market.

How to trade xStocks Perps on the Kraken Pro app

1. 1

   Open the Kraken Pro app, sign in if needed.
2. 2

   xStocks Perps can be found on the **Markets** tab of Kraken Pro in the bottom left corner, but you may also search for xStocks Perps by tapping the **Search** bar at the top of the screen.

   **Remember, at this time, only [certain xStocks](https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-perps-kraken) are available as xStocks Perps.**
3. 3

   Tap the xStock Perps market you’d like to trade. This will take you to the **Trade** page for this xStock market.
4. 4

   Next, follow the [instructions in this article](https://support.kraken.com/articles/13614490585620-placing-your-first-derivatives-trade-on-kraken-pro) for placing an order on a futures market.
