# xStocks Points Program FAQ

Last updated: March 26, 2026

The xStocks ecosystem has launched a points program (xPoints) that rewards onchain activity involving xStocks tokenized equities. Below are answers to common questions about how this relates to your Kraken account.

Does my Kraken activity earn xStocks points?

No. Holding or trading xStocks on Kraken does not currently earn points in the xStocks Points Program. The program is currently based on onchain ecosystem activity only.

What is the xPoints Program?

The xStocks Points Program (xPoints) rewards participants for engaging with xStocks through onchain activities. You can learn more at [xstocks.fi/points](https://xstocks.fi/us/news/introducing-xpoints).

Will Kraken support the points program in the future?

Kraken may explore ways to support xStocks ecosystem incentives at a later stage. Stay tuned to official Kraken channels for updates.

Are points on xstocks.fi connected to my Kraken account?

No. The xStocks Points Program operates separately from your Kraken account. Points earned through onchain activity are not reflected in or connected to Kraken.

Where can I learn more about the xStocks Points Program?

Visit [xstocks.fi/points](https://xstocks.fi/us/news/introducing-xpoints) for full details on how to participate in the onchain points program, also keep an eye on xStocks social media channels.
