# How do I look up my Canadian bank account information?

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Prevent deposit delays

If required and shown in the deposit instructions, make sure your deposit includes a [reference](/articles/360000381026-Why-is-the-deposit-reference-important-) to your Kraken [Public account ID](/articles/360028555092-How-to-find-your-account-number-Public-Account-ID-) which starts with AA and is 16 characters long.

A common issue for our Canadian clients when adding their Canadian bank account to Kraken for receiving [CAD withdrawals](/articles/360000411303) is entering the incorrect:

* •

  Bank code
* •

  Transit number
* •

  Account number

You can find these numbers by looking at a check for your account:

[그림: Funding\_CADBankAccountInfoCheque\_10072020.png]

If you do not have a check book for your account, you can request a **void check** document from your bank's branch or website.
