# 0% Maker Fees on Futures Trading on Kraken Pro

Last updated: August 24, 2026

**Until 31 March 2025,** if your futures trading volume is higher than your **January 2025 or February month to date (MTD) levels,** your additional maker/taker fees in excess of your January 2025 or February month to date fees are capped at **0% and 0.005% bps**. The cap will be achieved by means of Kraken depositing a rebate. Eligible volume tiers still get maker rebates.

Proof of external-venue activity required.
