# xStocks now available on Kraken Pro

Get fast, flexible exposure to U.S. stocks and ETFs — now in Kraken Pro for eligible non-U.S. clients.

Trade popular U.S. stocks and ETFs 24/5 – self-custodial, and on your terms.

We’re thrilled to announce that tokenized U.S. equities – powered by Backed’s groundbreaking xStocks innovation – are now live on [Kraken Pro](https://pro.kraken.com/).

For the first time, **eligible non-U.S. clients** can get instant, flexible exposure to some of America’s most iconic stocks and ETFs, directly from Kraken Pro’s advanced web, mobile app & [desktop](https://www.kraken.com/desktop).

[Trade xStocks on Kraken Pro](https://proapp.kraken.com/OBT6/hoe8qklv)

## **A new way to trade U.S. equities**

[xStocks](https://kraken.com/pro/xstocks) are tokenized representations of real-world equities, backed 1:1 by the underlying stock or ETF. Meaning you can trade them 24/5 on Kraken Pro, or withdraw them anytime to your wallet, and use them across DeFi protocols — all while benefiting from tight spreads.

We’ve launched with 60 tokenized assets — including names like Apple (AAPLx), Tesla (TSLAx), and the S&P 500 ETF (SPYx) — and we’ll continue expanding the list over time.

## **Why trade xStocks on Kraken Pro?**

With xStocks now in Kraken Pro, clients can:

* **Trade xStocks like crypto**: Buy and sell tokenized U.S. equities alongside digital assets, derivatives, and more.
* **Use all the Pro tools**: Access [advanced charting](https://blog.kraken.com/product/pro/charting-updates), price alerts and [9+ order types](https://pro.kraken.com/app/trade/btc-usd) including Iceberg, Trailing Stop and Take Profit.
* **Execute strategies with precision**: Tap into full market depth, live order books and low-latency trade feeds.
* **Access markets beyond market hours**: Trade 24/5, Sunday 8:00 p.m. ET through Friday 8:00 p.m. ET, and self-custody for 24/7 onchain use.
* **Invest flexibly**: Get started with as little as $1 and withdraw xStocks to a self-hosted wallet for maximum control.
* [**Competitive fees**](https://www.kraken.com/features/fee-schedule): At 0.1% for taker and rebate of -0.02% for maker volume.

With fractional shares, and no FX conversion required, Kraken Pro is now one of the most efficient ways to gain exposure to U.S. equities — with none of the legacy frictions.

## **One platform. All of your assets.**

xStocks make Kraken Pro the ultimate platform for traders and investors who want it all — tokenized stocks, crypto, derivatives, and more — in one seamless experience.

Whether you’re placing trades on the go, managing your portfolio from your desktop or using our [APIs](https://docs.kraken.com/api) for advanced strategies, Kraken Pro empowers you to:

* Build a diversified portfolio of tokenized assets
* Trade fast, with low latency and minimal slippage
* Control your assets via self-custody — anytime, anywhere

And with the addition of xStocks, you can now plan and execute multi-asset strategies from a single, professional-grade interface.

Get started today and experience the next frontier of finance — right inside Kraken Pro.
