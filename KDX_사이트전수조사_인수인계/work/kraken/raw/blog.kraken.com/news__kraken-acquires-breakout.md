If you’re an experienced crypto trader looking to scale your strategy — without putting personal funds on the line — we’ve got something new for you.

**[Kraken has acquired Breakout](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250904012956/en/Kraken-Completes-Major-Acquisition-of-Breakout-to-Offer-Prop-Trading-to-Clients-Globally)**, a prop trading platform that lets you access up to $200,000 in live trading capital and keep up to 90% of your profits. Just pass a one-step evaluation and start trading. There’s no deposit required (other than the cost of the evaluation).

[Get funded with Breakout](https://breakoutprop.com/kraken)

## **What is Breakout?**

[**Breakout**](https://kraken.com/breakout)is a **crypto-native proprietary trading firm** built to empower traders. By demonstrating your skill through a trading evaluation, you can get funded with up to $200,000 in funded capital and keep up to 90% of the profits.  
  
Now backed by **Kraken’s infrastructure and global platform**, Breakout allows traders to size up and scale their edge with **zero personal capital at risk**.

### **How it Works:**

1. Sign up at [breakoutprop.com/kraken](https://breakoutprop.com/kraken) with the email linked to your Kraken account
2. Pass the evaluation and qualify for up to $200K in trading capital
   * **Enter promo code KRAK for a 2% evaluation discount**
3. Start trading [50+ crypto pairs](https://www.breakoutprop.com/symbols) with low latency, deep liquidity and tight spreads
4. Keep up to 90% of profits, with fast USDC payouts

ℹ️ *Learn more about Breakout’s equity drawdown rules* [*here*](https://www.breakoutprop.com/article/mastering-drawdown-a-guide-to-equity-limits-at-breakout)*.*

## **Why Kraken x Breakout?**

* **✅ Up to $200K in funded trading accounts**
* **✅ No personal capital required (other than the cost of the evaluation)**
* **✅ Transparent rules and evaluation process**
* **✅ Up to 90% profit share with on-demand payouts**

Whether you’re already active or just breaking out, this is your chance to level up!
