**Submission Process**

* The design submission period will be from January 8, 2025 (00:00 UTC) to January 20, 2025 (23:59 UTC).
* Participants must download the Designated Design File from the Campaign Landing Page, incorporate their design within the provided areas, and submit it via the Binance Survey available on the same page.
* All entries must be submitted in a high-resolution image format (PDF, PNG, JPG, etc.) and must adhere to the design and brand guidelines provided.

**Prizes**

* The grand prize winner will:

  + Have their helmet design featured on Pierre Gasly's helmet during the Australian Grand Prix.
  + Take home a signed replica of Pierre’s designed helmet (estimated value: 3500 EUR).
  + Have the opportunity to join the BWT Alpine F1 Team, along with a plus one, at the Grand Prix closest to the participant's location. The Race Day Experience includes race day access only and does not include flights or accommodation.

Binance will not be liable for any additional costs incurred by the winners in relation to customs and taxes. **Winner Selection**

* 10 shortlisted designs from the Binance Survey submissions will be chosen by the Binance team based on creativity and alignment with the spirit of motorsport. The final winner will be selected by Pierre Gasly. The decision of the judging panel will be final and binding.

**Judging Criteria**

At the heart of our Helmet Design Contest lies a commitment to creativity and excellence. To ensure a fair evaluation, we've outlined clear criteria and a meticulous selection process. Your design will be assessed against the standards below.

* Originality and Creativity: Submissions will be evaluated based on the uniqueness and creativity displayed in the design. Designs that demonstrate innovative and fresh perspectives will have an advantage.
* Relevance to Theme: Entries that effectively capture the essence of the Formula 1 racing spirit and align with the Australian Grand Prix theme will be favorably considered.
* Helmet Sponsor Placement: Designs should ensure that helmet sponsor logos are prominently and appropriately placed as per the provided guidelines.
* Technical Execution: The technical proficiency of the design's execution, attention to detail, and skill in working with the provided helmet template will be taken into account.
* Aesthetic Appeal: The overall visual appeal, color palette, and visual harmony of the design will contribute to its evaluation.
* Reflection of Binance's Values: Designs that resonate with the Binance brand, innovative ethos and align with the campaign's goals of connecting the motorsport community and celebrating collaboration will be a crucial factor in the selection process.

**Notification of Winners**

* The grand prize winner will be notified via the email address provided in the Design Survey by March 11 (23:59 UTC) at the latest.
* Those who do not respond to the email within 3 days of notification will be considered forfeiting their prizes.
