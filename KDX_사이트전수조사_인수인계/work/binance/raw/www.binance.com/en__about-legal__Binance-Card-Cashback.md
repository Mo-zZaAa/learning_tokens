Binance Brazil Card is delighted to introduce a bonus **up to 3% cashback program** for qualified users, featuring **higher cashback rates across monthly tiers and a raised monthly cap from 120 BRL to 250 BRL**, with the reached tier determining the rate applied to all eligible transactions for that calendar month.

**How to Participate?**

1. Make eligible purchases with your Binance Brazil Card between December 2025 to February 2026.
2. Use your virtual or physical card at Mastercard-accepting merchants to accumulate monthly spend tiers; higher tiers increase your cashback rate for that month.
3. Cashback in BRL on settled, eligible purchases will be credited to your Funding Account by the 10th day of the following month.

**User Eligibility:**

1. Users located in Brazil where Binance Card is offered.
2. Users who have completed all required account verification (KYC).
3. Fulfilled Binance Card issuance requirements at Binance’s sole discretion.

**FAQs:**

1. How to participate in the Binance Brazil Card bonus cashback program and earn rewards?

- The Binance Brazil Card offers bonus cashback based on your total spending each calendar month during the period between December 2025 to February 2026. As you reach higher tiers, the increased cashback rate is applied retroactively to all eligible transactions for that month.

2. Who can participate?

- Users in Brazil who apply for a Binance Card and complete KYC verification.

4. Where can I see the earned cashback?

- You can see the cashback rewards under your Binance Card dashboard under **[Cashback]**.

5. How will I receive the cashback?

- Cashback is calculated on settled\*, eligible purchases and credited to your account by the 10th day of the following month. Cashback will be credited in BRL. Binance has the right to the final interpretation of whether the transaction is a subject of the cashback reward.

**\*"Settled"** purchase or transaction means that the funds of this purchase have arrived in the bank account of the seller, e.g., your grocery store. The process of settlement is performed by the payment network and may take **up to 30 days**. 

6. What if I get a refund?

- In case of a refund or other items that affect historically calculated spending, Binance has the right to claw back the cashback.

7. What are Monthly & Lifetime Cashback Earnings?

- Monthly Cashback Earnings are the cashback rewards you have accumulated for the month, and will be distributed to your Funding Account on the 10th of the next month.

- Lifetime Cashback Earnings are cashback rewards you receive in your Funding Account at the beginning of the cashback program.

8. Is this cashback campaign only applicable to Virtual or Physical cards?

- It applies to both cards. Either of the Virtual or Physical card meeting the spend requirement will get the rewards.

9. Where can I spend my card?

- Use your card anywhere the Mastercard logo is shown, at millions of stores worldwide, online, and by phone.

10. What transactions are not eligible for cashback?

- **Please note** that cashback won’t apply to these transactions, including but not limited to:

* ATM withdrawals, cash disbursements, or any transaction that works like taking out cash.
* Payments to financial institutions (like debt repayment, account funding, brokers, dealers, or investment platforms).
* Government-related payments, such as taxes, fines, court costs, or postal services run by the government.
* Gambling transactions.

\*Eligibility of cashback for transactions is up to the sole discretion of Binance.

11. Is there a monthly limit for the amount of cashback I can earn?

- Yes, the monthly limit for bonus cashback is **capped at 250 BRL**.
