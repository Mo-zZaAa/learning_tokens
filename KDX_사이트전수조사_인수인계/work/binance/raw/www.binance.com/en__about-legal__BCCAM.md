#### Activity Rules:

Binance Card is pleased to announce that qualified users are eligible to receive an exclusive $2 USD worth of reward in crypto when they apply for the card and make a purchase within 30 days of successful card approval, as a token of appreciation.

**How to Participate?**

* Apply for a Binance Card between 2026-02-01 00:00 (UTC) and 2026-02-18 23:59 (UTC).
* Once your card application is approved, spend a minimum of $5 USD equivalent on your first card transaction (in single receipt) using the virtual card at any Mastercard-accepting merchant within 30 days.
* Once you meet the minimum spending requirement,  $2 USD worth of reward in crypto will be credited to your Binance Funding Account.

**User Eligibility:**

* Users located in Armenia who request access to the Binance Card and its related services.
* Users who have completed all required account verification (KYC).
* Fulfilled Binance Card issuance requirements at Binance’s sole discretion.

1. **How to Participate in the Binance Card Welcome Offer and get the reward?**  
   - Request access to the Binance Card - Global Program and successfully apply for the card between 2026-02-01 00:00 (UTC) and 2026-02-18 23:59 (UTC), and spend a minimum of $5 USD equivalent on your first transaction using the card within 30 days to receive $2 USD worth of rewards in crypto.
2. **Who can participate?**   
   - Users in Armenia who apply for a Binance Card and complete KYC verification.
3. **When will I receive the reward?**   
   - $2 USD worth of reward in crypto will be credited to your Binance Funding Account after meeting the spend requirement.
4. **What if I get a refund?**  
   - The reward may be adjusted proportionally if the transaction is refunded.
5. **Where can I spend my card?**  
   - Use your card anywhere the Mastercard logo is shown, at millions of stores worldwide, online, and by phone.
6. **What transactions are not eligible for reward?**   
   Please note that reward won’t apply to these transactions, including but not limited to:

   1. Cash disbursements, or any transaction that works like taking out cash.
   2. Payments to financial institutions (like debt repayment, account funding, brokers, dealers, or investment platforms).
   3. Government-related payments, such as taxes, fines, court costs, or postal services run by the government.
   4. Gambling transactions.

      \*Eligibility of reward for transactions is up to the sole discretion of Binance.
