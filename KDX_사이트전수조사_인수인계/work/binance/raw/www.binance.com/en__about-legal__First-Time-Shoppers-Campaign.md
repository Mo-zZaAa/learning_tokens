**Terms & Conditions**

This Promotion offers an instant one-time discount of 50% off with no minimum spend required, capped at 2 USDT equivalent per user on Binance Pay transactions made during the Promotion Period.

Each Binance Pay user who fulfils the aforementioned Promotion criteria is eligible for one instant discount per transaction during the whole Promotion Period.

The discount is applied automatically at checkout.

The discount cannot be combined with other offers. If multiple discount campaigns are ongoing, the system will automatically apply the most favorable discount available to the user.

In the event of a refund, only the amount paid after the discount will be refunded.

The discount applies only to eligible transactions between 2026-03-11 00:00 (UTC) and 2026-04-10 00:00 (UTC), or until the Promotion budget for the period is fully used.
