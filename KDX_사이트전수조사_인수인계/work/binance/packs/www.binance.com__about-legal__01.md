[Binance Pay](https://pay.binance.com/en/merchant-stores?tab=1) is excited to announce a new promotion for **all Binance Pay users,** where eligible users can enjoy up to 15% off for mobile top-ups (capped at 3 USDT). 

**Promotion Period: 2026-05-01 00:00 (UTC) to 2026-05-31 23:59 (UTC)**

During the Promotion Period, eligible Binance Pay users who meet the following minimum spending requirements stand to enjoy exclusive discounts at the following rates:

* Spend a minimum of **1 USDT** and enjoy 15% off, discount capped at **3 USDT**. \*Capped at 20 transaction per user

Start saving on your mobile expenses with Binance Pay today! 

## How to Purchase a Mobile Top-Up via Binance Pay:

* **Step 1:** Login to the latest [Binance App](https://binance.onelink.me/y874/803c5d7d) (v2.80.4 or later).
* **Step 2:** Tap the **[Pay]** icon in the top-right corner of the homescreen.
* **Step 3:** Select **[Mobile Top Up]** and complete your purchase.
