# Celebrate the Holidays with Dinari: 12 Days of Asset Launches and dShares Giveaway! 🎄🎁

Rachel Ling

•

Dec 19, 2024

We are thrilled to announce our 12 Days of dShares —a festive celebration where we’ll launch a new dShare asset, and give away a dShare gift card every day!

The holiday season is here, and we at Dinari are excited to bring you something special! To show our gratitude for your incredible support and celebrate all our accomplishments in 2024, we’re thrilled to announce our **12 Days of dShares** —a festive celebration where we’ll launch a new dShare asset, and give away a dShare gift card every day!

This year has been remarkable for Dinari. We've surpassed 4,000 transactions and reached up to $11.5 million in Total Value Locked (TVL). We’ve onboarded 10+ API partners, bringing our assets to the hands of users around the world through payroll app, neobanks, crypto exchanges, and more (not to mention DeFi). And we’re only just getting started

Starting **December 20th and running through December 31st**, we’ll announce the addition of **a new dShare asset every day** on our platform. But that’s not all! To make this celebration even more exciting, we’ll also post a **NVDA.d dShare gift card** on one of our social channels each day. Make sure to follow all our channels so you don’t miss out!

‍

### **How It Works:**

Each day, we’ll reveal a new tokenized asset for trading and post a link to claim the gift card on one of our social media platforms.

1. **Follow Dinari** on all our social media channels to stay updated ([Twitter](https://x.com/dinariglobal), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dinari-global), [Farcaster](https://warpcast.com/dinariglobal), [Discord](https://discord.gg/dinari), [Telegram](https://t.me/dinariglobal), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCqZhF9TOkU66Z_YIlJP661Q/about))

2. **Check our channels daily** for the special giveaway link drop

3. **Be the first to click** and claim the NVDA.d gift card!

‍

### **A Giveaway Every Day!**

Each NVDA.d gift card unlocks a unique opportunity to own a tokenized asset, bringing you closer to the future of finance. This is our way of spreading holiday cheer and introducing more people to the power of tokenized stocks and ETFs.

Don’t miss this exclusive chance to earn your first dShares or add more to your portfolio!

‍

### **Stay Tuned**

The holidays are all about giving, and we can’t wait to celebrate with our amazing community. Make sure to follow us across our social media channels for daily updates on the new dShare asset launches and the giveaway links. Let’s make this holiday season one to remember!

Start exploring Dinari’s tokenized assets today at [sbt.dinari.com](https://sbt.dinari.com/)

Happy Holidays from all of us at Dinari! ✨

‍

### **Other Details**

Users must complete KYC verification on the Dinari platform ([sbt.dinari.com](https://sbt.dinari.com/)) and must meet requirement criteria (including not be from unsupported countries or regions including the USA) to claim any rewards. Gift cards are limited to one per user.
