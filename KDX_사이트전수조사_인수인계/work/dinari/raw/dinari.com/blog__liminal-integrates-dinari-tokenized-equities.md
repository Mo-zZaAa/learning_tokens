# Liminal Integrates Dinari to Bring Tokenized U.S. Equities to Its AI Financial Platform

Dinari

•

Jul 29, 2026

Managing money has traditionally meant using different products for banking, payments, savings and investing. As financial services become more connected, platforms are beginning to bring those capabilities together into a single experience.

Liminal combines multi-currency banking, cross-border payments, savings and investing in a self-custodial, AI-powered financial platform designed for globally connected users. Through its integration with Dinari, Liminal now offers over 272 U.S. stocks and ETFs directly within the platform, making investing another native financial capability alongside the rest of its products.

## Bringing Investing Into Everyday Finance

For many consumers, investing remains separate from the rest of their financial lives. While banking and payments have become increasingly seamless, investing often still requires opening and managing a dedicated brokerage account.

By integrating Dinari, Liminal makes investing another native capability within its platform. Users can access tokenized U.S. equities alongside the financial tools they already use, all through a conversational AI interface designed to simplify everyday financial decisions.

*"Our vision is to make every part of a person's financial life accessible through one intelligent, AI-powered platform. Investing is a natural extension of that experience, but it requires infrastructure that is both seamless and trusted. Dinari gives us a regulated way to bring tokenized U.S. equities to our users, allowing us to expand what's possible within Liminal while staying focused on delivering a simple, unified financial experience," said Fifi Tsamados, CEO and Co-Founder of Liminal.*

## Why Dinari

Dinari enables fintech platforms to offer tokenized U.S. stocks through a single integration, allowing partners to expand investment offerings without building brokerage infrastructure from the ground up.

Dinari enables fintech platforms to integrate tokenized U.S. equities without recreating the brokerage stack or compromising on investor protections. Built on the custodial model Dinari pioneered, dShares™ preserve the rights and protections associated with traditional securities, including a protected claim to the underlying security, while maintaining key investor benefits such as NBBO execution, dividends, and corporate actions.

As tokenized equities become more widely available, not every product offers the same level of ownership. While some models provide only synthetic price exposure, Dinari's custodial approach gives partners a regulated way to offer tokenized U.S. equities backed by real underlying securities. For platforms like Liminal, that means bringing investing onchain without sacrificing the trust, protections and market functionality investors expect.

*"We're seeing a new generation of financial platforms emerge that don't treat investing as a standalone product. Instead, it's becoming a core part of a broader financial experience. Liminal is building exactly that, and we're excited to help bring regulated tokenized U.S. equities to a platform that's making investing more accessible alongside the financial tools people use every day." said Gabriel Otte, Co-Founder and CEO of Dinari.*

## Building the Next Generation of Consumer Finance

Liminal launched publicly earlier this year on iOS and Android and is available to users globally. The platform is designed around a self-custodial model, allowing users to maintain control of their assets while using AI to simplify financial decision-making.

As more financial products become programmable, integrations like this make it possible for users to access payments, savings and investing through a single intelligent interface. By bringing tokenized U.S. equities to Liminal, Dinari continues to expand the reach of U.S. equities to the next generation of consumer financial applications.
