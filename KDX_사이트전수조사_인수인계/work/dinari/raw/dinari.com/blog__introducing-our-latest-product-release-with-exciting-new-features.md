# Introducing Our Latest Product Release with Exciting New Features!

Jake Timothy

•

Oct 12, 2023

We are thrilled to announce a series of significant enhancements to our platform that will elevate your trading experience to new heights.

We are thrilled to announce a series of significant enhancements to our platform that will elevate your trading experience to new heights. At Dinari, we're dedicated to providing you with the most cutting-edge tools and features in the most transparent fashion possible, and these latest updates are a testament to that commitment. From increased control through limit orders to simplified dividend distribution and fortified security measures, our goal is to make your investment journey smoother than ever before.

## What's in the Release?

1. **Improved Handling of Transfer Restrictions and Cancellations:** Ensuring the safety of your assets is our utmost priority. With improved handling of transfer restrictions and cancellations, we have additional checks and processes built into our contracts to secure your assets. To be clear, no vulnerabilities were discovered where an attacker could steal funds from the escrow contracts. These improvements reduce certain user error scenarios.
2. **Limit Orders:** Our new system now supports purchases and redemptions using limit orders, giving you increased control over your execution. Set the price you're comfortable with, and let our system take care of the rest.
3. **Stock Split Handling:** Understanding that stock splits can be a complex affair, we’ve built a token split and migration handler we can activate when a stock split occurs. Whether it's a forward or reverse split, our platform will automatically adjust and represent your holdings accurately.
4. **Dividend Distribution:** Receive dividends directly in your account with our enhanced dividend distribution feature. Your rewards for being a token holder are now seamlessly managed and deposited. We currently distribute dividends in the form of USDC. Register your account to start receiving dividends today.
5. **Paying for Network Fees in Stablecoin:** In our bid to provide more flexibility and stability, we've introduced the option to cover network fees using stablecoins. This feature not only simplifies fee payments but also offers a shield against the volatility of other cryptocurrencies. No more worrying about multiple tokens just to place an order. Deposit USDC into your account or use our onramp providers and start trading today.

These features correspond to our [smart contracts release version 0.1.0](https://github.com/dinaricrypto/sbt-contracts/releases/tag/v0.1.0)  and subsequent deployment.

As part of our commitment to maintain transparency and trust, we underwent a comprehensive security audit. These updates mark a milestone in our ongoing journey to provide you with the best trading experience possible. Your security and satisfaction are our top priorities, and we believe that these improvements reflect that dedication. As we continue to evolve and innovate, we look forward to your valuable feedback and insights. Be sure to explore our latest offerings and, for a more detailed look at our security enhancements, refer to our latest security audit report. Thank you for choosing Dinari as your trusted partner in the world of trading and investment.
