## New dShares: Energy (Exxon, Chevron)

We are pleased to introduce dShares four and five in our series of 10 new dShares, featuring energy leaders [Exxon](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=XOM.d) and [Chevron](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=CVX.d).

Stay tuned for our next dShare release Wednesday, June 26th, featuring the last of our 10 new dShares marking the start of dShare Summer where we are rolling out a series of exciting new dShare offerings over the next few months.

### New dShares: Exxon and Chevron

* [**ExxonMobil ($XOM)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=XOM.d) is one of the largest publicly traded international oil and gas companies, with operations in more than 20 countries. They produce approximately 2.4 million barrels of oil equivalent per day.
* [**Chevron ($CVX)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=CVX.d) operates in oil, natural gas, and petrochemicals. Their projects include hydrogen, renewable fuels, and carbon capture. In 2023, Chevron produced over 3 million barrels of oil equivalent per day and reported revenues exceeding $150 billion

### About dShares
