# USD+ and stUSD+ Launch on Arbitrum One

Jake Timothy

•

Mar 8, 2024

Dinari USD+ & stUSD+ are now available on Arbitrum One in addition to Ethereum Mainnet. Learn about the benefits of a compliant, 1:1 backed, yield-bearing Stablecoin Protocol at usd.dinari.com

**A Stablecoin for Stability and Growth**‍

We are proud to launch our multi-chain stablecoins USD+ and stUSD+ on Arbitrum One. USD+ and stUSD+ offer a solid foundation of US Dollar-pegged stability (with guaranteed 1:1 redemptions), coupled with the potential for growth based on fixed income assets.  
  
Our yield-bearing stablecoin has a 1:1 US dollar value, reliably backed by short-term US Treasuries! USD+ and stUSD+(StakedUSD+), have been live on Ethereum Mainnet for the past month and we are very excited about deploying on Arbitrum One. You can find the dapp to mint and burn USD+ and stUSD+ here: https://usd.dinari.com/  
  
Users can mint new USD+ tokens by depositing stablecoin (currently USDT, USDC, USDC.e) of equivalent USD value, and similarly, they can redeem USD+ tokens for stablecoin of equivalent USD value. When users then deposit their new USD+ tokens into the Staked USD+ contract, they become eligible to earn yield (if staked for longer than the minimum staking period). Digital asset reserves are held publicly in an on chain treasury for easy auditing and verification.  
  
  
**Benefits of USD+ on Arbitrum one**‍

Arbitrum is a Layer 2 scaling solution for Ethereum, offering faster transactions and significantly lower fees than the Ethereum mainnet. By deploying USD+ on Arbitrum, we aim to improve the user experience and make our stablecoin more accessible to a broader range of users by offering:

* Lower Transaction Fees: users can transact with USD+ at a fraction of the cost compared to the Ethereum mainnet. This makes it more affordable for users to interact with Dapps and manage their stablecoin holdings.
* Faster Transactions: fast confirmation times ensure that USD+ transactions are processed quickly, allowing users to take advantage of time-sensitive opportunities in DeFi.
* Increased Adoption: By introducing USD+ on Arbitrum, we can attract a larger user community, encouraging growth with a TVL of $3.475B (as per DeFiLlama\*) and boosting the overall adoption of our stablecoin.
