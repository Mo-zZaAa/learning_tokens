# Dinari dShares Now Live on Base

Rachel Ling

•

Aug 27, 2024

Dinari dShares are now Live on Base. Tokenized US stocks are accessible to qualified non-US users through our 1:1 backed dShares (subject to applicable law). Learn more on the Dinari blog.

We’re pleased to announce that Dinari’s full suite of dShares is now live on Base! This launch is a major milestone for Dinari as we continue our mission to democratize access to the global stock market through innovative, blockchain-based solutions.

‍

## **Why Dinari dShares on Base?**

Base is a Layer 2 Ethereum blockchain launched by Coinbase, ranking among the fastest-growing blockchains. With over 20 million users and an average of 3.8 million daily transactions1, Base has quickly gained popularity. It also boasts more than $1.3 billion in Total Value Locked2 (TVL). By launching on Base, we’re tapping into one of the largest and most active user bases, bringing our dShares to a wider audience and enabling faster, more cost-effective trading experiences.

Dinari is revolutionizing the investment landscape by making tokenized US stocks accessible to qualified non-US users through our 1:1 backed dShares (subject to applicable law). These tokenized assets provide investors with exposure to real-world securities, combined with the 24/7 liquidity and flexibility of decentralized finance (DeFi). With dShares, investors can participate in a seamless onchain trading experience.

‍

### **Expanding Global Liquidity and Access**

Our launch on Base is more than just a new offering—it’s a key step in expanding global liquidity and access to Real World Assets (RWAs). Dinari’s dShares are designed to be easily integrated into DeFi protocols, allowing for 24/7 trading and yield opportunities beyond the traditional market hours. We’re excited to roll out liquidity pools on Base’s DeFi ecosystem, providing more trading activity and better yield opportunities for our users.

High gas fees on other networks have been a challenge for many, but Base’s efficient infrastructure ensures lower transaction costs and faster settlement times. This aligns perfectly with our goal of making our assets accessible to as many users as possible, regardless of their location or financial background.

‍

### **Join Our Ecosystem**
