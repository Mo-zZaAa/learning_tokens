# Dinari Joins Blockchain Association to Advance the Future of Tokenized Securities Policy

Dinari

•

Jul 21, 2026

Membership brings Dinari's expertise in regulated, custodial tokenized securities to Washington as policymakers establish the framework for onchain capital markets.

Dinari, the pioneer of the custodial model for tokenized U.S. public equities, today announced it has joined Blockchain Association, the leading trade association representing the digital asset industry in Washington, D.C. As policymakers continue shaping the future of tokenized securities, Dinari will contribute its expertise to help advance thoughtful regulation that supports innovation while preserving investor protections.

## Bringing Regulated Capital Markets Onchain

Since pioneering the custodial model for tokenized equities, Dinari has worked to bring U.S. capital markets onchain through regulated infrastructure built on the belief that tokenization should enhance existing market infrastructure, not replace it.

Through its dShares™ platform, Dinari enables financial institutions to offer tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs that preserve the rights and protections of traditional securities while unlocking the benefits of blockchain technology, including guaranteed redemption at the National Best Bid and Offer (NBBO), cash dividends, automated corporate actions, and streamlined tax processing.

As an SEC-registered transfer agent, the company has built infrastructure that enables broker-dealers, exchanges, fintechs, and other regulated financial institutions to issue, custody, trade, and service tokenized securities within the existing regulatory framework.

## A Pivotal Moment for Tokenized Securities Policy

The announcement comes at a pivotal moment for tokenized securities in the United States. In its January 2026 statement on tokenized securities, the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission described a custodial model in which a third party holds an issuer's securities and issues crypto assets representing a holder's entitlement to the underlying security.

Dinari pioneered this custodial approach and has continued working with regulators and market participants to develop infrastructure that aligns blockchain innovation with the protections and market structure of traditional securities markets.

"We're thrilled to welcome Dinari to Blockchain Association and look forward to bringing their expertise on tokenized securities and blockchain-based capital markets into the policy conversations taking place in Washington," said Summer Mersinger, CEO of Blockchain Association. "Together, we can help shape thoughtful regulatory frameworks that support the future of tokenized securities in the United States."

## **Building the Dinari Financial Network**

In addition to its dShares™ platform, Dinari is building the Dinari Financial Network, an institutional framework connecting broker-dealers, exchanges, custodians, transfer agents, issuers, and other regulated market participants to support the issuance, trading, settlement, and servicing of tokenized securities.

By combining regulated financial infrastructure with blockchain technology, Dinari is helping establish a compliant foundation for the next generation of capital markets.

"We believe tokenization represents one of the greatest opportunities to modernize capital markets in decades, but long-term adoption depends on preserving the trust, transparency, and investor protections that have made U.S. securities markets the global standard," said Gabriel Otte, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Dinari. "Since our founding, we've believed tokenization should enhance the existing financial system, not replace it. We're excited to join Blockchain Association and contribute our firsthand experience building regulated tokenized securities as policymakers develop the framework that will define the future of capital markets."
