# Tokenized Commodities ETFs: Unlocking Liquidity and Accessibility for Investors On-Chain

Calvin Campos

•

Feb 27, 2024

Dinari is pleased to announce 4 new Commodity ETFs dShares. These 1:1 backed dShares correspond directly to, and receive dividends from, the US-exchange listed assets.

Our goal at Dinari has always been to help build the infrastructure to level the financial playing field by bringing traditional assets on to the blockchain in a compliant, stable, and accessible way. We have already tokenized 30+ US blue chip equities, ETFs, REITs, and more on to the blockchain (currently Arbitrum One) so investors around the world can access these investment opportunities. We have continued to forge partnerships in both TradFi and DeFi to get our assets into more investors hands, release core and B2B API product updates, and add to our list dShares of tokenized assets.

Today, we’re pleased to announce the release of 4 new dShare assets for Commodity ETFs (Electronically Traded Funds). These 1:1 backed dShares correspond directly to, and receive dividends from, the US-exchange listed assets. Moreover, these ERC-20 tokens are redeemable at a 1:1 ratio at any time for the underlying asset or it’s US Dollar value in Stablecoins. Commodities such as Steel, Wheat, and Timber underpin our economy, and are a staple of many investor portfolios around the world. We’re pleased to bring access to Commodities ETFs to the blockchain, and are excited to bring even more sectors of assets on chain in the future on a regular basis.
