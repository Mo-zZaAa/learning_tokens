# New dShares: Johnson & Johnson, AstraZeneca, TJMaxx Companies, Procter & Gamble

Davis Ballard

•

Jul 1, 2024

We are pleased to announce 4 new dShares featuring Johnson & Johnson, AstraZeneca, TJMaxx Companies, Procter & Gamble dShares are now live for buying & selling.

We are pleased to introduce four new dShares, featuring global market leaders [Johnson & Johnson](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=JNJ.d), [AstraZeneca](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=AZN.d), [TJMaxx Companies](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=TJX.d) and [Procter & Gamble](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=PG.d).

### New dShares

* [**Procter & Gamble ($PG)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=PG.d) has recent launches in their Oral-B and Pampers lines, contributing to a 4% increase in net sales last quarter. The company is focusing on reducing packaging waste and increasing the use of renewable energy in their manufacturing processes. Procter & Gamble’s stock has moved up 8% over the past six months.
* [**TJMaxx Companies ($TJX)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=TJX.d) is expanding its retail footprint with new store openings and increasing its e-commerce capabilities, reporting a 5% increase in comparable store sales in the latest quarter. The company’s stock has seen a 12% rise year-to-date.
* [**Johnson & Johnson ($JNJ)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=JNJ.d) is leading advancements in healthcare with recent approvals for new drugs in oncology and immunology. The company reported a 6% increase in revenue, driven by strong performance in its pharmaceuticals division. Johnson & Johnson’s stock has climbed 10% year-to-date, reflecting investor confidence in its innovation pipeline.
* [**AstraZeneca ($AZN)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=AZN.d) continues to make strides in the biopharmaceutical industry, particularly with its COVID-19 vaccine and new treatments for oncology and respiratory diseases. The company saw a 7% growth in sales in the last quarter, with its stock rising 15% this year, bolstered by robust clinical trial results and expanded global market presence.
