## Compliance-first infrastructure you can build on.

Global regulators are accelerating work on real-world asset (RWA) tokenization — emphasizing AML, fit-for-purpose market structure, investor protection, and support for self-custody. Dinari was built with a compliance-first philosophy so our partners can launch tokenized U.S. stocks confidently while policy advances.
