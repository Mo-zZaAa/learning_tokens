# How RWAX Exchange Expanded Access to Real-World Assets With Tokenized U.S. Securities

Powered by Dinari's dShares™, RWAX Exchange broadened its marketplace by adding regulated U.S. securities, giving users access to one of the world's largest asset classes alongside commodities, stablecoins, and other real-world assets.

Distribution Partner

## Overview

RWAX Exchange is building a blockchain-native marketplace for real-world assets. Through a single platform, users can access a growing range of tokenized investment opportunities, from commodities and yield-bearing assets to tokenized U.S. securities, creating a more diversified onchain investing experience.

As interest in tokenized real-world assets continues to accelerate, RWAX Exchange identified an opportunity to expand beyond its existing product offering by bringing regulated U.S. securities onto the platform.

Rather than building brokerage infrastructure from the ground up, RWAX Exchange partnered with Dinari to integrate tokenized U.S. securities through a single API integration.

The result is a broader marketplace that allows users to discover and invest across multiple categories of real-world assets without leaving the RWAX Exchange ecosystem.

## Beyond Tokenization

The goal was never tokenization. It was what tokenization makes possible.

For decades, access to U.S. public markets has largely depended on geography, financial intermediaries, and fragmented distribution. Investors often needed separate brokerage accounts, cross-border banking relationships, and multiple financial platforms to participate in global capital markets.

Tokenization changes that distribution model.

Rather than creating entirely new financial products, tokenization enables regulated securities to be delivered through the digital financial platforms people already use. It unlocks broader global accessibility, fractional investing, faster settlement, and the ability to integrate traditional financial assets into modern onchain experiences.

The conversation is no longer about whether assets can be tokenized. It is about what tokenization unlocks.

## A Market Reaching an Inflection Point

RWAX Exchange's expansion comes as tokenized real-world assets enter a new phase of growth.

According to CoinGecko's *RWA Report 2026*, the tokenized real-world asset market more than tripled over a fifteen-month period, growing from **$5.4 billion** to **$19.3 billion** by the end of the first quarter of 2026. As the market matures, the focus is shifting from proving that tokenization works to expanding the breadth of assets available and improving how investors access them.

For exchanges, that means creating destinations where users can discover multiple asset classes through one seamless experience.

## The Solution

RWAX Exchange integrated Dinari's dShares™ to bring regulated U.S. securities directly into its marketplace.

Built on Dinari's custodial model of tokenization, each dShare™ is backed 1:1 by a corresponding U.S. security held through regulated custody. This approach preserves key investor protections, including cash dividends, corporate actions, redemption rights, and a protected claim to the backing security, while making regulated securities accessible through blockchain infrastructure.

Through a single integration, Dinari manages the underlying brokerage infrastructure, including custody, compliance, settlement, order routing, dividend processing, and corporate actions.

This enabled RWAX Exchange to expand its marketplace with regulated U.S. securities without taking on the complexity of building brokerage infrastructure internally.

## Expanding the Marketplace

Adding tokenized U.S. securities complements RWAX Exchange's broader vision of creating a destination for real-world assets onchain.

Early activity has centered around income-focused ETFs, including **YMAX**, **MSTY**, and **JAAA**, reflecting investor interest in products that combine exposure to traditional financial markets with income-generating strategies.

As additional categories of real-world assets continue moving onchain, bringing them together within a single marketplace gives investors more opportunities to diversify across sectors, strategies, and asset classes through one platform.

## Why It Matters

For RWAX Exchange, integrating regulated U.S. securities expands the breadth of investment opportunities available to its users while strengthening its position as a destination for real-world assets.

For Dinari, the partnership demonstrates how exchanges can introduce regulated U.S. securities through a single integration while relying on compliant infrastructure for custody, settlement, corporate actions, and lifecycle management.

Together, RWAX Exchange and Dinari demonstrate that the next generation of financial marketplaces will not be defined by individual asset classes. They will bring them together, giving investors seamless access to a broader range of regulated investment opportunities through one connected experience.
