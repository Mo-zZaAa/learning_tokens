# Save the Date for Dinari's First Community Call on October 12 at 8pm

Matthew Eisner

•

Oct 7, 2023

In the fast-paced world of blockchain and decentralized finance, staying ahead of the curve is paramount.

In the fast-paced world of blockchain and decentralized finance, staying ahead of the curve is paramount. At Dinari, we strive to keep our community informed and engaged with our progress. That's why we're excited to announce our very first Twitter Spaces event, hosted by our CTO, Jake Timothy, and Head of Growth, Matthew Eisner. Mark your calendars for Thursday, October 12th at 8pm Pacific Time, as we dive deep into the world of decentralized finance, discuss what we're working on, and release new features.

## Why Twitter Spaces?

You might be wondering why we've chosen Twitter Spaces as our platform for this event. The answer is simple: it allows for real-time, interactive discussions with our community. While our blog posts and social media updates provide valuable insights, Twitter Spaces takes engagement to a whole new level. It's an opportunity for you to ask questions, share your thoughts, and connect with our team in a dynamic and authentic way.

## What to Expect

During this live event, we'll cover a wide range of topics, including:

1. Dinari Tech Updates and Releases: Our CTO will shed light on the latest technological developments at Dinari, including recent updates and what's on the horizon.
2. Growing Dinari: Our Head of Growth as they provide valuable insights on how to navigate the cryptocurrency landscape, from investing strategies to market analysis.
3. Community Q&A: We want to hear from you! This event is your chance to ask questions and engage with our speakers directly.

## How to Save the Date and Join the Call

Participating in Dinari's Twitter Spaces event is easy. On Thursday, October 12th at 8pm Pacific Time, simply log in to Twitter and visit our profile [@DinariGlobal]. You'll see a notification when the event begins. Click to join, and you'll be part of the conversation.

## Another Chance to Recieve a dShare Gift Card

To make this event even more exciting, we're running a special giveaway. We'll be giving away a gift card to a random person who Tweets ahead of the event with the hashtag #Dinari and tags @DinariGlobal for a chance to win a dShare Gift Card for a random amount of NVDA.d dShares. We appreciate your support and want to thank you for being a part of our growing community. Please see below for terms and conditions.

## See you October 12th at 8pm Pacific Time!

At Dinari, we're committed to transparency, innovation, and community engagement. Our first Twitter Spaces event on Thursday, October 12th at 8pm Pacific Time is an opportunity for us to connect with you, answer your questions, and explore the fascinating world of blockchain and finance together. We look forward to having you join us for this exciting conversation. Together, we're shaping the future of finance, one block at a time.
