# Eldora Expands Access to Tokenized U.S. Equities Across Southeast Asia

By partnering with Dinari, Eldora launched regulated tokenized U.S. equities across Southeast Asia, giving investors access to more than 272 U.S. stocks and ETFs backed by real underlying shares.

## The Opportunity

Demand for U.S. equities continues to grow across Southeast Asia, yet millions of investors remain underserved by traditional financial infrastructure. Opening international brokerage accounts can involve geographic restrictions, lengthy onboarding, high minimums, and limited product availability.

While tokenized stocks have begun addressing accessibility, many products provide only synthetic price exposure rather than ownership of the underlying security.

Eldora wanted to offer something different: a simple way for investors across Southeast Asia to access real U.S. equities through tokenization with real ownership.

## The Solution

Eldora partnered with Dinari to power its U.S. equities offering using Dinari's custodial model for tokenized securities.

Through a single API integration, Eldora launched access to more than **272 U.S. stocks and ETFs**, each backed 1:1 by the corresponding underlying security held through regulated custody. High demand tickers including SPY, QQQ, META, AAPL, NVDA, TSLA, AMZN, MSTR, and SPCX are available to trade 24/7.

Unlike synthetic tokenized products, Dinari's model preserves the rights and protections associated with equity ownership, including dividends, corporate actions, redemption rights, and a protected claim to the backing security.

## The Results

Within the initial launch period, Eldora generated more than **$101,000 in trading volume**, demonstrating strong demand for regulated access to U.S. equities.

More than **90% of trading volume** was concentrated in five leading U.S. technology companies, underscoring investor demand for globally recognized equities through blockchain-native infrastructure.

Beyond trading activity, the partnership established a foundation for expanding regulated U.S. equity ownership across Southeast Asia. By integrating Dinari's infrastructure, Eldora can continue to broaden access to U.S. capital markets while providing investors with tokenized equities backed by real underlying shares.
