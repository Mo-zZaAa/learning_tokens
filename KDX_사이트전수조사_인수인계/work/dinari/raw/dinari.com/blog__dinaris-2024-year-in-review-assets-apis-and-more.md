# Dinari’s 2024 Year in Review: Assets, APIs, and More!

Rachel Ling

•

Jan 7, 2025

As 2024 has come to a close, we’re taking a moment to reflect on our biggest year yet at Dinari. From product launches to strategic partnerships, we’ve made significant progress toward bringing access to stocks, ETFs, and more to investors and businesses around the world.

As 2024 has come to a close, we’re taking a moment to reflect on our biggest year yet at Dinari. From product launches to strategic partnerships, we’ve made significant progress toward bringing access to stocks, ETFs, and more to investors and businesses around the world. Here’s a look back at some of our key milestones and accomplishments.

### **Nearly 100 dShares Now Available**

Last year, we reached a significant milestone—offering nearly 100 dShares for stocks, ETFs, and more, on chain and via API. By expanding our tokenized asset lineup, we continue to empower investors with transparent, secure, and globally accessible financial opportunities. You can access the decentralized application for minting and burning dShares here: <https://sbt.dinari.com/>

### **USD+ Launch Powered by Chainlink CCIP**

In 2024, we introduced USD+, a yield-bearing stablecoin backed 1:1 by USFR (US Treasury Bills) and [powered by Chainlink’s Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP)](https://x.com/DinariGlobal/status/1778810873374503418). This technology enables seamless cross-chain transfers, making USD+ a reliable solution for a more connected financial ecosystem. You can access the decentralized application for minting and burning USD+ here: <https://usd.dinari.com/>

### **Expanding to New Blockchains**

We launched dShares on several new blockchain networks, including Ethereum, Base, Blast, and Kinto, complementing our presence on Arbitrum One. This expansion makes Dinari’s solutions more versatile and accessible than ever, empowering both developers and investors to operate on the chains that best suit their needs.

### **Partner API and Managed Accounts Solution**

In 2024, we also saw the launch of our Partner API and Managed Accounts solution. These tools enable FinTechs and financial businesses to integrate stocks, ETFs, and other financial products directly into their offerings. By simplifying integration, we’re helping partners deliver more robust solutions to their customers. You can sign up for a partner account and learn more here: <https://partners.dinari.com/>

### **Introducing the Dinari Partner Portal**

We unveiled the [Dinari Partner Portal](https://partners.dinari.com/), a comprehensive hub where developers can sign up for our test API and businesses can access our full trading API after completing KYB (Know Your Business) verification. This streamlined platform makes it easier than ever to collaborate with Dinari.

### **Dinari Assets Live in Partner Products**

Our solutions are now powering a growing number of partner platforms, including Fasset, Cadana, Offramp, LaProp, Xade Finance, Berry, and more. This momentum reflects the strength of our technology and the trust our partners place in us, with even more integrations on the way.

### **Supporting Innovation Through Incentives**

In 2024, we launched the [Business Start](https://dinari.com/business/business-start) and [FinTech Developer Incentive](https://dinari.com/business/developer-incentives) Programs, designed to support emerging businesses and developers by providing resources, incentives, and expertise. These initiatives align with our mission to lower barriers to entry and foster innovation.

### **What’s Next?**

As we step into 2025, we’re excited to continue building on last year’s success. With new partnerships, expanded offerings, and more announcements on the horizon, there’s so much to look forward to.

If you’re a FinTech developer, financial business, or investor looking to explore what Dinari can do for you, we invite you to visit our [Partner Portal](https://partners.dinari.com/), join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/dinari), or email us to learn more.

Together, we’re shaping the future of finance—one innovation at a time.

Here’s to an exciting 2025!
