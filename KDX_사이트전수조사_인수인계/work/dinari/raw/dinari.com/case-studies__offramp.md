## Overview

Managing money across borders has traditionally meant managing multiple financial platforms.

Users receive payments in one place, hold digital assets in another, spend through a separate provider, and invest through a traditional brokerage.

Offramp is changing that.

Its global financial account allows users to receive money through ACH, SEPA, and wire transfers, hold digital dollars, spend globally through a Visa card and local payment rails, and maintain self-custody of their assets. Through its partnership with Dinari, Offramp expanded that experience by adding regulated U.S. equities and ETFs powered by dShares™, allowing users to invest alongside the same financial tools they already use every day.

## The Opportunity

As work, commerce, and finance become increasingly global, users expect financial products to move with them.

Freelancers, remote workers, entrepreneurs, and digital asset users often manage income, savings, and spending across multiple currencies and jurisdictions. While digital dollars have made moving money faster and more accessible, investing has largely remained disconnected from the rest of that financial experience.

Opening a brokerage account, transferring funds, and navigating traditional financial infrastructure continues to create unnecessary friction.

Offramp saw an opportunity to make investing another native capability within its global account, allowing users to put their digital dollars to work without leaving the platform they already trust.

## The Solution

Offramp integrated Dinari's dShares™ to offer regulated U.S. equities and ETFs directly within its global financial account.

Built on Dinari's custodial model of tokenization, each dShare™ maintains a direct connection to the corresponding underlying U.S. security held through regulated custody. Unlike products that provide only synthetic price exposure, dShares™ are backed 1:1 and are designed to preserve the rights and protections associated with traditional securities, including cash dividends, corporate actions, redemption rights, and a protected claim to the backing security.

Users can seamlessly invest using balances already held within Offramp, including stable digital dollars, making investing feel like a natural extension of the platform rather than a separate financial workflow.

Behind the scenes, Dinari provides the regulated infrastructure required to issue and service each dShare™, allowing Offramp to focus on delivering a unified customer experience while giving users access to real U.S. public markets.

## The Impact

Since launch, Offramp users have traded more than $293,000 in regulated U.S. equities and ETFs across 107 unique securities, demonstrating demand for investing as part of a broader financial experience.

Trading activity spans 19 countries, with Argentina, Kazakhstan, and Brazil emerging as the platform's largest markets. The geographic diversity reflects demand for borderless access to U.S. public markets through modern financial infrastructure.

Investor behavior also pointed toward long-term portfolio construction. QQQ, SPY, and GLD ranked among the platform's most actively traded securities, suggesting users are using Offramp not only to move and store value, but also to build diversified investment portfolios.

## Why It Matters

Financial products are becoming less siloed.

Rather than managing separate applications for payments, savings, digital assets, and investing, users increasingly expect a single platform to support every stage of their financial lives.

Offramp reflects that evolution by bringing these experiences together within one global financial account.

By integrating Dinari's dShares™, investing becomes another native capability alongside receiving payments, holding digital dollars, spending globally, and self-custody. Rather than requiring users to leave the platform to access U.S. public markets, Offramp extends those markets directly into the financial experience they already use every day.

For Dinari, the partnership demonstrates how regulated U.S. equities can become a seamless component of modern financial products, enabling partners to deliver more complete financial experiences through a single integration.

## Looking Ahead

The future of financial services isn't about building more standalone products.

It's about creating connected financial experiences that remove friction for users living and working in a global economy.

As global accounts become the primary interface for managing money, investing will increasingly sit alongside payments, digital dollars, and everyday spending rather than behind separate brokerage accounts.

Through its partnership with Dinari, Offramp is helping define that future by making regulated U.S. public markets another native capability within a borderless financial account, giving users a simpler way to build wealth wherever they are in the world.
