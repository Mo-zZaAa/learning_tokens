### **Key Insights from TOKEN2049 Side Events and Networking**

TOKEN2049 brought together thought leaders, innovators, and entrepreneurs from across the globe, creating a hub for networking, collaboration, and insight-sharing on the future of blockchain. We had the opportunity to meet several partners in person and forge new connections. From neobanks to blockchains, discussions spawned new avenues for growth, partnership, and collaboration that we’re excited to share with you soon.

‍

### **Highlights of the Week:**

*Panel session with Rachel (left) representing Dinari*

‍

* Panel Session: Our team participated in a panel discussion at the Chain Innovation Malaysia Event, where the focus was on building a more collaborative Web3 ecosystem in Asia.
* DeFi Conversations: We engaged with various DeFi platforms, exploring potential partnership opportunities for Dinari as we continue expanding the reach of dShares and tokenized assets.
* Community Engagement: Our community members who connected with us in person received exclusive dShares gift cards, featuring portions of Nvidia shares (NVDA.d), offering a glimpse into the future of tokenized finance.

‍
