# April 2024 Monthly Recap

Davis Ballard

•

May 1, 2024

Dinari's April highlights demonstrate a focus on stability and expansion: Chainlink integration, new partners, API upgrades, and an enhanced USD+ stablecoin. Learn more

April brought major growth for Dinari, expanding our B2B reach with asset integrations and unlocking cross-chain power. Learn more about these exciting developments in this recap.

**April Community Call Twitter Spaces**

[Dinari](https://soundcloud.com/dinariglobal "Dinari") · [Dinari Community Call - April 2024](https://soundcloud.com/dinariglobal/dinari-community-call-april-2024 "Dinari Community Call - April 2024")

‍

**USD+ and Chainlink CCIP Integration**

We have enhanced our cross-chain capabilities by integrating Chainlink CCIP, which now supports our [USD+](https://usd.dinari.com/) stablecoin in performing secure transactions across Arbitrum One and Ethereum mainnets.

* [Learn more in our recent blog post](https://dinari.com/blog/chainlink-ccip-arbitrum-ethereum-usdplus)

‍

**Expanded Access Through Partners**

Two new B2B partners, [Xade](https://xade.finance/) and [LaProp](https://www.laprop.co/) have launched integrations with Dinari assets.

* REITs on LaProp: [Annaly Capital ($NLY.dw)](https://app.laprop.co/reit/QJkQQm6WevQKturtoMBk), [Prologis Inc ($PLD.dw)](https://app.laprop.co/reit/pCWTxbGIDy2AcjIUFhnJ)

‍

**B2B API Developments:**

Our B2B API now includes three new features: a managed account solution, price feeds, and asset information.

* [API Documentation](https://docs.dinari.com/api/enterprise-guide)

‍

**Ambassador Program Applications**

We're receiving strong interest in our Ambassador Program, submissions close soon!  Become an Ambassador to help us grow, share our vision, earn rewards, and gain exclusive access.

* [Apply to Ambassador Program](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfS2Wxgi4pOaJEX13x8ZeahxzGPB9x3rNL6cujt1PGM8X5a_g/viewform)

‍

**Upcoming**

* We're continuously enhancing [USD+](https://usd.dinari.com/)'s utility, including the upcoming introduction of new dividend features
* Factory contract upgrades to version 0.4.0 will optimize how Dinari assets are managed and tracked across platforms.
* [SolidViolet](https://twitter.com/SolidViolet), an exciting development in compliant real-world assets and a new expansion to the Dinari ecosystem, will be announced soon. Stay tuned to our social channels for updates.

‍

**Thank you for your ongoing support, and stay tuned for more exciting updates in the near future!**
