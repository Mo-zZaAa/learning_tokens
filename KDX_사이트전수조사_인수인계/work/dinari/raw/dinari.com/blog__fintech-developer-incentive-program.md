# Introducing the Dinari FinTech Developer Incentive Program

Jake TImothy

•

Sep 10, 2024

Are you a developer at a FinTech company? See if Dinari’s FinTech Developer Incentive Program is right for you!

Are you a developer at a non-US FinTech company looking to build with blockchain technology and expand your offering with US stocks, ETFs, and more? [Dinari’s FinTech Developer Incentive Program](https://dinari.com/business/developer-incentives) is designed for you, offering rewards for using our Testnet and Mainnet APIs. Here's everything you need to know to get started and take advantage of this exciting opportunity.

‍

### **What You Can Earn**

Developers working for qualifying non-US FinTech companies and applications that sign up through our partner portal can earn the following rewards:

**Testnet Rewards:**

* The first 4 developers to create a Testnet API call will receive $25 of Coinbase ($COIN.d) dShares each

**Mainnet Rewards:**

* The first developer to create a Mainnet API call will receive $175 of COIN.d dShares

**Company Rewards:**

* Companies that qualify for the FinTech Developer Incentive Program will be awarded $10,000 in credits for free trades, valid for 1 year

### **Requirements**

To qualify for these rewards, developers and companies must meet the following conditions:

* **Eligibility:** Developers and their companies must be based outside the US and in an eligible country.
* **KYC and KYB:** Developers must complete KYC through sbt.dinari.com to claim rewards. Businesses need to complete KYB and agree to Dinari API Terms and Conditions to access the Mainnet API.
* **Timeline:** The Mainnet API call must be made within 90 days of the Testnet API call.
* **Qualifying Companies:** Developers must work at a FinTech or Finance company. To submit your company for consideration, simply fill out [this Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScGyW9470HuFmQ2YQu3ZyIsh8Cux3we8ya1K1PGFpJ7Xa04-g/viewform).
* **Company Focus:** Preference will be given to companies focused on FinTech, incorporated with a live product, and clear usage plans for the API. Dinari will review applications and approve them at its discretion.

### **Why Join?**

By joining this program, you can kickstart your development efforts with tokenized assets, enabling you to integrate 1:1 backed tokenized US stocks into your products. Plus, you’ll benefit from free trades and generous rewards that make this an opportunity to accelerate your business while exploring the power of blockchain technology.

‍

### **How to Get Started**

1. **Sign Up for Our Partners Portal:** Visit [partners.dinari.com](https://partners.dinari.com/) to create an account and kick off your journey.
2. **Submit a Testnet API Call:** Make a testnet API call and fill out [this Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeLfqV6ObMbHcGjuuHzjkNU2bzf19SjjDndyJ6oO6RxYq-bjA/viewform) to receive your $25 COIN.d reward.
3. **Go Mainnet for Bigger Rewards:** If your company completes the Mainnet API call within 90 days, you'll be eligible for $175 in COIN.d dShares and $10,000 in free trading credits for your company.

Don’t miss out on the chance to earn real rewards and explore cutting-edge blockchain technology with Dinari.
