# October Community Call Recap ft. Mark Tang from Hydra X

Rachel Ling

•

Oct 30, 2024

We had an engaging October Community Call on X Spaces. Dive into all the updates and insights covered in the call.

We had an engaging Community Call on X Spaces yesterday, but due to a technical issue, the session recording isn’t available. No worries, though! We’re here to share all the updates and insights covered in the call.

### **🛠️ Product Updates**

The call kicked off with Jake diving deep into Dinari's product vision and recent developments.

* **Dinari Overview:** For new listeners, we reintroduced Dinari’s mission to bridge traditional finance and DeFi through tokenized assets like stocks and ETFs
* **Business Start Program:** Our recently launched program empowers businesses, fintechs, and startups to integrate tokenized assets seamlessly into their platforms or apps
* **New dShares on Kinto:** $SPY.d and $TSLA.d are now available on Kinto
* **SolidViolet:** We’ve revamped our upcoming plans for SolidViolet, our sister company, and we can’t wait to share more in the coming months!

### **🌟 Dinari as a Brand**

Matt highlighted Dinari’s recent milestones as we continue establishing a strong brand presence in the RWA industry.

* **B2B Partnerships:** We continue to collaborate with businesses and partners, driving tokenization adoption within B2B channels
* **Event Highlights:**
  + **Avalanche Summit:** Our co-founder and CTO, Jake, attended the Avalanche Summit in Buenos Aires and spoke on a panel focused on asset tokenization.
  + **Real World Asset Summit:** Our CEO, Gabe, represented Dinari in New York, joining industry leaders from Plume Network, m0foundation, and AvaLabs on a panel focused on improving the supply and demand for RWAs.
* **Transaction Volume:** We’ve surpassed 3,000 transactions on our platform!
* **Total Value Locked:** Our TVL now stands at $11.5 million

### **🔜 What’s Coming Next?**

We have several exciting initiatives coming up in the next few weeks, including new dShare asset offerings and the launch of our Community Referral Program.

### **🤝 Guest Speaker: Mark Tang from Hydra X**

In the second half of the call, we welcomed Mark Tang (VP, Client Solutions) from [Hydra X](https://www.hydrax.io/). Mark shared valuable insights on the evolving landscape of tokenization, and how Hydra X is playing its part in getting institutions and neobanks in the Southeast Asia region into the blockchain space in a compliant manner. It was great to have Mark on board, and we look forward to future collaborations with Hydra X!

‍

Thank you to everyone who joined us, and a special thanks to Mark for sharing his insights. Keep an eye on our social channels for all future updates!
