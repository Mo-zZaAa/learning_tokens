# USD+ Upgrade: Automatic Yield & dShares Stablecoin

Davis Ballard

•

May 6, 2024

Read about the upgraded USD+ that now effortlessly generates yield and serves as the official coin for dShares transactions.

We're thrilled to announce an exciting upgrade to [USD+](https://usd.dinari.com/), our yield-bearing stablecoin. These updates transform USD+ into a powerful tool within the dShares ecosystem.

‍

### **Automatic Staking & Yield Generation**

Say goodbye to manual staking, now your [USD+](https://usd.dinari.com/) balance automatically increases and accrues yield (5.36% APY as of 5/5/24).

‍

### **USD+ as the Heart of dShares**

We're elevating [USD+](https://usd.dinari.com/) to become the default stablecoin across the dShares platform. This means seamless buying and selling experiences powered by a stablecoin that automatically earns yield. With this change, you'll be able to directly **purchase dShares using USD+**

‍

### **What is USD+**

*Dollar-Backed Stability with USD+*

[USD+](https://usd.dinari.com/) is Dinari’s high-yield, dollar-backed stablecoin designed for investment stability and maximum confidence. Each USD+ is 100% backed by cash and short-term US Treasuries, offering an unparalleled guarantee that one USD+ maintains a 1:1 redemption rate with USD.

Our transparency and liquidity is further reinforced by regular reserve audits. Additionally, USD+ automatically yields a 5.36% APY\*

‍

**Key Benefits of USD+**

* **Optimal yields:** Competitive APY for investors optimizing returns while minimizing risk.
* **Flexibility:** Automatic staking for rewards, no minimum commitment needed.
* **Real-world asset backing:** Peace of mind knowing every token is backed by tangible assets.
* **Guaranteed redemption:** $1 USD per USD+, paid in USDC (redemption time 0-3 days).
* **Seamless user experience:** Easy purchase, redemption, and portfolio management via a user-friendly interface.
* **Business Integration:** APIs, SDK, and smart contracts enable expanded service offerings for businesses
