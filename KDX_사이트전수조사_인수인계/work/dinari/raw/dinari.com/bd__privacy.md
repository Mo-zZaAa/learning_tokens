DINARI SECURITIES, LLC.

PRIVACY POLICY

At  Dinari  Securities,  LLC  we  are  serious  about  protecting  your  privacy.  You  don’t
have to take our word for it though, you can read below to understand how we treat
your Personal Data. When you use our brokerage services pursuant to our Terms of
Service  and  Customer  Agreement,  we  require  that  you  acknowledge  and  agree  to
our  Privacy  Policy,  that  you  understand and consent to our Privacy Policy and that
we will collect, use, and share information as we outline below.

Remember  that  your use of our Website and brokerage services are subject to our
Terms  of  Service  and  Customer  Agreement,  which  incorporates  our Privacy Policy,
among  other  policies.  Capitalized  terms  in  this  Privacy  Policy  have  the  deﬁnitions
given to them in the Terms of Service and Customer Agreement, unless ﬁrst deﬁned
in this Policy.

Why We Have A Privacy Policy (And What It Covers)

Our  Privacy  Policy  is  here  to  help  you  understand  our  collection  and  use  of
information,  and  to  outline  your  rights.  Our  Privacy  Policy  covers  how  we  treat
Personal  Data  that  we  gather  when  you  access  or  use  our  brokerage  services.
“Personal  Data”  is  any  information  relating  to an identiﬁed or identiﬁable individual
and  is  protected  similarly  as  Personal  Data  or  personally  identiﬁable  information
under  applicable data protection laws. If your Personal Data is lawfully given to any
company that we don’t own or control, or to people we don’t directly oversee, then
the  privacy  policy  of that company is applicable, and not this Privacy Policy. In our
Privacy  Policy,  we  will  go  over  a  few  important areas: How we obtain your Personal
Data; The types of Personal Data we collect; How we Use your Personal Data; Sharing
your Personal Data; and Your rights with respect to your Personal Data. If any part of
this  Privacy  Policy  is  unclear  or  if  you  believe  that  your  Personal  Data  is  being
misused in violation of this Privacy Policy, please contact us immediately, so we can
look  into  it  and  try  our  best  to  remedy  the  situation  to  your  satisfaction.  Please
understand  that  our  brokerage  services  utilize  blockchain  smart  contract
technology for redundant recordkeeping, and as such, information on the blockchain
is  not  often  private;  however,  we  take  reasonable  steps  to  anonymize  this  public

Here Is How We Gather Personal Data

We collect Personal Data about you from a few different sources. For our brokerage
services, the main way we obtain your Personal Data is directly from you. As a user
of  our  brokerage  services,  you  will be required to provide Personal Data directly to
us, and while you use our brokerage services, we may also obtain additional Personal
Data as necessary to provide the brokerage services (including any future services)
and  to  be  compliant  with  applicable  laws  and  regulations.  From  time  to  time,
companies that we don’t own or control, or people we don’t directly oversee (“Third
Parties”), may provide us with Personal Data about you. As an example, we may use
analytics  service  providers  to  help  us  understand  how  you  use  our  brokerage
services, or third parties may help us provide you with ancillary services that we do
not  directly  provide.  We  automatically  collect  certain  Personal  Data  when you use
our brokerage services, you will be shown a cookie disclosure that will provide detail
on  what  cookies/pixels/tags  we  use  and  for  what  purposes.  Our  Website  may use
cookies  or  other  similar  methods  such  as  pixel  tags,  web  beacons, clear GIFs, and
JavaScript  (collectively,  “Cookies”)  to  help  our  Website  recognize  you  (your  web
browser,  phone,  etc).  Additionally,  these  Cookies  may  give  us  information  on  how
and  when  you  visit  the  Website  and  use  our  brokerage  services.  They  help  us  to
analyze  trends,  so  we  can  learn  more  and  improve  our  brokerage  services.  This
information  can  be  supplemented  with  other  information  received  from  third
parties. At this time, when you visit the Website and use the brokerage services, we
are unable to offer “Do Not Track” requests – if you ask us to not track, we may not
be  able  to  fulﬁll  the  brokerage  services for you. You can decide whether to accept
Cookies  through  settings  on  your  browser.  You  can also delete Cookies already on
your device. If you do this, some of your preferences may have to be adjusted when
you  visit  the  Website  and  use  our  brokerage  services  (it  is  possible  that  some  of
functions may not work until you allow Cookies).

●  Our clearing broker, Alpaca Securities, LLC and its Afﬁliates.

demonstration;

brokerage services;

Data Security and Retention

We employ a number of technical, organizational, and physical safeguards designed
to  protect  the  Personal  Data  we  collect.  However,  security  risk  is  inherent  in  all
internet and information technologies, and we cannot guarantee the security of your
personal information.

Personal Data Use/Storage

Your  Personal  Data  may  be  used,  hosted,  or  otherwise  processed  outside  of  your
home  jurisdiction  in  a  jurisdiction(s)  that  may  not  provide  equivalent  levels  of
protection  for  personal  information,  including  the  United States. You must consent
to this use and storage in order to use our brokerage services.
