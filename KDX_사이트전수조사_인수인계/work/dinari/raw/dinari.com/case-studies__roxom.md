## Overview

Bitcoin has become one of the world's largest financial assets, yet accessing traditional financial markets has historically required investors to step outside the Bitcoin ecosystem.

Buying U.S. public securities typically means converting bitcoin into fiat currency, opening a brokerage account, and managing investments across multiple financial platforms.

By partnering with Dinari, Roxom expanded its platform with regulated U.S. securities, enabling investors to buy and sell public equities directly in BTC. Investors can buy, sell, and track U.S. securities while measuring portfolio performance in bitcoin, creating a more seamless investment experience that brings U.S. public markets into the Bitcoin economy.

## The Opportunity

Headquartered in Latin America, Roxom is building in one of the world's most active crypto markets. According to Chainalysis, Latin America processed nearly **$1.5 trillion** in cryptocurrency transaction volume between July 2022 and June 2025, driven by growing adoption across countries including Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, Colombia, and Venezuela. Across the region, digital assets have become an increasingly important part of how people save, transfer value, and build wealth.

As crypto adoption has grown, so has demand for broader investment opportunities. For many Bitcoin holders, accessing U.S. public markets still means converting into fiat, opening a brokerage account, and moving between multiple financial platforms. Roxom saw an opportunity to simplify that experience by giving investors access to regulated U.S. equities directly in BTC.

## The Solution

Roxom integrated Dinari's dShares™ to bring regulated U.S. equities and ETFs directly into its BTC-denominated marketplace.

Built on Dinari's custodial model of tokenization, each dShare™ maintains a direct connection to the corresponding underlying U.S. security held through regulated custody. Unlike products that provide only synthetic price exposure, dShares™ are backed 1:1 and are designed to preserve the rights and protections associated with traditional securities, including cash dividends, corporate actions, redemption rights, and a protected claim to the backing security.

For Roxom, this meant offering investors across Latin America more than exposure to stock prices. It meant providing access to **real U.S. public equities** through a regulated tokenization model while allowing investors to transact directly in bitcoin.

Dinari provides the regulated issuance and servicing infrastructure behind each dShare™, while Roxom delivers a Bitcoin-first investing experience built for one of the world's most active crypto regions. Together, the partnership expands access to U.S. public markets without requiring investors to compromise on the protections associated with traditional securities.

## The Impact

Since launch, investors have traded more than $7.9 million in regulated U.S. securities through Roxom, executing 2,497 orders across 52 unique U.S. equities.

Trading activity has been led by SATA, STRC, and NVIDIA. The prominence of SATA and STRC, companies associated with the growing Bitcoin treasury movement, alongside one of the world's leading technology companies, suggests investors are using Roxom both to deepen their exposure to the Bitcoin ecosystem and diversify into the broader U.S. public markets.

The partnership demonstrates growing demand for investment experiences that allow users to access regulated U.S. equities while continuing to invest directly in bitcoin.

## Why It Matters

For years, investors outside the United States have often faced tradeoffs when accessing U.S. public markets. They could navigate cross-border brokerage infrastructure or rely on products that offered only economic exposure to an asset.

Partnerships like Roxom and Dinari demonstrate another path.

By combining a Bitcoin-native marketplace with Dinari's custodial model of tokenization, investors can access real U.S. public equities through the digital asset infrastructure they already use, while preserving the protections associated with regulated securities.

For Dinari, it's another example of how dShares™ enable trusted financial platforms to expand access to U.S. markets for new investor communities around the world. For Roxom, it strengthens its mission of giving Bitcoin holders more ways to participate in global financial markets without leaving the Bitcoin ecosystem.

## Looking Ahead

As digital assets become a larger part of how people save and invest, investors increasingly expect access to a broader range of financial products through the platforms they already use.

For Bitcoin holders across Latin America and beyond, that means expanding beyond digital assets into diversified portfolios that include regulated U.S. public equities.

By combining Roxom's Bitcoin-denominated marketplace with Dinari's regulated tokenized securities infrastructure, the partnership is helping make that transition simpler, bringing real U.S. public markets closer to a new generation of global investors.
