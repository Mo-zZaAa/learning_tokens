# How Fasset Expanded Global Investing With Tokenized U.S. Equities

Powered by Dinari's dShares™, Fasset broadened its investment platform by bringing regulated U.S. equities and ETFs to customers, expanding access to global financial markets through a single application.

## Overview

Fasset is a global digital asset platform focused on expanding financial access across emerging markets. Through a single application, customers can buy and sell digital assets, hold digital dollars, and access modern financial products designed to make investing more accessible.

As customer demand evolved, Fasset identified an opportunity to expand beyond digital assets by giving customers access to regulated U.S. public markets.

Rather than building brokerage infrastructure from the ground up, Fasset partnered with Dinari to integrate tokenized U.S. equities and ETFs directly into its existing investment experience.

The result is a broader investment platform that enables customers to diversify across digital assets and traditional financial markets without leaving the Fasset ecosystem.

## The Opportunity

Investors increasingly want access to multiple asset classes through a single financial platform.

While interest in U.S. public markets continues to grow globally, accessing those markets often requires opening separate brokerage accounts, transferring funds between institutions, and navigating complex onboarding processes.

Fasset wanted to eliminate that friction by making investing in regulated U.S. securities as seamless as investing in digital assets.

## The Solution

Fasset integrated Dinari's dShares™ to offer tokenized U.S. equities and ETFs directly within its existing application.

Built on Dinari's custodial model of tokenization, each dShare™ is backed 1:1 by a corresponding U.S. security held through regulated custody. This approach preserves key investor protections, including cash dividends, corporate actions, redemption rights, and a protected claim to the backing security, while making U.S. securities accessible through blockchain infrastructure.

Through a single API integration, Dinari manages the underlying brokerage infrastructure, including custody, compliance, settlement, order routing, corporate actions, and dividend processing.

This enabled Fasset to expand its investment offering without building brokerage capabilities internally.

## The Impact

Since launch, Fasset customers have generated more than **$1.44 million** in trading volume across **18,959 orders**, with activity spanning **57 unique U.S. equities and ETFs**.

Trading activity reflects a customer base seeking both growth opportunities and portfolio diversification.

Silver ETF **SIVR** emerged as the platform's most traded asset, representing more than **38% of total trading volume**, followed by NVIDIA, the iShares Core S&P Total U.S. Stock Market ETF (ITOT), and the iShares U.S. Aerospace & Defense ETF (ITA).

The mix of equities and ETFs highlights how customers are using the platform not only to invest in individual companies, but also to gain diversified exposure across sectors, commodities, and the broader U.S. market.

By bringing regulated U.S. securities alongside digital assets, Fasset expanded the ways customers can build diversified portfolios while strengthening its position as a comprehensive investment platform.

## Why It Matters

For Fasset, adding tokenized U.S. equities and ETFs broadened its investment offering beyond digital assets, giving customers greater opportunities to diversify and build long-term wealth.

For Dinari, the partnership demonstrates how digital asset platforms can expand into regulated U.S. securities through a single integration. Dinari manages the underlying brokerage infrastructure, including custody, compliance, settlement, and lifecycle management, allowing partners to launch new investment products without building brokerage infrastructure from scratch.

Together, Fasset and Dinari demonstrate how tokenization can expand access to traditional financial markets while preserving the protections associated with regulated securities.

## Looking Ahead

Investor expectations continue to evolve. Increasingly, customers want a single platform where they can access multiple asset classes without sacrificing simplicity or regulatory protections.

By partnering with Dinari, Fasset has demonstrated how digital asset platforms can bridge traditional and onchain finance, giving customers seamless access to U.S. public markets while continuing to expand financial access around the world.
