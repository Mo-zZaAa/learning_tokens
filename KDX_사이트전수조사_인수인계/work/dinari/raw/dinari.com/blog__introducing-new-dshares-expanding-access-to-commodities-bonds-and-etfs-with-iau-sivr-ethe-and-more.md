# Introducing New dShares: Expanding Access to Commodities, Bonds, and ETFs with IAU, SIVR, ETHE, and More

Jake Timothy

•

Oct 7, 2024

We are excited to announce the launch of several new dShares that cover a diverse range of sectors, including commodities, fixed income, and digital assets.

At Dinari, we’re always focused on broadening the range of investment opportunities for our users. Today, we’re excited to announce the launch of several new dShares that cover a diverse range of sectors, including commodities, fixed income, and digital assets. These new dShares will allow users to invest in physical gold, silver, corporate bonds, municipal bonds, Ethereum, and more—directly on the blockchain.

### New dShares

1. [**iShares Gold Trust ($IAU)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/IAU.d?orderType=0)

IAU represents the iShares Gold Trust, giving investors exposure to gold through blockchain technology. This dShare tracks the price of physical gold, providing a secure and simple way for investors to diversify their portfolio with one of the most trusted stores of value.

2. [**abrdn Physical Silver Shares ETF ($SIVR)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/SIVR.d?orderType=0)

SIVR provides exposure to physical silver, a key asset for investors looking to diversify into precious metals. This dShare tracks the value of silver, offering easy access to a tangible commodity that has historically been a hedge against market volatility.

3. [**Grayscale Ethereum Trust ($ETHE)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/ETHE.d?orderType=0)

ETHE gives investors the opportunity to own Ethereum through the Grayscale Ethereum Trust. Ethereum has been at the forefront of smart contract platforms and decentralized applications, making it a popular choice for those who believe in the long-term growth of blockchain technology.

4. [**iPath Series B S&P 500 VIX Short-Term Futures ETN ($VXX)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/VXX.d?orderType=0)

For those looking to manage risk and volatility, VXX offers exposure to S&P 500 volatility, giving users a tool to hedge against stock market fluctuations. This dShare tracks short-term VIX futures, allowing investors to capitalize on market volatility.

5.[**iShares Broad USD High Yield Corporate Bond ETF ($USHY)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/USHY.d?orderType=0)

USHY represents high-yield U.S. corporate bonds, providing investors with exposure to high-interest debt securities. This dShare is perfect for those seeking higher returns and diversification in the bond market.

6. [**SPDR Nuveen Bloomberg High Yield Municipal Bond ETF ($HYMB)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/HYMB.d?orderType=0)

HYMB focuses on high-yield municipal bonds, which offer tax advantages to investors. These bonds support infrastructure projects and public services, making them a socially conscious choice while providing stable returns.

These new additions open up a diverse range of opportunities for our users. Whether you’re looking to hedge against inflation with precious metals, capitalize on cryptocurrency growth, or seek stable income through bonds, our new dShares offer an accessible, blockchain-powered way to expand your investment strategy.
