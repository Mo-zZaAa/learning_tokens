# COIN.dw & USDC Lending/Borrowing Pool Available on Ajna Protocol

Brandon Ooi

•

Feb 1, 2024

Dinari COIN.dw is now live on Ajna Protocol's lending and borrowing pools. Read more on our blog about Ajna, our pool, and what Dinari has planned for DeFi in the near future!

We are pleased to announce that COIN.dw and USDC are now available on Ajna Protocol. For pool details, [visit this link](https://ajnafi.com/arbitrum/pools/0xe437ab61cf812df06e2702fd5eecdfa1dfbbe0bb).

‍

### Wrapping COIN.d for COIN.dw

To participate in the pools, **users must to wrap their COIN.d into COIN.dw**. This process, made simple and accessible on the Dinari platform, can be completed by visiting [sbt.dinari.com/defi](https://sbt.dinari.com/defi?actionType=0&selectedStockTokenId=14). This conversion wraps your COIN.d as COIN.dw for use on Ajna Protocol. Wrapped dShares can be unwrapped at any time.

‍

### Using Ajna Protocol

If you are interested in lending and borrowing using Ajna, and want to learn more, we'd suggest [heading to their FAQ page to learn more](https://faqs.ajna.finance/getting-started/welcome), or joining their community. Lending and borrowing in DeFi present substaintial risks, including of total loss. Please do not invest more than you are willing to lose.
