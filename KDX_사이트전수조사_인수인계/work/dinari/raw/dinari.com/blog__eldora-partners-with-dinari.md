# Eldora Partners with Dinari to Expand Access to Tokenized U.S. Equities Across Southeast Asia

Dinari

•

Jul 1, 2026

As interest in U.S. equities continues to grow around the world, millions of investors still face barriers to accessing the world's largest capital markets. Geographic restrictions, traditional brokerage requirements, and fragmented financial infrastructure have left many investors underserved.

Today, we're excited to announce our partnership with **Eldora**, a digital investing platform focused on expanding access to global financial markets across Southeast Asia.

Through Dinari's dShares™, Eldora is bringing more than 272 U.S. stocks and ETFs to investors across Southeast Asia and 85+ supported countries worldwide, giving users access to tokenized U.S. equities backed by real underlying shares.

## Expanding Global Access to U.S. Markets

Southeast Asia is home to one of the fastest-growing digital investor populations in the world. At the same time, global demand for U.S. equities continues to accelerate. Foreign investors held nearly [$20 trillion in U.S. equities as of mid-2025](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0482), highlighting the continued appeal of U.S. capital markets worldwide.

By integrating Dinari's API, Eldora can offer investors access to leading U.S. companies through a seamless investing experience without sacrificing the protections associated with regulated securities.

The result is a simpler path to participating in U.S. capital markets from anywhere in the region.

## Built on Real Ownership

Not all tokenized equities are structured the same.

Many products available today provide synthetic exposure designed to replicate a stock's price. While these products may follow market performance, they do not represent ownership of the underlying security.

Dinari pioneered the custodial model for tokenized equities in 2021.

Every dShare™ is backed by the corresponding underlying security held through regulated custody. This preserves the rights and protections associated with equity ownership, including dividends, corporate actions, redemption rights, and a protected claim to the backing security.

For platforms like Eldora, this means they can offer investors U.S. equity ownership through infrastructure purpose-built for global distribution.

## Building the Next Generation of Investing Platforms

As demand for regulated tokenized securities continues to grow, financial platforms are looking for new ways to deliver global market access without sacrificing investor protections.

By combining Eldora's regional reach with Dinari's custodial infrastructure, the partnership expands access to real U.S. equity ownership for investors across Southeast Asia while establishing a foundation for continued growth across the region.

We're excited to support Eldora as they continue building the next generation of global investing.
