# March 2024 Monthly Recap

Matthew Eisner

•

Apr 3, 2024

March saw Dinari's dynamic entry onto Blast and Ethereum Mainnet, paired with the launch of RDDT.d, marking a pivotal expansion. Delve into our exciting advancements and learn how to leverage dShares for your benefit on our blog!

March has been a productive month at Dinari, with noteworthy expansions onto Blast and Ethereum Mainnet, and the exciting introduction of the RDDT.d asset. Let’s take a further look at the most important updates of March.

**March Community Call Live on Discord**

[Dinari](https://soundcloud.com/dinariglobal "Dinari") · [Dinari Community Call - March 2024](https://soundcloud.com/dinariglobal/community-call-march-2024 "Dinari Community Call - March 2024")

‍

**Launch on Ethereum Mainnet, Blast**

The Ethereum mainnet launch signifies a monumental stride in making Dinari's services and assets widely accessible, especially across the vibrant ecosystem of Ethereum, teeming with liquidity, legacy protocols and vast composability potential.

Building on this, Dinari has also expanded to Blast, enhancing its multi-chain footprint and facilitating improved asset management and wider user access.

‍

**New Asset (RDDT.d) Released, Camelot Pool**

Dinari listed Reddit (RDDT.d), within 48 hours of its IPO. This move, particularly through the Camelot pool offering a RDDT.dw-USDC swap option, opens doors to immediate exposure to newly IPO'd assets, enhancing investment opportunities within the crypto community.

* <https://camelot.exchange/>

‍

**B2B API Integration**

We've been busy refining our APIs, tailoring them for Web 2 & Web 3 companies looking to simplify stock trading and investing through tokenization. Expect announcements soon about partnerships with five companies currently integrating our solutions. 

* [https://dinari.com/busines](https://camelot.exchange/)

‍

**Guardian Role**

The Guardian role on Discord is now active! A quiz campaign is in full swing for these guardians, with the next session scheduled for next week. Congrats to our recent quiz champion!

Participating in these activities not only brings you closer to Dinari's core but might also set you up for our upcoming ambassador roles and future rewarding opportunities.

* [discord.gg/dinari](https://t.co/fnGoI86gO0)

‍

**Contract Update, Better DeFi Data**

We're gearing up for a pivotal contract update this month, set to be the last major overhaul of our primary protocol for some time, aimed at enhancing data availability and accuracy.

This change, rooted in our off-chain minting and burning process, will refine how we handle trading volumes and prices, improving integrations with platforms like CoinGecko and DeFi Llama. This initiative is expected to fine-tune asset tracking, increase our visibility, and correct any data inaccuracies previously encountered.

‍

**Dividends and Financial Enhancements**

An upcoming update to our dividends process is set to revolutionize how wrapped shares in pools accrue value from underlying asset distributions and USDC dividends, offering enhanced yield opportunities. This ensures that those interacting with our wrapped tokens, including liquidity providers, retain full dividend benefits.

‍

**Expanding Partnerships**

Looking ahead, significant integrations with our B2B API are in the pipeline, especially in non-traditional blockchain spaces like payroll services and conventional exchange products.

These integrations hint at a seamless blockchain experience, potentially onboarding millions without the complexities of managing seed phrases. Our excitement builds as we anticipate the launch of major partnerships, propelling Dinari’s presence in the market.
