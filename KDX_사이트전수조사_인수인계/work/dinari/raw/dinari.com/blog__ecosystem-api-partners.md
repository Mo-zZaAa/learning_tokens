# Dinari Ecosystem Highlights: API Distribution Partners

Rachel Ling

•

Jul 17, 2024

We are excited to highlight our strategic partnerships with Hydra X, Fasset, LaProp and Xade that are transforming the accessibility and diversity of investment opportunities for our customers.

In addition to our 1:1 backed tokenized Real World Assets like dShares and [USD+](https://usd.dinari.com/), we've been building a number of B2B solutions at Dinari to provide more utility to investors and businesses. These B2B offerings include APIs, a partner dashboard, and much more, and enable businesses to embed dShares in their applications and seamlessly offer exposure to US stocks and ETFs to their users around the globe. If you're interested in learning more, visit [dinari.com/business](https://dinari.com/business) or [partners.dinari.com](https://partners.dinari.com/) to get started.

We've already seen a number of integrations of dShare assets via our API into various FinTech and web3 apps (and many more launching soon). We wanted to take the time to highlight some of these early integrations and share some of the exciting solutions being built in the Dinari ecosystem.

‍

## **Hydra X: Building the Future of Digital Capital Markets**

[Hydra X](https://www.hydrax.io/) is a technology infrastructure builder for digital capital markets, offering regulatory-compliant, white-label trading platforms to financial institutions. Their mission is to facilitate the transition to Web 3.0, making them an invaluable partner in our journey to enhance trading experiences and broaden market access. Our collaboration with Hydra X aims to provide a regulatory-compliant, end-to-end platform which will support the trading of tokenized US stocks outside of the US.

‍

### **Fasset: Diversifying Portfolios with Secure Digital Assets**

[Fasset](https://fasset.io/) is a global digital assets exchange platform renowned for its secure and fully regulated environment. Dinari’s expertise in issuing compliant and stable tokenized Real World Assets (RWAs) combined with Fasset’s secure platform allows investors around the world to diversify their portfolios with innovative yet secure and ethical investment options. This collaboration aims to provide access to some of the world’s most trusted dShares to previously underserved investors, laying the groundwork for a more inclusive financial ecosystem.

‍

### **LaProp: Democratizing Real Estate Investments**

[LaProp](https://app.laprop.co/) is revolutionizing the real estate market through fractional ownership. Through our partnership, REITs powered by Dinari, such as [Annaly Capital ($NLY.dw)](https://app.laprop.co/reit/QJkQQm6WevQKturtoMBk) and [Prologis Inc ($PLD.dw)](https://app.laprop.co/reit/pCWTxbGIDy2AcjIUFhnJ) have been launched on LaProp. This collaboration enables investors to gain exposure to high-quality real estate investments in a fractionalized and accessible manner, democratizing access to lucrative real estate opportunities.

‍

### **Xade: Simplifying Digital Asset Trading**

[Xade](https://www.xade.finance/) is a cutting-edge digital assets exchange trading app that simplifies access to digital assets. Dinari dShares, including popular assets like $TSLA, $AAPL, $RDDT, and Bitcoin ETFs, are now available for trading through the Xade Mobile App. This partnership enhances the trading experience by offering a wide range of tokenized shares and ETFs, providing investors with greater flexibility and convenience in managing their portfolios.

‍

### **Embracing the Future of Finance**

These strategic partnerships mark a significant milestone in Dinari’s mission to democratize access to financial markets. By integrating our innovative solutions with the advanced platforms of [Hydra X](https://www.hydrax.io/), [Fasset](https://fasset.io/), [LaProp](https://(https/app.laprop.co), and [Xade](https://www.xade.finance/), we are paving the way for a more transparent, accessible, and efficient financial system.

‍
