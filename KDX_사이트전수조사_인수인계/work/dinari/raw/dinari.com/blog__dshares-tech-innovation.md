### New dShares

* [**Uber**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=UBER.d) **($UBER)** is expanding autonomous vehicle trials with Aurora and Volvo, and aims for 100% electric vehicles by 2030. Uber Freight now includes over 50,000 carriers.
* [**Cisco**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=CSCO.d) **($CSCO)** leads the 5G transition with Verizon and AT&T, and has enhanced its optical networking through Acacia Communications. Their AI-driven SecureX platform advances network security.
* [**Adobe**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=ADBE.d) **($ADBE)** launched AI tools like Sensei and the acquisition of Workfront improve marketing efficiency. The Adobe Experience Platform centralizes customer data for personalized experiences.
* [**Broadcom**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens?tokenSymbol=AVGO.d) **($AVGO)** pioneers Wi-Fi 6E for faster connectivity and partners with Apple for key semiconductors. They are also expanding into AI chipsets for machine learning applications
