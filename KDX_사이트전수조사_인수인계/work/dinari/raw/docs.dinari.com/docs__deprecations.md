# Deprecation Notices

Dinari is committed to maintaining the highest standards of security, availability and operational efficiency for our users. To ensure the long-term integrity of our ecosystem, we periodically conduct comprehensive reviews of the blockchains and protocols we support.

In the event that a specific network or protocol is slated for deprecation due to technical obsolescence or shifting industry standards, we want to reassure our holders that all underlying assets remain 100% safe. All deprecation notices will be listed here.

If a blockchain you are using is deprecated, your holdings are not lost. Impacted users can simply reach out to the Dinari support team to facilitate a seamless transition, allowing your assets to be reissued on a currently supported and secure network.

# Deprecations

## Blast Blockchain (April 20, 2026)

Dinari support for the [Blast blockchain](https://blast.io/) is ending on **April 20, 2026** due to minimal blockchain activity and risk of shutdown.

> 🚧
>
> Dinari will be transferring remaining dShares™ on Blast to the [Arbitrum One](https://arbitrum.io/) blockchain.

Orders will no longer be fulfilled on the Blast blockchain. If you have any questions regarding how to access your dShares™, please reach out to us at [[email protected]](https://docs.dinari.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection).

## Kinto Blockchain (Sept 30, 2025)

The [Kinto blockchain](https://www.kinto.xyz/) is scheduled to be shutdown on September 30, 2025.

> 🚧
>
> Dinari will be processing all dShare™ asset redemptions on the Kinto blockchain until this time.

Access to any dShares™ remaining on the Kinto blockchain after this time is not guaranteed. Due to the nature of the Kinto blockchain, we are unable to automatically transfer dShares to another supported blockchain. If this has happened to you, please reach out to us at [[email protected]](https://docs.dinari.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection).

For more information regarding the shutdown, please read the [Kinto is Shutting Down](https://medium.com/mamori-finance/kinto-is-shutting-down-fca59862f0e7) article.
