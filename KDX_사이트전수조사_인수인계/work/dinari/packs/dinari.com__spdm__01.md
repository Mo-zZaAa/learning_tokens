# S&P Digital Markets 50 Index

## A New Benchmark for the Digital Economy

The S&P Digital Markets 50 Index is a first-of-its-kind benchmark that combines 35 U.S. public equities with crypto businesses or exposure with 15 top digital assets into one, rules-based index.

In exclusive partnership with S&P Global, Dinari is issuing the S&P Digital Markets 50 Index Token ("SPDM"), built with equity-backed dShares and cryptocurrencies. The index makes it possible for qualified investors and institutions in the U.S. and around the world to gain diversified access to the digital markets economy.

[Contact Us](https://dinari.com/spdm)

## Key Highlights

### Dual Asset Class

35 U.S. equities and 15 major cryptocurrencies selected by market capitalization, liquidity, and relevance to the digital-asset ecosystem.

### Proven Methodologies

Objective rules-based eligibility criteria, quarterly rebalances and capped weights to ensure diversification and fairness.

### Comprehensive Digital-Economy Exposure

Captures growth across crypto infrastructure, blockchain innovation, exchanges, payment platforms, and leading digital tokens.

### Trusted Index Governance

Managed under S&P DJI’s rigorous index oversight and methodology standards.

### Tokenized Accessibility

In partnership with Dinari, the index can be accessed through fully backed digital tokens on blockchain infrastructure, bringing transparency, efficiency, and global reach.

## Gateway for Product Innovation

The Index can serve as a foundation for ETFs, tokenized funds, structured notes, or other investment vehicles.

### Example Use Cases

**Asset Managers** seeking to launch passive or hybrid digital-asset funds.

**Digital-asset Platforms** integrating tokenized benchmark investment products for user portfolios.

**Institutional Investors** requiring a diversified benchmark for performance comparison or allocation design.

**Advisers** aligning client portfolios with digital-asset growth trends via a single, auditable index.

## Risk & Considerations

Investing in digital-asset markets involves risk. Prospective investors should review all offering materials carefully.

* **Volatility Risk** — Both cryptocurrencies and crypto-related equities exhibit high price volatility.
* **Regulatory Risk** — Digital-asset rules and classifications are evolving globally.
* **Liquidity Risk** — Market liquidity for certain assets or tokens may vary significantly.
* **Custody & Technology Risk** — Secure storage and blockchain operations involve technical dependencies.
* **Token Infrastructure Risk** — Tokenized access introduces smart-contract, platform, and redemption risks.
* **Tracking & Implementation Risk** — Realized performance may differ from index returns due to fees or operational factors.
