These new dShares will allow users to invest in physical gold, silver, corporate bonds, municipal bonds, Ethereum, and more—directly on the blockchain.

### New dShares

1. [**iShares Gold Trust ($IAU)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/IAU.d?orderType=0)

IAU represents the iShares Gold Trust, giving investors exposure to gold through blockchain technology. This dShare tracks the price of physical gold, providing a secure and simple way for investors to diversify their portfolio with one of the most trusted stores of value.

2. [**abrdn Physical Silver Shares ETF ($SIVR)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/SIVR.d?orderType=0)

SIVR provides exposure to physical silver, a key asset for investors looking to diversify into precious metals. This dShare tracks the value of silver, offering easy access to a tangible commodity that has historically been a hedge against market volatility.

3. [**Grayscale Ethereum Trust ($ETHE)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/ETHE.d?orderType=0)

ETHE gives investors the opportunity to own Ethereum through the Grayscale Ethereum Trust. Ethereum has been at the forefront of smart contract platforms and decentralized applications, making it a popular choice for those who believe in the long-term growth of blockchain technology.

4. [**iPath Series B S&P 500 VIX Short-Term Futures ETN ($VXX)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/VXX.d?orderType=0)

For those looking to manage risk and volatility, VXX offers exposure to S&P 500 volatility, giving users a tool to hedge against stock market fluctuations. This dShare tracks short-term VIX futures, allowing investors to capitalize on market volatility.

5.[**iShares Broad USD High Yield Corporate Bond ETF ($USHY)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/USHY.d?orderType=0)

USHY represents high-yield U.S. corporate bonds, providing investors with exposure to high-interest debt securities. This dShare is perfect for those seeking higher returns and diversification in the bond market.

6. [**SPDR Nuveen Bloomberg High Yield Municipal Bond ETF ($HYMB)**](https://sbt.dinari.com/tokens/HYMB.d?orderType=0)

HYMB focuses on high-yield municipal bonds, which offer tax advantages to investors. These bonds support infrastructure projects and public services, making them a socially conscious choice while providing stable returns.

These new additions open up a diverse range of opportunities for our users. Whether you’re looking to hedge against inflation with precious metals, capitalize on cryptocurrency growth, or seek stable income through bonds, our new dShares offer an accessible, blockchain-powered way to expand your investment strategy.

**Unique Advantages of Blockchain-based Traditional Assets**

In the evolving landscape of finance, blockchain technology stands as a beacon of innovation, bringing transformative benefits to traditional investment assets. The integration of blockchain in financial transactions offers a unique blend of transparency, security, efficiency, and innovation, crucial for today's savvy investors.

**Transparency and Trust**: One of blockchain's most compelling features is its inherent transparency. This technology provides an unalterable ledger, visible to all network participants, fostering a new level of trust in financial transactions. In contrast to traditional systems, where records are often centrally stored and managed, blockchain's approach eliminates the need for intermediaries, reducing potential delays and errors.

**Security and Immutability**: Blockchain employs advanced cryptographic algorithms to secure data, ensuring that once a transaction is recorded, it cannot be altered. This immutability is a significant deterrent against fraud and unauthorized manipulation, a common concern in traditional banking systems. Such robust security measures are especially critical in an era where digital threats are ever-increasing.

**Efficiency and Speed**: By cutting down the intermediaries and streamlining processes, blockchain greatly enhances the efficiency and speed of financial transactions. Especially in areas like cross-border payments and settlements, blockchain's ability to facilitate direct peer-to-peer transactions reduces costs and time delays associated with traditional systems.

The new dShares launched on Arbitrum One encapsulate these advantages, offering blockchain investors and traditional asset enthusiasts a unique opportunity to diversify their portfolios with enhanced security, transparency, and efficiency.

# Kakaopay Securities and Dinari Partner to Expand Global Access to Korean Equities

Dinari

•

Sep 30, 2026

Collaboration will explore the application of Dinari’s tokenization infrastructure to Korean-listed equities and establish a framework for their international distribution.

Dinari is partnering with Kakaopay Securities, a leading mobile-first securities firm in South Korea, to advance international access to Korean-listed equities, including in the United States, through tokenization.

The partnership will explore the application of Dinari’s tokenization and distribution infrastructure to Korean-listed equities, with the goal of evaluating an additional channel for connecting Korean public markets with investors outside the country.

## Building a Framework for Korean Equities

Kakaopay Securities and Dinari will collaborate on a framework for sourcing underlying Korean-listed equities, conduct a proof of concept for their tokenization, and establish a joint task force to evaluate the operational, technical and regulatory requirements for potential international distribution.

The partnership will explore the application of Dinari’s dShares™ model to Korean-listed equities. dShares™ use a custodial model in which each token is backed one-to-one by the corresponding underlying security and designed to preserve applicable shareholder rights, including dividends and voting.

The companies will evaluate the technical and operational requirements for applying this model to Korean-listed equities as well as the framework for distribution through Dinari’s network of partners in the United States and international markets.

## Expanding International Access

The partnership is intended to explore an additional channel for Korean public companies to reach investors outside Korea. Today, international access to Korean equities remains limited, with only a subset of Korean companies accessible through depositary receipts listed on overseas exchanges.

Tokenization could provide another means of connecting Korean-listed securities with international markets while preserving the relationship to the underlying shares.

“Through this partnership with Dinari, which has experience operating tokenized securities within the U.S. regulatory framework, we are working to develop a concrete framework for the international distribution of Korean equities,” said Inyoung Chung, Executive Vice President of Kakaopay Securities. “Starting with the sourcing of Korean-listed equities and advancing through a proof of concept for equity tokenization, we will carefully assess the technical feasibility and help create new pathways connecting shares of leading Korean companies with a broader global market.”

“Korea is home to some of the world’s most important public companies, yet access to Korean equities remains limited for many investors outside the country,” said Gabe Otte, CEO of Dinari. “Our partnership with Kakaopay Securities is an opportunity to explore infrastructure that can connect Korean public markets with a broader global investor base. By combining Kakaopay Securities’ expertise in the Korean market with Dinari’s tokenization and distribution infrastructure, we can evaluate a model for expanding international access to Korean equities.”

## Dinari’s Tokenized Equities Infrastructure

Dinari has built tokenized equities infrastructure spanning issuance, distribution and trading. Through dShares™, Dinari provides access to 724 tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs for eligible investors in the United States and more than 85 jurisdictions worldwide.

dShares™ are designed to preserve shareholder rights and protections, including dividends, corporate actions and voting where applicable, alongside NBBO execution and redemption.

As part of the collaboration, Dinari and Kakaopay Securities plan to establish a joint task force later this year to further develop the operational framework and design of the proof of concept. The work will examine the issuance and redemption of tokens against underlying shares, reconciliation between tokenized and underlying securities, the treatment of applicable shareholder rights, and the infrastructure required for potential distribution through Dinari’s network of partners.

Dinari

•

Aug 27, 2025

Offramp becomes first partner to offer USD+ accounts with integrated Rain-issued cards

NEW YORK — August 27, 2025 — [Rain](https://www.rain.xyz/), the enterprise-grade infrastructure for stablecoin-powered payments, today announced native support for USD+, a yield-bearing stablecoin issued by [Dinari](https://dinari.com/), the leading provider of tokenized U.S. public securities. With this integration, Rain’s partners—including [Offramp](https://www.offramp.xyz/)—can now offer customers outside of the United States the ability to hold USD+ and use it globally through Rain-issued cards.

USD+ is backed 1:1 by U.S. Treasuries and cash equivalents and currently yields approximately 5% APY. It enables users to earn while they hold, creating a radically better financial experience for those in regions where traditional dollar accounts are inaccessible or underwhelming.

Through Rain’s API, partners can now offer users digital dollar accounts that automatically earn yield, without requiring minimum balances, foreign bank accounts, or complex investment products. These balances are instantly spendable anywhere Visa is accepted—turning idle funds into an income-generating, global payment source.

Offramp, a global neobank with a focus in emerging markets such as LATAM, is the first to roll out this capability. Users of Offramp located outside of the United States can hold USD+ in their accounts, earn daily yield, and use that balance for purchases around the world with their Offramp card and invest in tokenized equities through Dinari’s dShares™.

“From the very beginning, we designed Rain’s technology to be token-agnostic, stablecoin-native, and built to evolve as the asset landscape expands. Our ability to seamlessly extend support to USD+ exemplifies the strength of our platform,” said Charles Yoo-Naut, CTO & Co-Founder at Rain. “By supporting USD+, we’re giving partners like Offramp the ability to launch products that are both financially compelling and frictionless.”

USD+ is issued and settled on the Dinari Financial Network, which also powers dShares™—Dinari’s fully-backed, compliant tokenized equities. These building blocks enable neobanks, brokers, and fintechs to offer their customers regulated access to U.S. financial products that were previously out of reach.

“Rain’s integration of USD+ proves our infrastructure powers both yield-bearing stablecoins and tokenized equities for partners,” said Gabe Otte, Co-Founder & CEO of Dinari. “Our mission is to democratize access to U.S. dollars and equities globally. With Rain, fintechs can now put those dollars directly into users’ hands, where they can grow and remain spendable.”  
  
“We’re excited to deepen our partnership with Rain and Dinari as the first launch partner for USD+. Through Rain’s support for USD+, we’ll be able to unlock the true potential of stablecoins for users in emerging markets by combining spendability with yield generation,” said Luc Loja, CEO & Founder of Offramp. “This is a historic milestone in stablecoin innovation.”

# dShare Stock Splits: NVDA

Davis Ballard

•

Jun 7, 2024

Explore how Dinari handles stock splits seamlessly. Our automated system adjusts dShare balances to reflect new share quantities while maintaining their value, as demonstrated during NVIDIA’s 10-for-1 split.

Between market close today, June 7th and market open Monday June 10th, NVDA dShare holders will see the quantity of their dShares increase 10x (with underlying value remaining the same) with the company's 10-for-1 stock split. This move, announced after a strong first-quarter performance and a dividend increase, followed NVIDIA’s surge to become the world’s second most valuable company, surpassing Apple. With a valuation soaring past $3 trillion, NVIDIA is now second only to Microsoft.

### **How Dinari Handles Stock Splits**

Dinari has developed an innovative system to manage stock splits and reverse splits for its dShares, ensuring seamless transitions and maintaining the integrity of investors' holdings.

### **Dinari’s Rebalancing Mechanism**

At the core of Dinari’s solution is an automatic rebase process that adjusts token balances to reflect changes in the underlying share quantity. This ensures that every dShare remains 1-to-1 backed by its corresponding real-world asset at all times.

### **Split Execution Process**

1. **Notification and Verification**:
   * When a company announces a stock split, Dinari is notified via its clearing partners. Dinari verifies the split ratio and other relevant details before proceeding.
2. **Pre-Split Actions**:
   * Before the split occurs, Dinari temporarily stops accepting new orders for the affected dShare and cancels any existing orders. This suspension is crucial to ensure that all token balances can be accurately updated without any transaction conflicts.
3. **Rebase Execution**:
   * At the end of trading hours on the day before the split, Dinari executes the token rebasing process. This involves updating the token balances for all holders to reflect the new share quantities. For example, in a 10-for-1 split, each holder’s dShare balance is multiplied by ten. (NVIDIA's stock split increases the number of shares you own, but it does not change the total value of your investment)
4. **Post-Split Trading**:
   * Before trading resumes, Dinari lifts the temporary suspension on the dShare. Investors can then place new orders based on the post-split share quantities, ensuring a smooth transition back to normal trading activities.

### **Case Study: NVDA dShare Split Through Dinari**

When NVIDIA announced its stock split, Dinari’s automated systems kicked into action. The split ratio was verified, and a temporary suspension on new orders for NVDA dShares was put in place. Existing orders were canceled to prevent discrepancies during the rebase process.

Between the end of trading hours today, June 7th, and the start of trading hours on Monday, June 10th, Dinari will execute the rebase, adjusting all NVDA dShare balances according to the 10:1 split (10 shares will become 100 shares, with the total value of the stock holdings remaining the same).
