1.   Introduction

Dinari Securities, LLC (“Dinari”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is registered with the Securities and Exchange
Commission (SEC) as a broker-dealer and is a member of the Financial Industry Regulatory
Authority (FINRA) and the Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC).  Brokerage and
investment advisory services and fees differ, and you need to understand these differences.  Free
and simple tools are available to research firms and financial professionals at Investor.gov/CRS,
which also provides educational materials about broker-dealers, investment advisers, and
investing.

2.   What investment services and advice can you provide me?

Our primary business is providing retail customers with a web-based brokerage platform (the
“Platform”) to place orders to buy and sell equity stocks.  Dinari also offers private placements to
retail customers.  Dinari does not provide any recommendations or investment advice.  We do not
review the performance of your account, and your trades should be based on your risk tolerance
and investment objectives identified in your account application.  Our employees are registered
representatives for the purposes of providing customer service and trading support.  Therefore, our
employees are not investment advisers and do not offer any investment advice.  Further,

●  We do not offer account monitoring services.
●  We do not offer not-held orders.
●  We do not offer for sale or purchase proprietary products.
●  Dinari does not require any minimum to open a domestic account.

DINARI SECURITIES, LLC Business Continuity Plan Disclosure

Business Continuity Planning

Dinari Securities, LLC (“Dinari” or the “Firm”) has developed a business continuity plan on how
we will respond to events that significantly disrupt our business. Since the timing and impact of
disasters and disruptions are unpredictable, we will maintain flexibility in responding to actual
events as they occur. With that in mind, we are providing you with this information on our
business continuity plan.

Contacting Us – If after a significant business disruption, you cannot contact us as you usually
do via the Dinari web application, you should call our main office at 650-880-2038 or you
should go to our website at dinari.com.  If you cannot access us through either of those means,
you should contact our clearing firm, Alpaca Securities LLC, for instructions on how it may
assist you with the following services: order entry / processing / execution and access to funds
and securities.

Our Business Continuity Plan – We plan to quickly recover and resume business operations
after a significant business disruption and respond by safeguarding our employees and
property, making a financial and operational assessment, protecting the Firm’s books and
records, and allowing our customers to transact business. In short, our business continuity plan
is designed to permit our firm to resume operations as quickly as possible, given the scope and
severity of the significant business disruption.

Our business continuity plan addresses: data backup and recovery; all mission critical systems;
financial and operational assessments; alternative communications with customers, employees,
and regulators; alternate physical location of employees; critical supplier, contractor, bank and
counter-party impact; regulatory reporting; and assuring our customers prompt access to their
funds and securities if we are unable to continue our business.

Our clearing firm, Alpaca Securities LLC, backs up our important customer records in a
geographically separate area.  Additionally, certain customer account information is backed up
on the Ethereum blockchain.  While every emergency situation poses unique problems based
on external factors, such as time of day and the severity of the disruption, we have been
advised by our clearing firm that its objective is to restore its own operations and be able to
complete existing transactions and accept new transactions and payments within 24 hours.
Your orders and requests for funds and securities could be delayed during this period.

Varying Disruptions – Significant business disruptions can vary in their scope, such as only
our Firm, a single building housing our Firm, the business district where our Firm is located, the
city where we are located, or the whole region. Within each of these areas, the severity of the
disruption can also vary from minimal to severe.  In an SBD, we plan to continue in business
and notify you through our website www.dinari.com.  If the significant business disruption is so
severe that it prevents us from remaining in business, we will strive to assure our customer’s
prompt access to their funds and securities.

For more information – If you have questions about our business continuity planning, you can
contact us at us-support@dinari.com or call our office at 650-880-2038.

dShares™ Risk Disclosure

Please read this before you trade

This document describes risks of using the Dinari Securities platform and of holding your positions as
dShares™. It does not describe every risk of investing, and it is not investment advice. Dinari Securities
does not recommend securities and does not advise you on your orders. You are responsible for your
own investment decisions.

The most important thing to understand: you take on two different kinds of risk at the same time. The
first is ordinary investment risk, the stock you buy can fall in value, and you can lose money. The second
is the set of risks that come from recording your position on a blockchain. The second kind does not
replace the first. It is added to it.

1. Investment risk

The value of stocks and exchange-traded funds fluctuates. You may lose money, including the entire
amount you invest. Past performance and past price movement do not guarantee future results.
Securities held through Dinari Securities are not deposits, are not insured by the FDIC, are not
guaranteed by any bank, and may lose value.

Exchange-traded funds carry their own risks and costs. Before investing in an ETF, read its prospectus,
which is available from the fund’s sponsor or at www.sec.gov.

2. What you own, and the two records of it

When you buy a stock through Dinari Securities, you own that stock in your brokerage account, which is
carried by our clearing firm, Alpaca Securities LLC. Your position is also recorded on a public blockchain
as a dShare™. The dShare™ is a secondary record of the position in your account. It is not a separate
investment.

If the two records disagree. Your account statements and trade confirmations are the official record of
your holdings and transactions. If what you see on a blockchain explorer differs from your account
records, contact us before acting on it.

Reconciliation may lag. The blockchain record and your account records are maintained by different
systems. They may be out of step temporarily following a trade, a corporate action, a network delay or a
system failure. A balance you see on-chain may not reflect your actual position at that moment.

3. Risks of the blockchain record

•  Smart contract defects. dShares™ are governed by smart contracts, software deployed on a public
blockchain. Software can contain errors or vulnerabilities that are not discovered until they are
exploited. An audit reduces this risk but does not eliminate it. A defect could prevent transfers,
misstate balances or allow loss of tokens.

•  Network failures and congestion. Public blockchain networks can slow down, become

congested, fork, or stop producing blocks. During these periods transfers may be delayed, may fail,
or may cost significantly more than usual.

•  Irreversibility. Blockchain transactions generally cannot be reversed. A transfer sent in error

cannot be recalled by us, by our clearing firm, or by anyone else.

•  Multiple networks. dShares™ may be issued on more than one blockchain. Sending a dShare™ to
an address on the wrong network can result in permanent loss. Support for a particular network
may be added or withdrawn at any time.

•  Transfer controls. dShares are subject to transfer restrictions and Dinari reserves the right and
ability to freeze, block, reverse, burn or reissues dShares™ for the purposes of compliance,
corporate actions and errors to ensure accurate representation of the customer holdings.

•  Public visibility. Blockchain records are public. Transactions associated with your wallet address

may be visible to anyone and may be linked to you by third parties analyzing blockchain data.

4. Transferring dShares™ to a self-custody wallet

dShares may be transferred to self-custodial wallets owned by you. This does not affect your underlying
stock positions. Wallets must be registered and verified by Dinari or an affiliated partner prior to use.

•  Eligibility rules still apply. dShares™ may be transferred only to wallets that have completed

identity verification and are not associated with restricted jurisdictions. Transfers that do not meet
these requirements will fail.

•  Costs. Third parties may charge minting, redemption, network or custody fees in connection with

transfers. These are not fees of Dinari Securities and are not included in our fee schedule.

•  Corporate actions and dividends after transfer. Dividend and voting rights are preserved even
when dShares are held in self-custody wallets. Dividends will be deposited as stablecoin into the
wallet that holds the dShares. Voting will continue to be offered over email of the registered
customer over the customers underlying stock.

•  Third-party platforms. If you send dShares™ to another platform, exchange or protocol, you are

dealing with that party and not with Dinari Securities. We do not control and are not responsible for
what happens to your tokens there, and their terms, fees and protections, or lack of them, will
apply.

5. Rights associated with your position

Dividends. Dividends are issued within 2 business days from dividend payment date as USDC deposited
into the wallet holding the customer dShares™. USDC is issued by Circle Internet Financial, please see
USDC Terms for more information.

Corporate actions. dShares™ are designed to be 100% reflections of actual underlying securities and
related corporate actions including stock splits, reverse splits, mergers, tender offers, exchange offers,
spin-offs, rights offerings, stock dividends and delistings, and how fractional entitlements are handled.
Dinari reserves the right and ability to perform corporate actions on dShares™ to reflect the underlying
securities.

Voting and proxy materials. Holders of dShares™ and the underlying securities are eligible to receive
proxy materials and participate in voting. These materials and actions will be delivered to the email
registered with Dinari at the time of sign up.

Fractional positions. Fractional dShares™ are supported on selected stock tickers and are backed by
fractional positions in the underlying stock. Fractional positions are a product of our clearing broker
partner Alpaca Securities, LLC. Fractional positions may be terminated at any time due to corporate
actions, lack of liquidity, risk or other related reason. If a fractional position is closed due to this, it will be
liquidated at fair market value and stablecoin will be deposited into the customer wallet. The stock
tickers that are eligible for fractional shares are subject to change without notice.

6. Reliance on affiliated technology

The technology that creates and maintains dShares™ was developed by our parent, Dinari, Inc., an SEC-
registered transfer agent that is not a broker-dealer, and is licensed to Dinari Securities. We depend on
that technology and on our parent to operate it.

Your stock position and your brokerage account are separate from the dShare™ record, and are carried
by our clearing firm.

Conflict of interest. dShares™ are proprietary products that use our affiliate’s technology, and our
brokerage offerings on the platform are limited to them. Because our affiliate benefits when dShares™
are used, we have an incentive to offer them rather than other ways of holding the same stocks. You can
buy the same stocks through other brokers without the use of tokens. See our Form CRS for more about
our conflicts.

7. Funding your account with USDC

Extended-Hours Trading Disclosure

Please read this before trading outside regular market hours

Trading outside regular U.S. market hours involves risks that do not apply during regular
hours. This document describes those risks. It is not investment advice, and Dinari
Securities does not recommend securities or advise you on your orders.

When you can trade. Regular U.S. market hours are 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Eastern on
days the markets are open. Dinari Securities also accepts orders during pre-market hours
of 4:00 a.m. to 9:30 a.m. Eastern and after-hours from 4:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern.
Orders you place outside those sessions will be queued and eligible for execution when
the next session opens / will be rejected. Trading is not available on weekends and market
holidays.

Trading outside regular market hours involves risks that do not apply during regular
hours. Please read the following carefully before placing an order in an extended-hours
session.

Lower liquidity. Liquidity means the ability to buy or sell a security readily. There are
generally fewer buyers and sellers outside regular market hours, and fewer shares
available to trade. Your order may be executed only in part, or not at all.

Higher volatility. Volatility means the degree to which prices change. Because there is
less trading activity outside regular hours, prices can move more sharply. Your order may
be executed at a price substantially different from the last price displayed, or worse than
you expected.

Changing prices. Prices in an extended-hours session may not reflect the price of the
same security at the opening of the next regular session, or at any other time. A price you
see outside regular hours may differ significantly from the price available when regular
trading resumes.

Unlinked markets. Outside regular hours, different trading venues may not be connected
to one another. Prices displayed on one venue may not reflect prices available on another.
You may receive a worse price than would have been available elsewhere at the same
moment.

News announcements. Companies often release earnings and other significant news
outside regular market hours. Combined with lower liquidity and higher volatility, this can
cause exaggerated and unsustainable price movements. A price reached in an extended-
hours session may not hold when regular trading resumes.

Wider spreads. The spread is the difference between the price at which a security can be
bought and the price at which it can be sold. Lower liquidity and higher volatility outside
regular hours can widen spreads, increasing the cost of trading.

Exchange-traded funds: index and intraday indicative values. For exchange-traded
funds and similar products, the value of the underlying index and the intraday indicative
value may not be calculated or published outside regular market hours. You may not be
able to judge whether an ETF's price reflects the value of the securities it holds, and the
price you pay or receive may differ materially from that value.

Market data outside regular hours. Prices, volume and other market data displayed on
the Platform outside regular market hours may be limited, delayed or drawn from fewer
sources than during regular hours.

Halts and suspensions. If trading in a security is halted or suspended on its primary listing
exchange, Dinari Securities will not accept or execute orders in that security until trading
resumes.

Order estimates. Any quantity or price shown to you when you enter an order is an
estimate based on conditions at that moment. The price and quantity at which your order
is actually executed may differ, and the difference can be larger outside regular market
hours.

Your positions and dShares™. Dividends, corporate actions and the recording of your
positions as dShares™ are handled the same way regardless of when your order was
executed.
