About

What are Coinbase Tokenized Stocks?

Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are individual US company shares brought onchain, issued by Coinbase and backed 1:1 by shares held in regulated custody.

Backed by a real share

Each token is a beneficial claim on a real share, held in regulated, bankruptcy-remote custody separate from Coinbase.

Issued by Coinbase

Coinbase issues a matching token 1:1 against the share it holds, so the token tracks real economic exposure to the underlying stock.

Tokenized & usable across DeFi

The token is issued as a B20 token on Base, then held in a self-custodial wallet and used across the Base DeFi ecosystem.

Available only in eligible jurisdictions outside the US. [Read the prospectus & legal disclosures.](https://www.base.org/stocks)

Features

Stocks that do more.

On Base, stocks do more than sit in a brokerage account. Through participating apps and supported protocols, they can be self-custodied, traded around the clock, and put to work across DeFi.

Stocks meet DeFi

Hold tokenized stocks, then lend, borrow, or use them as collateral across the Base DeFi ecosystem.

Trade 24/7, 365

Trade tokenized stocks any hour of any day, including weekends and US market holidays.

Your keys, your stocks

Hold your tokenized stocks in a wallet only you control. No brokerage account needed.

### What are Coinbase Tokenized Stocks, and what is Base's role? 누른 뒤

- What are Coinbase Tokenized Stocks, and what is Base's role?
- Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are shares in individual US companies brought onchain. Coinbase is the issuer, licensed and authorized by the Abu Dhabi Global Market's Financial Services Regulatory Authority and Alpaca, a regulated broker and custodian, holds them in a bankruptcy-remote structure, in a trust for token holders. Base is the network the tokens run on: they are issued as B20 tokens, can be held in a self-custodial wallet, and can be traded and used across DeFi.

### How is this different from other tokenized stock products? 누른 뒤

- How is this different from other tokenized stock products?
- Coinbase Tokenized Stocks give you exposure to a real underlying share. Holders have a direct, senior claim on the underlying equity. Some other products offer a synthetic, a debt claim, or a derivative wrapper rather than a claim on a real share.

### Who is eligible? 누른 뒤

- Who is eligible?
- Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are offered under Regulation S and are not available in every market. They are not available to persons in the US and other restricted jurisdictions.

### What is B20? 누른 뒤

- What is B20?
- A token standard on Base, for tokenized stock products. It behaves like an ERC-20 token while supporting the compliance and corporate-action features these assets require. See docs.

### How are dividends and splits handled? 누른 뒤

- How are dividends and splits handled?
- Through an onchain multiplier rather than by changing your token balance. Your raw token count stays the same while the multiplier scales to reflect dividends (reinvested after applicable withholding and fees) and splits. Your share-equivalent value updates without breaking DeFi positions.

### Who can mint or redeem tokens? 누른 뒤

- Who can mint or redeem tokens?
- Primary minting and redemption are limited to KYC-onboarded institutional partners and Authorized Participants, using the same create-and-redeem model as traditional ETFs and ADRs.

### Can these stocks be traded around the clock? 누른 뒤

- Can these stocks be traded around the clock?
- The tokens live onchain and can be traded around the clock including market holidays.

### How do I verify a contract address? 누른 뒤

- How do I verify a contract address?
- Check the ticker and issuer against the registry on this page and confirm the contract address matches before interacting. Official addresses link to BaseScan. If a token, issuer, or venue is not listed here, treat it with caution.
