## Taylor Brown

Chief of Staff

Taylor Brown is Chief of Staff of Robinhood Markets. She serves as an advisor to the CEO and leads the CEO Office, strategic planning, leadership and business operations, and executive communications. Prior to joining Robinhood in 2020, she held roles in Human Resources at Mapbox and served a long tenure leading Management Research and Consulting teams at Gartner.
