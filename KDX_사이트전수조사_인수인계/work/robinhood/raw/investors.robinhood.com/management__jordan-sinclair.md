## Jordan Sinclair

President of Robinhood UK and General Manager of Bitstamp UK

Jordan Sinclair is the President of Robinhood UK Ltd and the General Manager of Bitstamp UK Ltd, leading the UK’s brokerage and crypto business. Prior to joining Robinhood, Jordan was Managing Director, Europe at Freetrade, where he launched Freetrade's first international brokerage market, and Director, Corporate Development & Strategy at Barclays. Jordan has wide ranging financial services experience and is a qualified Chartered Accountant. Jordan holds a Bachelor of Commerce and Post Graduate qualification from the University of Cape Town and a MSc from the University of Edinburgh.
