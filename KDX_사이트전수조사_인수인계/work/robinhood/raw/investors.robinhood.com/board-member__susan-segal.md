## Susan Segal

Susan Segal was appointed as President and CEO of Americas Society and Council of the Americas (“AS/COA”) in 2003. Prior to joining AS/COA, Ms. Segal founded an investment advisory firm focused on Latin America and the U.S. Hispanic market. She was also a partner and head of the Latin American Group at JPMorgan Partners/Chase Capital Partners, where she pioneered early-stage venture capital investing in Latin America. She currently serves on the boards of Vista Energy, S.A.B. de C.V. and MercadoLibre, Inc. Ms.Segal received a master’s degree in business administration from Columbia University and a bachelor’s degree from Sarah Lawrence College.
