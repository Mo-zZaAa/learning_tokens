## Dara Treseder

Dara Treseder has served as a member of our board of directors since November 2021. Ms. Treseder is Chief Marketing Officer at Autodesk, a provider of 3D design, engineering, and entertainment software and services, a position she has held since October 2022. From 2020 to 2022, she served as Senior Vice President, Head of Global Marketing & Communications at Peloton, an interactive fitness platform. From 2018 to 2020, she served as Chief Marketing and Communications Officer at Carbon, a 3D printing technology company. Previously, Ms. Treseder was Chief Marketing Officer at GE Business Innovations and GE Ventures from 2017 to 2018, and held various positions at Apple from 2013 to 2017, most recently as Global Head of Demand Generation, Filemaker. She also served in a FINRA-registered position at Goldman Sachs & Co., an investment banking firm, from 2010 to 2012. Ms. Treseder currently serves on the board of directors of the Public Health Institute, a non-profit organization, which she joined in 2017, and previously served on the board of directors of PG&E Corporation, a large natural gas and electric utility, from 2020 to 2021. Ms. Treseder holds an M.B.A. from Stanford University, and an A.B. from Harvard University.
