## Carley Olivas

Senior Director, Brand Marketing and Marketing Operations

Carley Olivas is the Senior Director, Brand Marketing and Marketing Operations at Robinhood Markets. She leads the brand, social, partnerships, and creative teams with the goal of elevating the Robinhood brand and connecting authentically with customers. Carley also oversees operations for the marketing organization, focused on driving efficiencies. Prior to joining Robinhood in 2021, she spent a decade building and leading brand teams and efforts across tech and fashion at Uber, Uber Eats, and Ralph Lauren. Carley holds a BBA in Finance from University of Iowa.
