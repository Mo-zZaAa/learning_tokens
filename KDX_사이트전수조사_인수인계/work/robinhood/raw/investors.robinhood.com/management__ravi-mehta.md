## Ravi Mehta

Vice President, Credit Cards and Chief Credit Officer

Ravi Mehta is the Vice President of Credit Cards and Chief Credit Officer at Robinhood Markets. Prior to joining Robinhood, Ravi spent over 20 years in the consumer lending industry, including as the Global Head of Credit Risk at Nubank in his last role. He also held various leadership roles at Capital One Bank. Ravi holds an engineering degree from IIT Bombay and an MBA from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania
