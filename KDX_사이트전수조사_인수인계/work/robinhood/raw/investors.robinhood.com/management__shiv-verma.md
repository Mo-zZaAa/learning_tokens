## Shiv Verma

Chief Financial Officer

Shiv Verma is the Chief Financial Officer at Robinhood Markets. Prior to his current position, Shiv served as the Senior Vice President of Finance and Strategy, where he oversaw the company's Finance, Treasury, Corporate Strategy, and Corporate Development teams. Prior to Robinhood, Shiv held roles at Oportun, PIMCO, Franklin Templeton Investments, Symphony Asset Management, JPMorgan, and the Oakland A's. Shiv received a B.A. in Economics from Stanford University and an MBA from UCLA.
