# Release Details

## Robinhood Markets, Inc. to Announce Third Quarter 2026 Results on October 27, 2026

October 1, 2026

[PDF Version](https://investors.robinhood.com/node/16731/pdf)

MENLO PARK, Calif., Oct. 01, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Today, Robinhood Markets, Inc. (“Robinhood”) (NASDAQ: HOOD) announced that it will release its third quarter 2026 financial results on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, after market close. Robinhood will host a video call with Chairman & Chief Executive Officer Vlad Tenev and Chief Financial Officer Shiv Verma to discuss its results at 2:00 PM PT / 5:00 PM ET on the same day. The video call and supporting materials will be available at [investors.robinhood.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=rTfSz3KprZKOUvog_kZCeh10Qi_LMUx-w0Y8_6F4ISBP-vPBojWQPenBdaKmY64HvBzKcGzZahHpZRoSuj6kcamMLs5Lzs_svxOMKaiXtjc%3D). The event will also be live streamed to YouTube and [X.com](https://x.com/) via Robinhood’s official channels, @RobinhoodApp, and within the Robinhood mobile app. Following the call, a replay and transcript will be available at [investors.robinhood.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=rTfSz3KprZKOUvog_kZCeh10Qi_LMUx-w0Y8_6F4ISCizRjwFNoveV8ng-jn7ns_HKIeQ0O0N-9CMwvd7DJ4GZ0zqqZhL3tjA1VrLMPhyEc%3D).

Ahead of the call, Robinhood shareholders can visit [https://app.saytechnologies.com/robinhood-markets-2026-q3](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=z00O9fRANTDOJHPCnyuNXaTULmm0rYWRYEJxP2R7Wk6ww5edeaR_DCMHYuwAv22ccSFP6G8MnDdaYpntt4oyCkDeTXTHGwncscomAVO4hVNzHv1wCGM3U3lqn0Pjcf8OSHelXVUAySwcTOs6bq5ncNmLtVZoumGtqjjupxsoSKXuwvC0UhxWxwE2Y53Gjb3N) to submit and upvote questions for management using the Q&A platform developed by Say by Robinhood. The Q&A platform will be open for question submission starting Tuesday, October 20, 2026, at 2:00 PM PT / 5:00 PM ET. Shareholders will be able to submit and upvote questions until Monday, October 26, 2026, at 2:00 PM PT / 5:00 PM ET. Management will address a selection of the most upvoted questions relating to Robinhood’s business and financial results on the earnings call. Shareholders can email [hello@saytechnologies.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=pGUFxxEU4FrIuQOubJFOrGPiTnxrNTQYPxV9BuSa9wjqER7EOWehxZNEw5c64h8c48J3fNdMWRJEpvbIvi0SVobxBf2qLSBYGVL375t4UJTVcRGUwDFoTpwdM3h8v4bk) for any support inquiries.
