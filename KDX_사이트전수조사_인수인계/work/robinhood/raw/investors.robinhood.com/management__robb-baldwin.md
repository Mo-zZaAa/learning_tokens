## Robb Baldwin

Vice President and General Manager, TradePMR

Robb Baldwin is the Vice President and General Manager of TradePMR at Robinhood. He joined in February 2025 following Robinhood's acquisition of the firm. He founded TradePMR in 1998 to offer service-oriented technology and custodial solutions to registered investment advisors. Robb began his career in wealth management as a financial advisor and holds FINRA Series 7, 4, 24, and 53 licenses.
