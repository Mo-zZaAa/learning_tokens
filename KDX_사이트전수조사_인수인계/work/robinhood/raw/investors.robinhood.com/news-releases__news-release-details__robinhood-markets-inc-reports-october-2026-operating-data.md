# Release Details

## Robinhood Markets, Inc. Reports October 2026 Operating Data

November 12, 2026

[PDF Version](https://investors.robinhood.com/static-files/60041c13-fa62-4bc5-ae00-36b45fe8564c "Robinhood Markets, Inc. Reports October 2026 Operating Data.pdf") 325.8 KB

MENLO PARK, Calif., Nov. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Robinhood Markets, Inc. (“Robinhood”) (NASDAQ: HOOD) today reported select monthly operating data for October 2026.

* Funded Customers at the end of October were 28.9 million (up approximately 130 thousand from the end of September 2026, up approximately 1.8 million year-over-year).
* Total Platform Assets at the end of October were $383 billion (down 3% from the end of September 2026, up 12% year-over-year).
* Net Deposits were $15.1 billion in October, or a 46% annualized growth rate relative to September 2026 Total Platform Assets.  
  + Over the last twelve months, Net Deposits were $80.5 billion, or an annual growth rate of 23% relative to October 2025 Total Platform Assets.

* Trading Volumes in October:
  + Equity Notional Trading Volumes were $325 billion (up 1% from September 2026, up 2% year-over-year). Average daily volumes (“ADVs”)3 were $14.8 billion (down 4% from September 2026, up 6% year-over-year).
  + Options Contracts Traded were 289 million (flat from September 2026, up 8% year-over-year). ADVs were 13.1 million contracts (down 4% from September 2026, up 13% year-over-year).
  + Crypto Notional Trading Volumes were $17.0 billion (down 21% from September 2026, down 48% year-over-year), including Robinhood App Notional Trading Volumes of $6.8 billion (down 24% from September 2026, down 51% year-over-year) and Bitstamp Notional Trading Volumes of $10.2 billion (down 18% from September 2026, down 45% year-over-year). Robinhood App ADVs were $219 million (down 26% from September 2026, down 51% year-over-year), and Bitstamp ADVs were $328 million (down 21% from September 2026, down 45% year-over-year).
  + Event Contracts Traded were 5.9 billion (up 5% from September 2026, up 136% year-over-year). ADVs were 190 million contracts (up 2% from September 2026, up 135% year-over-year).
* Interest Earning Assets in October:  
  + Margin balances at the end of October were $22.9 billion (up 4% from the end of September 2026, up 39% year-over-year).

* + Cash and Deposit balances at the end of October were $16.4 billion (down 20% from the end of September 2026, up 12% year-over-year). The month-over-month decline was mostly driven by strong customer net buying. This decline has largely reversed in early November.
  + Cash Sweep balances at the end of October were $30.8 billion (down 5% from the end of September 2026, down 10% year-over-year).
* Total Securities Lending Revenue in October was $34 million (up 6% from September 2026, down 43% year-over-year). This includes Securities Lending, Net revenues of ($12) million.
