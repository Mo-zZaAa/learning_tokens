## Paula Loop

Paula Loop has served as a member of our board of directors since June 2021. Ms. Loop was most recently a Partner at PwC where she led PwC’s Governance Insights Center, a position from which she retired in June 2021. Ms. Loop has extensive experience in governance, technical accounting and SEC and financial reporting matters. Ms. Loop previously served as PwC’s New York Metro Regional Assurance Leader and prior to that as the U.S. and Global Talent Leader. She also previously served on PwC’s Board of Partners and on the firm’s Governance, Risk and Quality and Executive Compensation Committees. Ms. Loop also currently serves on the boards of directors of APi Group, Inc., a construction engineering company, and Fastly, Inc., a cloud computing services provider. Ms. Loop is a Certified Public Accountant. Ms. Loop holds a B.S. from the University of California at Berkeley.
