## Nora Chan

Senior Director, Communications

Nora Chan is the Senior Director of Communications at Robinhood Markets, leading executive communications. She first joined Robinhood in 2019 and worked at the company until 2022, when she joined a Web3 startup as Vice President of Communications. She returned to Robinhood in 2024. Previously, she was a Corporate Communications Manager at Meta, specializing in financial communications. Nora holds a B.S. in Journalism and Mass Communication from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.
