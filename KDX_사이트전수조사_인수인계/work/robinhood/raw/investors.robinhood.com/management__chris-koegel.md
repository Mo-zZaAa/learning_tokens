## Chris Koegel

Vice President, Corporate Finance & Investor Relations

Chris Koegel is the Vice President of Corporate Finance and Investor Relations at Robinhood Markets. Prior to joining Robinhood in 2022, Chris was the Head of Investor Relations at LPL Financial and a Principal at Bain & Company. Chris holds a B.A. in Mathematics from Williams College and an MBA from Dartmouth College.
