## Deepak Rao

Senior Vice President and General Manager, Robinhood Money, Robinhood Gold, Growth, and Marketing

Deepak Rao is the Senior Vice President and General Manager, Robinhood Money, Robinhood Gold, Growth, and Marketing. He is the Co-Founder and CEO of X1, which was acquired by Robinhood Markets in July 2023. Before starting X1, Deepak was a Group Product Manager at Twitter where he led Twitter's core consumer product and was responsible for news feed, messaging, tweeting, and growth. Before joining Twitter, he co-founded Mine, which was acquired by Twitter in 2013. Deepak grew up in India and holds an M.S. in Computer Science from Stanford University.
