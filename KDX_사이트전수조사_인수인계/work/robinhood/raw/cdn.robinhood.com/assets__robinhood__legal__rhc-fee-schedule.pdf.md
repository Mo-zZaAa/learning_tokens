Robinhood Crypto’s default routing routes orders to market makers (excluding limit orders). We receive volume rebates from those market makers. For Exchange
Routing, Robinhood routes orders to one or more partner exchanges—EDX Markets, Inc. and Bitstamp USA, Inc. Robinhood Crypto does not receive any rebates on
Exchange Routing and charges a transaction based fee for such orders. Where Exchange Routing is available, it is used to route all limit orders as well as all orders
placed through the Robinhood Legend trading platform (via the website) and orders placed through the Ladder and advanced charts functionality (via the app or
website), irrespective of whether you have selected market maker routing or Exchange Routing as your default setting for order routing.

Robinhood reserves the right to vary its rates and fees among customers in connection with special offers, promotions, or arrangements based on criteria such as
account values, account balances or customer loyalty.

Robinhood Connect fees are calculated at the time you place your order and can be influenced by factors such as your chosen payment method, the participating
dApp, and other costs we incur to facilitate your transaction. You can view the fees in the review order screen before submitting your transaction.

Processing and service fees assessed by third parties may be passed through to the customer’s account. Some products and services may require additional fees or
transaction minimums not specifically listed here. Prior to placing a trade or requesting any services, it is the customer’s responsibility to ask Robinhood Crypto if
any additional fees will apply.
