Information for Law Enforcement Authorities

The  following  operational  guidelines  are  for  law  enforcement  officials  or  authorized  government  agencies
seeking certain records from the affiliates1 of Robinhood Markets, Inc. (collectively, “Robinhood”).

Robinhood appreciates the work of authorized government agencies and law enforcement authorities around
the world to keep people and communities safe. Upon receipt, Robinhood is prepared to review, validate, and
respond to law enforcement requests based on applicable law and policy. This information may be updated at
any time.

Civil requests should be served upon the relevant registered agent (United States) or registered office.

Submission of Law Enforcement Requests

Except in extraordinary circumstances, Robinhood requires a valid subpoena, warrant, court order, summons
or  other  similar  document  issued  by  a  government  agency  or  law  enforcement  official  (“Legal  Request”)
before disclosure of personal data or other regulated information.

Legal Requests must be addressed to the appropriate Robinhood entity(ies).  Robinhood also reserves the
right  to  direct  any requesting law enforcement authority or authorized government agency to make requests
via  the  appropriate  legal  channels,  including  any  applicable  mutual  legal  assistance  frameworks.   Please
include as much specific information as possible that would identify the relevant Robinhood account(s) and/or
transactions.

To  ensure  a  timely response by Robinhood, Legal Requests should be sent to LERequests@robinhood.com.
Please  do  not  send  any  Legal  Request  or  other  inquiries  to  Robinhood  Support  or  any  other  channel  not
intended  for  law  enforcement.  If  you  do  so,  you  should  expect  longer  response  times.   Currently,  our
minimum  response  time  is  approximately  45  days  from  receipt  of  valid legal process.   Robinhood will not
respond to correspondence sent by non-law enforcement officials to the email address above.

Receipt of a Legal Request through the LERequests@robinhood.com email is for convenience purposes only.
Robinhood does not waive, and does not intend to waive, any potentially applicable objections, including lack
of jurisdiction or proper service.

UK and EU Legal Process Requirements

Robinhood  discloses  account  records  in  accordance  with  the  RHUK  Customer  Agreement, RHUK Privacy
Policy,  RHEU  Customer  Agreement,  RHEU  Privacy  Policy  (EU),  and/or  the  Privacy  Policy  for  Bitstamp

Under  applicable  laws  in  the  UK and EU/EEA, a Legal Request issued by an authorized government
agency  or  law  enforcement  official  is  required  to  compel  the  disclosure  of  account  or  customer
information.

Robinhood will search for and disclose information that is specified with particularity in an appropriate form
of  Legal  Request  and  which we are reasonably able to locate and retrieve. As stated in the relevant privacy
policies,  we  may  collect,  use,  preserve,  and  disclose  customer  or  account  information  to  an  authorized
governmental  agency  or  law enforcement official if we believe that it is in accordance with, or required by,
any  applicable  law,  legal  process,  court  order,  or  legal  investigation,  or  to  protect  and  defend  the  rights,
interests, safety, and security of Robinhood, our users, or the public.

Legal requests that involve the transfer of personal data outside of the UK and EEA are subject to review and
assessment  of  the  transfer  risk  and  compliance  with  applicable  local  regulation. In some cases, Robinhood
may not be able to comply with such Legal Requests.

Robinhood  exercises  reasonable  efforts  to  respond  in  a  timely  manner  to  Legal  Requests  that  are  validly

clearly lists uniquely identifying information, such as an account number or tax identification number.

Notification

Robinhood reserves the right to notify customers who use our services of requests for their information prior
to disclosure, unless we are prohibited by law from doing so or in exceptional circumstances, emergencies, or
when notice would be counterproductive.

Testimony

Robinhood  does  not provide expert testimony or witness support. Robinhood records are self-authenticating
pursuant to law and should not require the testimony of a records custodian. If a special form of certification
is required, please attach it to your Legal Request.

Fees for Compliance

Robinhood reserves the right to charge reasonable administrative fees (unless otherwise prohibited by law) for
the  production  of  documents  in response to any Legal Request. Such fees apply on a per account basis. We
may  also  charge  additional  fees  for  costs  incurred  in  responding  to  unusual  or  burdensome  requests.
Robinhood  reserves  the  right  to  waive  such  fees  in  matters  investigating  potential  harm  to  Robinhood,  its
customers, and emergency requests.

Attachments, Garnishments, Seizures, and Levies/Liens

Law enforcement requests that involve the turnover of funds pursuant to attachment, garnishment, or seizure
should  be  emailed  to  LERequests@robinhood.com  for  processing.  Robinhood  will  not  respond  to
correspondence sent by non-law enforcement officials sent to this email address.

Government  requests  related  to  tax  levies/liens  and  child  support  levies/liens  should  be  emailed  to
accountrestraints@robinhood.com for processing.

Updates to the Guidelines

Robinhood may update this information periodically. Please consult the guidelines before making any request.
