preauthorization amount will place a “hold” on your available funds on the Card until the merchant sends
us the final payment amount of your purchase. Following receipt of the final payment amount, the
preauthorization amount on hold will be removed, but it may take up to seven (7) days (up to thirty (30)
days for hotels) for the hold to be removed. During the hold period, you will not have access to funds in
your Card Account for the pre-authorized amount.

person (such as for a mail order, telephone, or Internet purchase), the legal effect will be the same as if
you presented the Card to the merchant in person.
