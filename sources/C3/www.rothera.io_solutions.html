Broader Access. Deeper Markets. Better Service.
Rothera brings FCMs and market makers together to expand participation and support liquid, high integrity event contract markets wrapped in world-class service.

Solutions For:

Futures Commission Merchants (FCMs)

Rothera enables FCMs to offer event contract access to their customers through a CFTC-regulated exchange and clearinghouse.

Familiar Workflows and Integrations:

  • Standard FIX API access for order entry and market data.
  • Support for ISV applications (front-, middle-, and back-office software integrations via API).
  • Conventional FCM workflow support (admin UIs, SFTP file access, reference data download, deposits/withdrawals including wire and RTP).

Become a Trading Participant and/or Clearing Member:

  • Access a venue that does not compete with you for end customers (retail or institutional).
  • Confidently serve new and existing customers seeking access to event contract markets without disrupting operations, compliance, and trading workflows.
  • Offer institutional customers cleared risk-management products that can reduce reliance on bilateral arrangements.
  • Experience support and service that understands your business and makes onboarding, integration, and participation easy and efficient.

Solutions For:

Market
Makers

Rothera enables professional market makers to provide liquidity for retail and institutional order flow through a central limit order book (CLOB).

Participation
Requirements:

  • Qualify as an eligible contract participant (ECP).
  • Enter into Rothera’s market making program agreement.
  • Commit to, and meet, minimum quoting metrics.

Program Benefits

  • Market maker pricing
  • Direct market access via FIX API
