Rothera: Built for the Frontier
Our vision for scaling prediction markets through regulated infrastructure, responsible listings and institutional access.

Prediction markets are entering a new phase in their evolution. Our mission is to help them scale with the discipline and technology institutions and intermediaries demand.

‍

Over the past few years, prediction markets have moved from the margins of financial services toward the mainstream. In the last eight months alone, monthly event contract volumes have nearly quintupled, rising from less than $5 billion last fall to around $24 billion in April, according to Pew Research and The Block.

What began as a novel way for retail investors to express views on discrete event outcomes is quickly becoming a more serious market structure conversation involving the world’s most prominent financial institutions. How can event contracts be used to precisely manage risks that historically have been difficult to target? How should event risks be listed, traded, cleared and governed? How can markets support better information discovery without sacrificing integrity? What is needed to support it all reliably, at capital markets-level scale and performance?

Rothera was created to answer those questions.

We are a CFTC-regulated exchange and clearinghouse backed by Robinhood and Susquehanna International Group and built to support event contract markets. Rothera was designed not as a retail destination like others in the market, but as institutional infrastructure for a fast-emerging asset class. Our role is to provide the premier regulated, professionally operated prediction marketplace that enables institutional participants to manage risk through an intermediary-based structure they recognize and trust.

That mission is now taking shape in the market. Today, Robinhood announced the release of its new World Cup trading experience through which it will route select World Cup event contracts to Rothera. We are thrilled to support this launch, helping bring regulated event contract trading tied to one of the largest global sporting events in history to market through a trusted intermediary.

But the World Cup is only the beginning.

Sports contracts more generally have demonstrated the scale of demand for event-based markets. They also show why these markets need the right infrastructure. Major events significantly influence business activity, both locally and globally – everything from local businesses whose fortunes may rise and fall with the home team’s success to the publicly traded media, hospitality and consumer brands whose revenues can be materially affected by which clubs qualify for a tournament or the postseason. Event contracts can help investors and businesses with exposure manage risk, express their views, gauge sentiment and respond to market-driven signals around outcomes that have real economic consequences.

The same logic extends well beyond sports. Inflation, interest rates, labor data, weather, energy flows, policy decisions, geopolitical developments and other measurable events all shape how businesses and investors understand risk. Properly designed prediction markets can help turn uncertainty into actionable market intelligence, just as futures markets once transformed agricultural risk from an unavoidable fact of life into something that could be priced, traded and managed. But that potential can only be realized if the market develops responsibly.

For Rothera, that comes down to three practical commitments:

  • Responsible listing. Event contracts should be clear, objective and capable of supporting durable markets. Rothera reviews contracts with a focus on compliance, market integrity, liquidity and susceptibility to manipulation. We are not here to list every possible event, and we will not pursue markets that undermine trust or credibility for the broader ecosystem.
  • Non-conflicted access. Rothera is not a consumer destination and does not compete with brokers for their end customers. We provide exchange and clearing infrastructure for FCMs, market makers and other ecosystem participants, helping them access event contract markets while preserving their own client relationships, brands and business models.
  • Rigorous innovation. The future of prediction markets will depend on thoughtful, competent execution as much as demand. Rothera is committed to the product work, market structure design, research and collaboration required to help institutions participate with confidence. Our name takes inspiration from Antarctica’s Rothera Research Station, a facility built for one of the world’s most demanding environments. We bring a similar approach to these markets. Innovation matters, but so do preparation, precision and discipline.

These are very early days for the institutional evolution of prediction markets. In the months ahead, we look forward to expanding our market ecosystem, onboarding additional participants and supporting a broader range of event contract use cases. Our goal is simple: to help prediction markets grow into a regulated, resilient and fully credible part of the financial markets landscape.

We are built for the frontier – and built for what comes next.
