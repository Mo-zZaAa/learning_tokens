 Introducing Ondo Global Listing: A New Service to Tokenize Stock IPOs on Listing Day Across Multiple Blockchains
 Ondo Finance
 Official Blog
 New service for crypto wallets, exchanges, and blockchains to offer day-one IPO access to their non-US users worldwide
 Enables NYSE- and NASDAQ-listed companies to be tokenized across Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain, and more
 The tokenized stocks are permissionless and transferable outside the US, with stablecoin-like composability across onchain wallets, exchanges, and protocols.
 Today, we are excited to introduce Ondo Global Listing , a new service designed for crypto wallets, exchanges, and blockchains to offer access to tokenized US stock IPOs on the day of the public offering.
 Ondo Global Listing enables newly listed NYSE- and NASDAQ-listed companies to be tokenized across Ethereum, Solana, and BNB Chain on day one of listing. This allows crypto platforms like wallets and exchanges to provide access to day-one IPO exposure to their users worldwide in near real time.
 The service builds on Ondo’s recent tokenization of BitGo stock on IPO day, which demonstrated the ability to tokenize economic exposure to a newly public US company onchain across multiple blockchains at the moment it entered public markets.
 Unlike prior tokenized IPO offerings, Ondo tokenized stocks are permissionless and freely transferable, with stablecoin-like composability across onchain wallets, exchanges, and protocols (subject to certain jurisdictional prohibitions and restrictions). For non-US investors, Ondo Global Listing expands access to US capital markets by making exposure to IPO stocks available through the onchain platforms those investors already use, without the traditional geographic and infrastructural restrictions that have historically limited participation. Users will also be able to buy these tokenized IPO stocks at substantially the same price as in a brokerage account.
 The service is an expansion of Ondo Stocks (previously known as Ondo Global Markets), Ondo’s tokenization platform for public securities. Ondo Stocks is now the largest platform for tokenized stocks, representing more than 50% of the total market share and exceeding all other platforms combined by total value locked, despite having the lowest time in market. Since launching in September 2025, Ondo Stocks has now surpassed $500 million in total value across 200+ tokenized stocks with tens of thousands of asset holders driving over $9 billion in cumulative trading volume. Ondo tokenized stocks are now live across Solana, Ethereum, and BNB Chain, and supported by leading wallets, exchanges, custodians, and protocols. and are supported by many major wallets, custodians, and exchanges, including Binance Wallet, Bitget, Trust Wallet, Blockchain.com, and more.
