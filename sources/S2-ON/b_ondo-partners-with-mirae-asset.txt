 Ondo Partners With Mirae Asset to Tokenize Its Global X ETF Lineup
 Ondo Finance
 Official Blog
 We're excited to announce a new partnership between Ondo Finance and Mirae Asset Global Investments , one of Asia's largest asset management firms. Together, we've signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) to tokenize Mirae Asset's Global X ETF lineup through Ondo Stocks , the largest tokenized securities platform globally.
 Tokenized Global X ETFs Spanning AI, Space, Robotics, & More
 Mirae Asset becomes the first asset manager in Asia to bring exposure to its existing Global X ETF lineup onchain. Global X is an ETF arm of Mirae Asset with several of its funds among the most widely held in their categories.
 The first batch of Global X tokenized ETFs covers:
 Space Innovation ETF (ORBX)
 AI & Technology ETF (AIQ)
 Robotics & AI ETF (BOTZ)
 Silver Miners ETF (SIL)
 Blockchain ETF (BKCH)
 Defense Tech ETF (SHLD)
 Lithium & Battery Tech ETF (LIT)
 S&P 500 Covered Call ETF (XYLD)
 Digital Content Creators ETF (DTCR)
 NASDAQ 100 Covered Call ETF (QYLD)
 The collaboration will start with Global X’s U.S.-listed ETFs and expand to funds listed across Europe, Hong Kong, Japan, Canada, and Australia. Mirae Asset remains the asset manager of the underlying ETFs, while Ondo provides the tokenization infrastructure and distribution layer.
 "Tokenization is one of the key technologies that will reshape the paradigm of the asset management industry," said Young Hwan Kim, President and Head of the Global Business Division at Mirae Asset Global Investments. "Through our collaboration with Ondo Finance, we aim to position our global ETF lineup for the next generation of capital market infrastructure and deliver more efficient investment solutions by leveraging blockchain technology within existing regulatory frameworks."
 "Mirae Asset is a top-tier global asset manager that launches and manages a diversified lineup of ETF products through its overseas subsidiaries," said Ian De Bode, CEO of Ondo Finance. "Mirae Asset's decision to bring its ETFs onchain through Ondo Stocks demonstrates that not only retail but also institutional investors are now entering the tokenization space in earnest."
 Since launching in September 2025, Ondo Stocks has become the largest tokenized stocks platform globally, with over $1 billion in total value locked, 260+ tokenized securities, and more than $20 billion in cumulative trading volume.
