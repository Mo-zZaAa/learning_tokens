Ondo partners with Binance to bring tokenised securities to a global retail base

26 Feb 2026 | International

Vikramaditya Shrivastava

Initially, only ten Ondo tokenised securities will be made available, including Apple, Google, Tesla, Nvidia, Nasdaq 100, Circle, Meta, Amazon, S&P 500 and Microsoft.

New York-based Ondo Global Markets (Ondo) has partnered with leading cryptocurrency exchange Binance to offer tokenised US securities on the Binance platform, which will be accessible via Binance Alpha. Initially, only ten Ondo tokenised securities will be made available, including Apple, Google, Tesla, Nvidia, Circle, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, as well as two index-tracking exchange-traded funds (ETFs) - the Invesco QQQ Trust, tracking Nasdaq 100 (QQQ) and the State Street SPDR S&P 500 ETF T
