Ondo Finance Acquires Oasis Pro to Accelerate Tokenized Securities
Ondo Finance has acquired Oasis Pro, an SEC-registered broker-dealer, alternative trading system (ATS), and transfer agent, to provide a comprehensive US regulatory infrastructure for tokenized securities. The deal combines Ondo's institutional tokenization technology with Oasis Pro's licenses and team to expand regulated tokenized securities offerings and enable broader market access.
Oasis Health Partners Acquires Premier Health
Oasis Health Partners has acquired Premier Health, a healthcare administrative services organization focused on revenue cycle management and practice operations, to expand Oasis’s independent primary care platform. The combined company aims to modernize primary care by integrating clinical, operational, and financial infrastructure—particularly to support value-based care while keeping physicians independent.
Ondo Finance has acquired blockchain development firm Strangelove to expand its engineering and product capabilities for building full-stack tokenized real-world asset (RWA) infrastructure. The deal brings Strangelove's team and expertise in secure, modular blockchain infrastructure and validator operations into Ondo to accelerate its omnichain asset issuance and forthcoming blockchain launch.
ORO Labs, the enterprise-grade procurement orchestration platform, has acquired ProcureTech, a procurement-focused digital accelerator. The acquisition brings ProcureTech’s ~20 procurement experts and partner network into ORO to strengthen EMEA operations, accelerate global growth, and expand orchestration and AI-led procurement capabilities.
Elmwood Agency Acquires Oasis Group & Associates
Rochester-based Elmwood Agency has acquired Buffalo-based Oasis Group & Associates, LLC and moved its headquarters to Pittsford, New York. The acquisition expands Elmwood's footprint in the Rochester and Buffalo markets and broadens its product offerings across personal, commercial and financial/benefits lines.
INX Signs Term Sheet to Acquire Openfinance Securities LLC
INX Limited has signed a term sheet to acquire Openfinance Securities LLC, a FINRA/SIPC-registered U.S. broker-dealer and alternative trading system (ATS) based in Chicago, subject to definitive agreements and regulatory approvals. The deal would transfer Openfinance's broker-dealer and ATS business — including systems, digital asset listings, client relationships and licenses — to INX as it expands its regulated digital-asset trading capabilities.
SigmaTel Acquires Oasis Semiconductor for $82M
SigmaTel Inc. acquired Oasis Semiconductor Inc., a Waltham, Massachusetts-based fabless designer of semiconductor solutions for the multi-function printer market, in a transaction valued at up to $82 million (approximately $57 million in cash up front plus up to $25 million in earn-out). The acquisition gives SigmaTel entry into the printer system controller business and expands its product portfolio beyond digital audio and video chips.
