Ondo Global Markets Reaches $1 Billion in Tokenized Stocks and ETFs

By Patricia Miller

May 13, 2026

2 min read

Ondo Global Markets achieved $1 billion in value locked in tokenized stocks, making it the fastest in onchain finance. Here’s what to know.

#What milestone has Ondo Global Markets achieved?

Ondo Global Markets recently reached a significant milestone, achieving $1 billion in total value locked across its tokenized stocks and ETFs in under eight months. This rapid growth positions Ondo as the fastest platform to hit this figure in the onchain finance sector, a field that includes established stablecoins.

#What are the key numbers behind this achievement?

The current statistics reveal that Ondo Global Markets commands over 70% of the market share in tokenized equities. With more than 260 live assets available, the platform covers diverse sectors such as artificial intelligence, biotechnology, defense, and energy. Additionally, the cumulative trading volume has surpassed an impressive $18 billion, demonstrating strong market engagement. Ondo operates across prominent blockchain networks including Solana, BNB Chain, and Ethereum. Recently, it has also integrated with Hyperliquid's HyperEVM through the Ondo Bridge, facilitating seamless transactions of tokenized stocks and ETFs within Hyperliquid's trading infrastructure.

#Why are tokenized stocks becoming increasingly popular?

There are several reasons for the rising interest in tokenized stocks, particularly on platforms like Ondo. These stocks provide continuous trading opportunities, unrestricted by the traditional hours of 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern Time that characterize conventional U.S. stock exchanges. Moreover, they allow for fractional ownership, which opens doors for small investors, especially in emerging markets where expensive shares could otherwise be out of reach.

Institutional interest has also played a pivotal role in driving growth. Recent favorable regulatory changes have made large investors more open to the concept of tokenized real-world assets, encouraging the development of this financial innovation.

#What does this mean for investors?

The cumulative trading volume of $18 billion relative to the $1 billion total value locked is noteworthy. This volume-to-TVL ratio of approximately 18 times suggests a high velocity of asset trading on the platform, indicating that the tokens are actively traded rather than stashed away.

However, as with any emerging financial market, regulatory risks loom large. Tokenized stocks exist in a legislative gray area in various regions. Therefore, while monitoring Ondo's impressive growth metrics, it remains crucial for investors to keep an eye on the regulatory landscape monitored by the SEC and equivalent bodies internationally. The regulatory environment will ultimately determine whether tokenized equities can maintain their rapid growth trajectory or face significant limitations.
