Key findings
Revenue follows crypto trading.
Q3 2026 total revenue $1.22bn, (35.0%); transaction revenue $590.2m, (43.6%), as consumer spot volume fell 59.2% to $23.4bn. Operating margin 5.1% against 25.7%.
Share gains in a smaller market.
Crypto trading volume market share 10.7% against 5.6% a year earlier; crypto derivatives volume $963bn, +14.1%; prediction markets above $200m of annualized revenue.
Stablecoins hold up.
Stablecoin revenue $306.9m, (5.4%): +$84.6m from higher USDC balances, ($52.8m) from lower rates. Subscription and services reached 49.1% of net revenue against 40.6%.
Costs cut twice.
Total operating expenses $1.15bn, (16.9%); employees 4,204 against 4,988 in Q1 2026 after the May 2026 restructuring; FY2026 Adjusted Expenses outlook lowered to $4.15bn to $4.30bn.
Net income follows crypto marks.
Q3 2026 net income $718.7m includes a $715.0m gain on crypto assets held for investment, against operating income of $61.7m; 9M 2026 net loss ($34.9m).
Shares closed at $143.54 on 30 Oct 2026
, (10.6%) after results; 6.4x EV/Sales TTM. Three year model: base case $119, best $349, worst $14.
Figures are read from the Forms 10-K for FY2022, FY2024 and FY2025, the Forms 10-Q for Q1 to Q3 2026, the Forms 8-K with all their exhibits, incl. the quarterly shareholder letters and earnings decks (Ex 99.1) with the CFO commentary and outlook sections, the Texas plan of conversion, certificate of formation and bylaws (Ex 2.1, 3.1, 3.2), four supplemental indentures for the convertible notes (Ex 4.1 to 4.4), three legal opinions (Ex 5.1 to 5.3) and two officer advisor agreements (Ex 10.1), the proxy statement of 2026 and the Form 8-K Item 5.07 vote results, Schedules 13G and Forms 3, 4 and 5 on the record for central index key 1679788, together with the comparable filings of Robinhood, CME Group, Intercontinental Exchange, Circle, Galaxy Digital, Bullish, Gemini and Bakkt used in chapters 2 to 4 and 7. 1 Revenue & Business Model
Storyline: revenue follows crypto trading activity, while the mix moves toward stablecoins and services.
Transaction revenue was 50.9% of Q3 2026 net revenue against 59.4% in Q3 2025; subscription and services rose to 49.1% from 40.6%. Chapters 2 to 4 cover the three revenue drivers: trading, stablecoins and USDC, and staking, lending and subscriptions.
Business:
Coinbase runs four exchanges (Coinbase Exchange, Coinbase International Exchange, Coinbase Derivatives Exchange and Deribit), a consumer app and custody. It reports one segment and three revenue types: transaction revenue (consumer, institutional, other), subscription and services (stablecoin revenue, blockchain rewards, interest and finance fee income, other) and other revenue (corporate interest and other income).
FY2025 revenue $7.18bn, +9.4%:
transaction $4.06bn (56.5%), subscription and services $2.83bn (39.4%), other $297.9m. Revenue peaked at $7.84bn in FY2021 and fell to $3.19bn in FY2022; TTM Q3 2026 $5.63bn, (26.6%).
Regions:
United States $6.01bn of FY2025 revenue (84%), international $1.17bn; Q3 2026 United States $1.02bn, international $199.5m.
Employees 4,204 at 30 Sep 2026
, against 4,795 a year earlier and 4,988 at 31 Mar 2026.
Bases:
annual revenue by type is shown as filed in each Form 10-K. From Q1 2026 Coinbase moved revenue on corporate USDC balances from subscription and services (stablecoin revenue) into other revenue and recast the 2025 quarters; the FY2025 Form 10-K was not recast. Quarterly series in this report use the recast basis.

2 Trading: Spot Volumes Fall, Derivatives and Prediction Markets Grow
Transaction revenue $590.2m in Q3 2026, (43.6%), 50.9% of net revenue.
Consumer $450.1m, (46.6%); institutional $91.7m, (32.1%); other $48.5m.
Volumes:
consumer spot $23.4bn, (59.2%); institutional spot $111.1bn, (53.1%); crypto derivatives $963.0bn, +14.1%; Coinbase Total Trading Volume $1.21tn, (2.8%).
Market share 10.7% of total crypto trading volume
, the fourth quarterly gain in a row, against 5.6% in Q3 2025; crypto derivatives volume TTM $4.34tn against $3.24tn a year earlier.
Prediction markets
, launched in the US with initial market flow from Kalshi (Q4 2025 letter), passed $200m of annualized revenue in Q3 2026 with 82% quarterly compound growth; with derivatives they were about 25% of transaction revenue (Q3 2026 call).
Derivatives venues:
Deribit (closed Aug 2025) and Coinbase International Exchange were combined into one exchange in Oct 2026; the advanced trading platform relaunches as Coinbase Pro in Q4 2026 with spot, futures, perpetuals, options and equities.

3 Stablecoins & USDC: Record Balances, Lower Rates
Stablecoin revenue $306.9m in Q3 2026, (5.4%), 26.4% of net revenue;
the 10-Q attributes +$84.6m to higher USDC balances and ($52.8m) to lower rates.
Average USDC held in Coinbase products $22bn, +49% (deck)
, about 30% of average USDC market capitalization of $73bn, against $68bn a year earlier.
Where the revenue comes from (deck):
USDC in Coinbase products $202m against $159m, of which corporate balances $21m against $30m, now in other revenue; off platform USDC $126m against $196m. Other revenue $55.1m, (47.9%).
USDC rewards paid to customers $129.2m (+16%)
, in sales and marketing, equal to 42.1% of stablecoin revenue.
Circle arrangement renewed on the same terms in 2026;
Coinbase says it captured about 50% of USDC economics over the past year. Payment stablecoins of $2.42bn sit in cash and cash equivalents.

How stablecoins are changing
Market size:
stablecoin market capitalization $304bn at the end of Q3 2026; Coinbase cites the US Treasury Secretary's Nov 2025 remarks expecting $3tn by 2030 (8-K Ex 99.1).

against 40.6%; the non stablecoin lines below total $263.3m against $392.1m.
Blockchain rewards $85.7m, (53.6%):
($111.8m) from lower Solana and Ethereum prices and ($18.9m) from lower reward rates, partly offset by higher staked balances (10-Q).

average Borrow/Lend balances $1.87bn against $610m a year earlier.
Other subscription and services $109.0m, (23.6%)
, incl. Coinbase One; paid Coinbase One subscribers reached an all time high, +24% (no count disclosed; nearly 1m in the Q4 2025 letter).
Assets on platform $376.6bn at 30 Sep 2026
, (27.0%), 12.5% of total crypto market capitalization; Bitcoin $252.5bn of it. Coinbase is custodian for over 80% of US Bitcoin and Ether ETF assets (Q4 2025 letter).
