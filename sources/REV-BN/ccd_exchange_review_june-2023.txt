July 5, 2023 Report Summary In June, the combined spot and derivatives trading volume on centralised exchanges rose 14.2% to $2.71tn, the first monthly increase in trading volume since March. Key Takeaways In June, spot trading volume on centralised exchanges rose 16.4% to $575bn, recording the first increase in three months. Binance’s spot trading market share has declined for the fourth consecutive month, falling to 41.6% in June. This is the lowest market share for the exchange since August 2022 Coinbase currently represents 61% of the Bitcoin trading volumes among exchanges registered in the US and is on track to increase its market share in the region for the third consecutive month (as of July 4th). Derivatives trading volume on centralised exchanges rose 13.7% to $2.13tn in June, the first increase in derivatives volume in three months.
