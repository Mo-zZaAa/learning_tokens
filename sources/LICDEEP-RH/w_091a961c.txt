 Marex North America accounts to transfer to Marex Capital Markets Inc.
 As of Friday July 14, 2023, all accounts carried by Marex North America (“MNA”) transferred to Marex Capital Markets Inc. (“MCM”), an affiliate under common control with MNA.
 The aim of this move is to consolidate all of Marex’s US futures activity into a single entity – MCM. MCM is a registered broker-dealer as well as a futures commission merchant, which means clients will have greater access to a broader choice of product offerings.
 Our customers will continue to deal with the same people using the same emails, addresses and phone numbers as before.
 Should you have any questions regarding this matter, please contact us at +1 312 795 7770 or mnaclientservices@marex.com .
