Kraken Top Rated and Most Secure

The Bitcoin trading news website TheBlogChain performed an extensive independent review of the industry’s top exchanges. Kraken scored an “A” rating, and received the highest rankings in the industry for legitimacy, customer service, and particularly for security.

In summary, the review stated, “Kraken is an exemplary institution that the rest of the Bitcoin world should look up to. On top of being one of the first exchanges to pass an independent audit, they’ve consistently shown themselves to be leaders on security practices, particularly in the realm of security services offered to users.”
