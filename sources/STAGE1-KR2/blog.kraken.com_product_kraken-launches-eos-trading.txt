Kraken Launches EOS Trading

Kraken is pleased to announce support for EOS trading! The EOS platform is designed to scale the mainstream development of decentralized applications for large businesses, and claims to the first platform to do so.

Which EOS Pairs are Available For Trading?

EOS-Ethereum (EOS/ETH)

EOS-Bitcoin (EOS/XBT)

EOS-U.S. Dollar (EOS/USD)

EOS-Euro (EOS/EUR)

How Do I Deposit EOS Into My Kraken Account?

Create an EOS deposit address by going to Funding > Deposit > EOS and click “Generate New Address,” then send your deposit to this address.
