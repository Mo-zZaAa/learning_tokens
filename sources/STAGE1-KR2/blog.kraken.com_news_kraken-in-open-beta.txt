Kraken in Open Beta

After nearly two years of intensive development by a highly skilled engineering team, the Kraken website enters open beta testing today. Users are able to create accounts, get funded with play money, and try out the new system. The open beta creates a lot of excitement, eliciting comments from users such as, “This interface is great. Best in the business,” “Shut up and take my fees,” and “Looks amazing, great work, very promising.”
