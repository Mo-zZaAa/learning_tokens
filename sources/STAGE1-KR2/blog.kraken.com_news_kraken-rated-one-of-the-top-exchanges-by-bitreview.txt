Kraken Rated One of the Top Exchanges by BitReview

Kraken is rated one of the top bitcoin exchanges by BitReview. BitReview is a popular site containing a wealth of bitcoin news and information, including extensive reviews of bitcoin-related goods and services. 

“Kraken is one of the best products in the market, period. For European traders, Kraken is hands down the best bitcoin exchange option,” according to BitReview. “The trading platform, which is arguably the best of all the bitcoin exchanges, is number one in terms of BTC/EUR trading volume. Kraken has recently slashed trading fees, catapulting what was already a competitive structure into top two or three.”
