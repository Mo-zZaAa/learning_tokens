MtGox Update: You can still file your creditor claim through Kraken and receive $1MM in free trades!

If you haven’t filed our MtGox claim yet, it’s not too late! The MtGox trustee has announced that the deadline for filing creditor claims online has been extended to 8:00pm, July 28, 2015 PDT (GMT-7). Kraken has also extended the offer of up to $1MM in free trades to creditors filing their claim through kraken.com. You can file your claim and get your free trades in two steps:

Create a free account at https://www.kraken.com.

Click the “MtGox Claim” tab in your account and follow the instructions.

To learn more about the free trade offer and other benefits of filing your claim through Kraken, see our previous blog post.
