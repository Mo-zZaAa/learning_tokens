Trade Volume Doubles Previous Record (Again!)

Over 12,200 bitcoins traded on Kraken today, marking a new all time daily high. The new high almost doubles the previous high that came on October 4th, 2014, when a volume of over 6,400 coins was reached. We’re very pleased with how fast Kraken continues to grow!
