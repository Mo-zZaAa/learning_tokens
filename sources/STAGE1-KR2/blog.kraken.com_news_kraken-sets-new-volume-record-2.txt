Kraken Sets New Volume Record

About 17,400 bitcoins traded on Kraken today, setting a new all-time daily record! The record comes only one day after the record was broken with a daily volume of about 14,500 bitcoins. With our two-year anniversary coming up, it’s great to see this pattern of steady growth!
