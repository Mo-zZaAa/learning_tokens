Introducing the Kraken iOS App

We are very excited to announce the launch of the Kraken iOS app! Read on for screenshots and a quick tour!

Account: Add your Kraken API key to view your balances and open orders at a glance. We recommend that you create a special API key just for your iOS device and only give it the following permissions: 1) query funds, and 2) query open orders and trades. Those are the only permissions the app needs. After you create the API key, you can scan the available QR code with your iOS device to enter the API information.

Markets: Market data and candlestick charts (15m, 1h, 1d). Last price, 24 hour high/low, today’s open/close, 24 hour volume, 24 hour volume weighted average price, today’s volume weighted average price (since 00:00:00 UTC). 

Pair selection: Pressing the currency pair brings up a scrolling menu for changing the currency pair selection. 

Conversion calculator: Pressing the icon in the upper right of the screen brings up a currency conversion calculator for the selected pair. Press the screen to toggle the conversion.

Alerts: Read the latest Bitcoin news.

Download the Kraken iOS app: click here!

Trading is not yet supported. If you would like to trade with the app, let us know! Also, let us know what other features you’d like to see on the app. We always appreciate feedback in order to provide the best exchange experience. To send us feedback, go to https://support.kraken.com and click “Submit a request.” Choose “Feature Request” for the category of your request. Thank you!
