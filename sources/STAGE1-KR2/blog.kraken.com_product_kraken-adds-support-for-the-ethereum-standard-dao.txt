Kraken adds support for the Ethereum Standard DAO Framework

You can now access the full power of the Ethereum network from Kraken’s platform! We have added a data field for ether withdrawal addresses that is capable of interacting directly with the Ethereum blockchain, including existing DAOs (Decentralized Autonomous Organizations) and existing smart contracts. Support for deposits from Ethereum contracts is coming soon as well!
