QR Codes Added for Bitcoin Deposits

Depositing bitcoins (and other digital currencies) just got even more convenient. Deposit addresses can now be displayed as QR-codes for fast, easy, and reliable integration with wallet clients on mobile devices. To generate the QR code, simply click the “QR Code” button next to the address you want to deposit to.
