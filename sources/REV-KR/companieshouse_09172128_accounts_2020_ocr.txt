For the Year Ended 31 December 2020

Principal risks and uncertainties (continued)
Credit Risk

The company is not subject to any significant credit risks. The company does not extend credit to any of the
clients on its trading platform and does not intend to change this aspect of its business model. The company has
no plans to extend into lines of business that would alter its credit risk profile. Trade debtors comprise a minimal
proportion of its balance sheet and this is not forecast to change in a significant manner. The company's main
banking facilities are with BW Bank of Germany, a bank that enjoys strong ratings from Moodys (Long-term
Bank Deposits Aa3, Short-term rating P-1)

Financial key performance indicators

For the year ended 31 December 2020, the Company key performance indicators ("KPI") include, but are not
limited to trading volume and revenue. These metrics provide indicators as to the performance of the Company
and its ability to retain and grow its client base.

The Company turnover increased from £6.2 million to £7.4 million in the year 2020, an increase of 18%, driven
by significant enhancements to the trading platform and the gaining of regulatory authorisation to operate as a
Multilateral Trading Facility (MTF) from the Financial Conduct Authority, the firm's primary regulator. Trading
volume doubled 100% in the year 2020.

Future developments and COVID-19

Stakeholder Engagement
The company manages its engagements with its key stakeholders in the following ways:
Clients

The company’s clients are the users of its trading platform, and the board is focused on delivering a high-quality
user experience. This is achieved through collecting client feedback and monitoring the wider industry to inform
decisions about adding new features and’ other improvements to the platform. Client engagement is achieved
through community building, direct client outreach and building meaningful relationships with individual clients. In

. respect of the company’s target market, the company’s long-term plans are to expand its institutional investors
member base since it is now operating a Multilateral Trading Facility (MTF). as well as ensuring that individual
investors can access the company's services through user-friendly and trusted channels.
