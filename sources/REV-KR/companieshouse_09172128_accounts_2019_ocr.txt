Crypto Facilities Ltd

Strategic Report
For the Year Ended 31 December 2019

Introduction

The directors present their strategic report together with the audited financial statements for the year ended 31
December 2019.

Business review

Crypto Facilities Ltd, (the “Company”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Crypto Research Ltd (The “Parent’), a
company registered in England and Wales. The company operates a platform which allows participants to trade
in Futures based on the price of cryptocurrency. The company has one core regulated business, enabling
customers to trade in digital asset Futures.

On 4 February 2019, the company initiated a client “introducing arrangement” with Payward Inc, (its ultimate
parent company), a United States (US) corporation, to enable single sign- on (“SSO”) for its client to trade
futures with Crypto Facilities. The company’s client base grew as a result of the arrangement, increasing 2019
revenue significantly from 2018.

The company derives revenue from trading fees charged to users for the matched transactions of the notional
value of derivatives contracts conducted on its exchange platform. This fee was 7.5 BPs of matched notional
volume for liquidity taking orders and approximately minus 3.65 BPs (fee rebate and trade discount) of matched
notional volume for liquidity providing orders and trade discount.

As of 31 December 2019, the company had just under £7 million of cash on hand.
Principal risks and uncertainties
Personnel and thi

Digital Assets and cryptocurrencies are supported by digital networks, operated through protocols established
and governed by consensus that are implemented through computer software. They are therefore exposed to
risks due to fraud, technological glitches, malware, and hackers. The loss of access to its private keys or other
data loss could adversely impact the company's reputation, business, financial condition, and operations. The
company employs strict measures and controls in ensuring that customer data and assets are safeguarded.
Furthermore, the company has on-going mechanisms to monitor platform activity and reconciliation of
transactions against platform data.

Market Risk

The primary activity of the company is the provision of a trading platform that allows its users to transact in
cryptocurrency derivative contracts. The company generates trading revenues through the charging of fees to
the clients of the platform each time they execute a transaction, and these fees are in the form of cryptocurrency.
The company's trading revenues are therefore subject to significant cryptocurrency market risk.

Liquidity Risk
The company is unlikely to encounter liquidity risks in the short to medium term. The Firm holds substantial liquid
cash reserves that exceed its typical annual cash out-flows (cash balances in 2020 are £9.4 million and expected

to rise to £14 million in 2021). It therefore does not depend on the liquidation of illiquid assets to satisfy its
liabilities.

Credit Risk

The company is not subject to any significant credit risks. The company does not extend credit to any of the
clients of its trading platform and does not intend to change this aspect of its business model. The company has
no plans to extend into lines of business that would alter its credit risk profile. Trade debtors comprise a minimal
proportion of its balance sheet and this is not forecast to change in a significant manner. The company's main
banking facilities are with BW Bank of Germany, a bank that enjoys strong ratings from Moodys (Long-term
Bank Deposits Aa3, Short-term rating P-1).

Financial key performance indicators

For the year ended 31 December 2019, the company key performance indicators (“KPI”) include but are not
limited to total registered clients, trading volume and turnover. These metrics provide indicators as to the
performance of the SSO partnership and the Company's ability to retain and grow its client base.

In the year ended 2019, the company registered users grew by 53k, a year on year increase of 572%.

The company turnover increased from just over £1 million to £6.3 million in the year 2019, an increase of 509%,
largely driven by the SSO arrangement and enhancements to the trading platform. Trading volume grew
approximately by 343% in 2019.

Future developments and COVID-19
The company continues to enhance its trading platform in order to attract and retain customers.

Key elements of the company’s strategy include but are not limited to the following:

. Continuous enhancement of the trading platform

. Customer retention and growth

. Providing customers with the option to trade on the platform by depositing legal tender

The current global events surrounding COVID19 present an unprecedented situation. Whilst prices of traditional
asset classes experienced their worst weekly returns in over a decade, and crypto assets also witnessed price
reduction mid-March, (the bitcoin price fell from $8,300 to $5,300 between 13 and 19 March 2020), the bitcoin
price has now stabilised between $6,000 and $7,000. We expect prices to continue to fluctuate which in return
drives volume and attracts investors to trade crypto assets. We currently do not see a systematic negative
impact of COVID19 on our business model since trading volumes and revenues have increased since the
outbreak of COVID19 (average daily trading volumes: $51m in January, $79m February and $75m in March
2020). In addition, the strength of our balance sheet (cash reserves at February 2020 £7.3M from £6.9M in
December 2019) suggests that the firm is well placed to absorb any unforeseen negative future impact of
COVID19.
