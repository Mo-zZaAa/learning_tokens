Robinhood Presents: To Catch a Token
Robinhood to unveil next-generation crypto products from keynote event in Cannes, France
Mark your calendars—Robinhood is hosting Robinhood Presents: To Catch a Token, a keynote event happening on Monday, June 30, live from the French Riviera.
Hosted by Chairman and CEO Vlad Tenev, Robinhood’s first-ever international and crypto-focused event will usher in a bold new chapter—not just for Robinhood, but for crypto. Be among the first to hear about what’s next for crypto at Robinhood.
The keynote will begin at 5:00pm CEST / 11:00am ET and will be livestreamed in the Robinhood app, as well as on X, YouTube, and at go.robinhood.com/presents. 
To celebrate, starting June 17 and running through July 7, Robinhood Crypto customers will have access to exclusive new promotions rolling out each week across the U.S. and Europe. To kick things off:
In the U.S., customers who deposit cash to their individual investing account between June 17 and July 7 will be entered into a sweepstakes to win one of five Bitcoin.¹ Each winner will receive 1 Bitcoin, and every eligible dollar deposited counts as one entry.
In Europe, eligible customers who deposit crypto will receive a 1% match in the same crypto²
To learn more about the event, visit go.robinhood.com/presents. 
For the latest updates on Robinhood Crypto, follow us on @RobinhoodApp and @RobinhoodApp_EU.
