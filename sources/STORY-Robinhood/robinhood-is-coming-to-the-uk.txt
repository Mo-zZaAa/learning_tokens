Robinhood Is Coming to the UK
Invest in thousands of stocks commission-free
In 2013, Robinhood set out on a mission to democratize the financial system. We pioneered commission-free investing in the US to enable everyone to participate in the markets. Since then, over six million Americans have discovered Robinhood.
Today, we’re excited to continue our journey and introduce the Robinhood investing platform to the UK. With Robinhood, you can invest in thousands of US and global stocks, commission-free. There are no foreign exchange fees and no account minimums. Sign up today to get early access when we launch in early 2020.
Get Early Access
Invest commission-free in a few taps
Invest commission-free in some of the world’s most popular companies, including Nike, Amazon, and Apple. Choose from over 3,500 US stocks and over 1,000 global stocks, including Burberry, BMW, Barclays. Learn more about Robinhood UK.
Get started with confidence
Learn to navigate the markets and stay on top of your investments with a variety of free features. Get access to business news articles and videos from trusted sources, including The Wall Street Journal, Reuters, and CNN Business. View analyst ratings, earnings snapshots, candlestick charts, and stay in the loop with price movement alerts, watchlists, and more.
Upgrade to Robinhood Gold for premium features including Level II market data, in-depth research reports, and more advanced tools such as margin investing for experienced investors.
Fund your account in an instant
Top up your account with your debit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, and withdraw your cash just as easily. We use an independent mid-market exchange rate without fees or markups.
Regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority
Robinhood U.K. Ltd is authorized and regulated by the FCA, the conduct regulator for financial services firms and financial markets in the UK, and we use industry standards to ensure your account is safely secured.
Protected by SIPC
Your securities and cash are held by Robinhood Securities, LLC. Robinhood Securities is a member of the Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC), which protects securities customers of its members up to $500,000 (including $250,000 for claims for cash). Explanatory brochure available upon request or at www.sipc.org. Learn more about how you’re protected. Robinhood will be available in the UK in early 2020. Sign up to be one of the first to get access.
