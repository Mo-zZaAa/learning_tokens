Robinhood Presents: The World is Flat
We’re bringing new technologies to the old world.
On Wednesday, July 1, Robinhood will transport you back to the days of exploration, at Robinhood Presents: The World is Flat, live from the historic Old Royal Naval College.
Hosted by Vlad Tenev, Chairman & CEO, and Johann Kerbrat, SVP, GM of Crypto & International, our event keynote will unveil our new suite of products that chart a new course beyond traditional finance and introduce new ways to interact with the global markets. Join us as we share the next step in our international journey and explore entirely new possibilities beyond what traditional finance can offer.
The livestream begins at 7 p.m. BST / 2 p.m. ET in the Robinhood app, as well as on X, YouTube, and at robinhood.com/presents.
To learn more, visit robinhood.com/presents. For the latest updates on Robinhood Crypto, follow us on @RobinhoodApp and @RobinhoodApp_EU.
Robinhood Presents: The World is Flat is sponsored by Robinhood Markets, Inc.
