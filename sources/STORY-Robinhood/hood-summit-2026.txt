Robinhood Puts the Power of Hedge Funds in Every Trader's Pocket 
Robinhood introduces Robinhood Agents, perpetual futures, earnings contracts, and more, plus weekend equities trading coming early next year 
Tonight, live from HOOD Summit 2026 at the George R. Brown Convention Center in Houston, we unveiled our most ambitious lineup of active trading products yet. We announced Robinhood Agents, a fully embedded AI experience that can analyze the market, build strategies, and trade on your behalf around the clock, and Agent Apps, which give your Robinhood Agents another layer of intelligence with institutional-grade data and specialized tools from leading third party providers. 
We also announced plans to launch 24/7 weekend trading for equities; perpetual futures for eligible U.S. customers; earnings contracts for key company metrics; and more. Together, this is Robinhood’s boldest step forward in our mission to become the #1 place for active traders.
"Ownership doesn’t work without markets, and markets don’t work without traders," said Robinhood CEO Vlad Tenev. "We're making Robinhood the best place in the world for active traders by delivering tools once reserved for hedge funds, big banks, and quant firms."
Robinhood Agents, Right in the App
In May, we launched agentic trading, giving tech-savvy customers a way to connect their own third-party AI agents to Robinhood, safely and autonomously. Since launch, over 150,000 customers have opened agentic trading accounts and now, agents use Robinhood’s tools almost 30 million times a day. 
Today, the future of agentic finance is going mainstream with Robinhood Agents. The experience now lives right inside the Robinhood app, meaning anyone, regardless of their familiarity with AI, can easily build their own agents to help them develop powerful strategies, run detailed research, or trade automatically.
“With safety and security at its core, we're setting the standard for what agentic finance can be,” said Abhishek Fatehpuria, VP of Product Management at Robinhood. “Whether you're an expert or are just getting started with AI, you're at a disadvantage if you're not using Robinhood.”
Setup is simple: give your agent a name, open a dedicated agentic account, choose a model from several leading AI labs including OpenAI, and you're ready to go–with your agent trading within the strategy and limits you set. Plus, usage on OpenAI GPT-Luna will be free until the end of the year.
From there, you can:
Create and execute a strategy: Chat with your agent, analyze the market in seconds, and make trades, all without ever leaving the Robinhood app. The Robinhood Trading MCP we launched this summer is still there for customers who want to connect their own third-party agents. Robinhood Agents is a new option built right into the app, so no linking or setup is required – we take care of that for you. 
Manage your safety controls: Your agent can only access the funds in your dedicated agentic trading account. You can choose to manually approve each trade, which is a setting shown during setup and defaults to on, and it is adjustable at any time.*  
Set and run it with Loops: Coming soon, we’ll launch Loops, which allow you to turn a strategy into a standing, ongoing instruction for your agent to execute on repeat, around the clock. For example, you can set a Loop to check the market every morning and execute a trade when certain conditions are met, or run a continuous overnight strategy to look for opportunities while you sleep. As always, you’re in control – you can turn off a Loop whenever you’d like.

Trade Equities, Even on Weekends
The stock market never sleeps, and neither should your portfolio. Coming soon, pending regulatory review, Robinhood is expanding its trading hours to let customers trade a selection of U.S. equities 24/7, including weekends. No more watching a headline break on Saturday and waiting until Monday to act. New weekend trading is an extension of the Robinhood 24 Hour Market, which first launched in 2023 and enables customers to trade hundreds of stocks and ETFs from Sunday at 8pm ET through Friday at 8pm ET. 
“Breaking news doesn’t wait for an opening bell. With the upcoming launch of weekend trading, we’re making sure our customers won’t have to either, no matter what day it is,” said Steve Quirk, Robinhood Chief Brokerage Officer.
Weekend trading will start with a curated list of stocks and ETFs across all account types. Weekend trading is powered by Bruce ATS, an institutional-grade alternative trading system that offers strong liquidity, resiliency and execution quality.
“We’re about to change when the world can trade,” said Jason Wallach, CEO, Bruce Markets. “Soon 24/7 trading won’t be a dream, it will be the new global standard. Working alongside Robinhood, we will be giving traders the freedom to trade when they want, not when Wall Street decides.”
New Assets Are Coming to Robinhood
We're expanding the types of trades you can make on Robinhood with the launch of perpetual futures and earnings contracts. 
Perpetual Futures: In the coming months, eligible U.S. customers will be able to trade perpetual futures right inside the Robinhood app. You’ll be able to take long or short positions on BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, ADA, LINK, or HYPE with no expiration dates, and up to 10x leverage on BTC and ETH perpetuals (3x leverage on everything else). Offered by Robinhood Derivatives through Bitstamp, through the end of the year we’ll charge just one basis point per trade (0.01%), which are some of the lowest fees in the industry. You can set stop-loss and take-profit orders, track your liquidation price in real time, and get alerts if a position is at risk. 
Earnings Contracts: Ahead of Q3 earnings season, we’re launching new earnings contracts in our prediction markets hub. They’re binary options contracts made available through Cboe that allow you to trade on company-specific KPIs like whether a company beats earnings, hits a revenue target, or crosses a key financial milestone. Earnings contracts will require options approval and will begin rolling out to eligible customers in the coming weeks. Cboe will not charge fees on earnings contracts through the end of the year, subject to regulatory review. Robinhood will not charge any contract fees through the end of the year. 
The Next Chapter for Robinhood Social
Robinhood Social launched in beta earlier this year, and tonight it’s going live to all eligible customers in the U.S. We’re also making several major updates to the experience, including: new post types for sharing crypto and stock charts, portfolio allocations, and profit-and-loss; and coming in time for Q3 earnings season, a new live earnings experience, which lets customers watch earnings calls and trade in real time as they happen. 
Coming soon, verified market commentators from other websites, news, and company profiles will bring breaking market news directly into the app so you can stay on top of major events from the companies you care about. Robinhood Social is also coming soon as a Legend widget, putting your trading community right alongside your active trading hub. 
To learn more about Robinhood Social and get started visit robinhood.com/social. 
Even More Tools, Built for How You Trade
To cap things off, we rolled out several other notable upgrades as we make the trading experience even more powerful for active traders:
Extended options hours: Starting in October, options trading will begin at 7:30am and run until 4:15pm, a 35% increase in trading hours across Robinhood’s most popular symbols. 
One-cancels-the-other (OCO) orders: With OCO orders you can set a take-profit and a stop-loss on the same position so that when one executes, the other cancels automatically. OCOs are launching for equities in October, with options support following early next year.
4x intraday margin: Beginning next month, eligible margin customers will be able to opt-in to trade with up to 4x buying power during market hours, up from 2x today. Built-in safeguards include push notifications ahead of market close, plus a one-time notification directly in-app the first time you sign up, so you're never caught off guard.
Foldables and tablet support: In time for the launch of Apple’s iPhone Duo, we’re rolling out new versions of the Robinhood App for foldable iOS and Android phones, as well as iPad and Android tablets.
Robinhood Legend, leveled up: Available now for customers, you can run your third party agents natively inside Legend via our Robinhood Trading MCP. And coming later this year, you’ll be able to build a custom indicator to flag unusual volume, mark support levels ahead of a pullback, or scan the market for breakouts, all without switching windows. 
Performance enhancements with Helios: Over the past year, we’ve rebuilt parts of the engine that powers Robinhood. We’re calling it Helios, and it means faster charts and clicks on Legend and predictive loading on mobile, so you can move across the app to get the latest information and make decisions faster than ever before.

Unlike equity and ETF options, index options have some unique features that should be understood before investing in them. This includes, but is not limited to, settlement, exercise, expiration, tax, and cost characteristics.
Binary KPI options such as earnings options are exchange-listed, cash-settled option contracts listed on an underlying KPI of an issuer. A call binary KPI option pays out if the settlement value of the underlying KPI is at or above the exercise price at expiration. A put binary KPI option pays out if the settlement value is below the exercise price at expiration.
The settlement value is determined not by the market price of the issuer's stock, but by whether a specific financial or operating metric reported by the issuer in an earnings-related filing submitted to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission meets the applicable condition.
Each contract has a maximum payout of $1.00. Binary KPI options have a multiplier of 1 rather than the standard equity option contracts multiplier of 100.
