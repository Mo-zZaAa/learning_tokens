 Whole Portfolios, Single Tokens 
 Building and maintaining a portfolio takes time, from selecting assets and setting allocations to rebalancing as markets move. Ondo Intelligent Portfolios simplify that process, giving investors exposure to a professionally designed investment strategy through one token without having to buy each asset, set target weights, and manually rebalance positions themselves. 
 Ondo intelligent Portfolios work in three layers: 
 
 Portfolio Strategy: Ondo or a third party supplies a portfolio allocation or strategy. 
 Programmatic Implementation: The underlying positions are Ondo Stocks, tokenized equities and ETFs backed by real securities and connected to traditional market liquidity. Allocation, rebalancing, and fee logic are encoded into smart contracts, with rebalancing following preset rules on a defined schedule. 
 Single-Token Holding Mechanics: Investors gain exposure to the entire portfolio through a single token they can hold in their own wallets without having to to manage or rebalance each constituent position. 
 
 Seven Portfolios Spanning Income, Growth, and Thematic Exposure 
 The seven portfolios give Solana investors a range of approaches to choose from, whether they are seeking income, broader growth exposure, or a focused allocation to themes such as AI. Three of the seven Ondo Intelligent Portfolios are based on portfolio strategies developed by BlackRock, the world’s largest asset manager, for use by Ondo. Investors can hold a portfolio individually or combine multiple portfolios to build their own allocation. The full lineup includes: 
 Income Portfolios 
 
 BLKHIon: Ondo High Income Powered by BlackRock 
 YLD5on: Ondo 5 Targeted Income TR Portfolio 
 YLD8on: Ondo 8 Targeted Income TR Portfolio 
 
 Growth Portfolios 
 
 BLKGRWon: Ondo High Growth Powered by BlackRock 
 BLKDIGon: Ondo Diversified Growth Powered by BlackRock 
 
 Thematic Portfolios 
 
 MAG7Xon: Ondo Magnificent 7 and Crypto Portfolio 
 BRAINon: Ondo AI Leaders Portfolio 
 
 The Best of DeFi and TradFi, Combined 
 
 Ondo Intelligent Portfolios bring the best of TradFi and DeFi to investors in one token: 
 
 Simple. A professionally designed portfolio in a single token. 
 Precise. Exposure to a defined portfolio, underpinned by tokenized assets. 
 Transparent. Portfolio holdings, weights, and rebalances are visible onchain. 
 Flexible. Transferable peer-to-peer and composable with DeFi applications that support them. 
 Accessible. Available to eligible investors in permitted jurisdictions, with tokens transferable 24/7. 
 Efficient. Programmatic rebalancing reduces the work involved in maintaining a portfolio.
For developers, this creates another building block for financial applications. A single token representing a curated portfolio could serve as collateral in a supporting lending market, be incorporated into a savings product, or form part of another investment strategy.
