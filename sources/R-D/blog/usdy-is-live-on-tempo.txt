 Bringing Yield to the Payments Layer 
 Hundreds of trillions in value moves across global payment rails annually, and at any given moment a meaningful share of it sits idle inside payment flows. Payroll waits to be disbursed, merchant balances await settlement, and remittance corridors are pre-funded in advance. The money is always moving, but between movements it earns nothing.  Today, that changes for businesses on Tempo. Value moving through Tempo can now earn Treasury-backed yield in a wallet, a treasury, or a payment flow. 
 Purpose-Built Chain for Global Payments 
 Tempo&#x27;s architecture is purpose-built for: moving value at global scale. It provides core infrastructure for stablecoin settlement, commerce, and cross-border money movement. 
 Key design choices: 
 
 Sub-second finality: Payments settle in under a second, so transfers confirm at the speed users expect from modern payment apps, not the speed of legacy rails. 
 Stablecoin-denominated fees: There&#x27;s no volatile gas token. Fees are denominated in USD stablecoins and stay low and predictable, so businesses can price payment flows with certainty. 
 Throughput built for payment scale: Dedicated payment lanes reserve blockspace for payment transactions, so payroll runs and disbursements execute predictably even when other network activity spikes. 
 
 Permissionless Access to Tokenized Treasuries 
 What makes USDY a natural fit for a payments network is that it works like the rest of the payments stack: it&#x27;s permissionless. Unlike most tokenized Treasury products, which restrict transfers to whitelisted addresses and require every holder to onboard directly with the issuer, USDY is freely transferable onchain. Any eligible non-US business, or protocol can hold it, send it, and receive it, with no approval process standing between a payment and its recipient. 
 For a payments chain, this distinction is everything. An allowlisted asset can&#x27;t flow through payroll rails, merchant checkout, or remittance corridors, because every counterparty would need pre-clearance. In contrast, USDY can. Builders on Tempo can integrate USDY and support peer-to-peer transfers the same way they can for stablecoins, except the asset also earns US Treasury-backed yield. 
 What this means for builders: 
 A yield primitive for payments applications: Builders on Tempo can integrate USDY as a yield-bearing balance layer in wallets, neobank products, payroll rails, and merchant treasury tools. 
 
 Secure and productive collateral: With institutional-grade backing, USDY can serve as reliable collateral in lending, trading, and liquidity-provision contexts. 
 Seamless integration: Tempo builders can quickly and easily integrate USDY as productive collateral, treasury assets, or yield primitives into their applications. 
 Dedicated liquidity: Dedicated market maker liquidity supports 1:1 exchange between USDY and stablecoins on Tempo. 
 
 “Bringing USDY to Tempo puts institutional-grade yield directly into the payments layer. Every business transacting on Tempo can now hold dollars that earn between transactions, backed by short-term U.S. Treasuries. This is another key step toward establishing USDY as a core primitive of onchain finance,” said Ondo Finance’s Ian De Bode. 
 “Global payment platforms already hold balances that sit idle between transactions. Launching Ondo&#x27;s USDY on Tempo enables them to put those balances to work,&quot; said Eric Kang, GTM at Tempo. &quot;Tempo Earn gives platforms control over how idle balances are used, and makes it simple to build rewards into existing products.”

 This launch marks a key milestone in USDY&#x27;s multichain expansion, delivering yield-bearing dollar exposure with deep liquidity and seamless composability on a chain purpose-built for global payments and further embedding USDY as a core primitive of onchain finance.
