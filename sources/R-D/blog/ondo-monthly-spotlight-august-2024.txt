 🎂 Celebrating One Year of USDY 
 
 One year ago, we launched USDY, the world’s first permissionless, yield-bearing tokenized note secured by US Treasuries. In that time, it has grown to become the world’s leading permissionless yieldcoin with over $380m in TVL. The USDY ecosystem has expanded to over 70 projects across 8 blockchains: Ethereum, Solana, Arbitrum, Aptos, Sui, Mantle, Mantra, and Cosmos via Noble. For many of these chains, Ondo’s USDY was the first native tokenized real-world asset and introduced new use cases and utility for users and developers in these ecosystems. Continue Reading. 
 💙 USDY Goes Live on Arbitrum   
 
 USDY is now available natively on Arbitrum for users and developers. This integration brings our tokenized US Treasuries product to one of the largest DeFi ecosystems, continuing Ondo’s momentum of expanding the utility and access of real-world assets onchain. USDY is available in Arbitrum’s leading DeFi protocols, including Camelot and Dolomite. Continue Reading. 
 💼 Mark Janoff Joins Ondo Finance as General Counsel 
 
 We’re excited to have announced that Mark Janoff has joined Ondo Finance as our new General Counsel! Ondo has developed a robust reputation for its institutional-grade products, with investor protection, safety, transparency, and legal frameworks at the forefront. Mark brings extensive experience in blockchain-related legal work, making him uniquely positioned to provide valuable insights into real-world asset tokenization and the nuances of investor protection. Following his appointment as General Counsel at Ondo Finance, we sat down with him to discuss his perspectives. Continue Reading . 
 🌌 Case Study: Bringing Utility to Payments with USDY 
 
 In December of 2023, Ondo launched USDY on the Solana blockchain, offering users access to native USDY through a growing array of dApps in the Solana ecosystem. Since then, crypto payment platforms in the ecosystem have enabled merchants to accept payments with and settle in yield-bearing assets like USDY, offering new utility to merchants and their users. Continue Reading . 
 💸 Ondo Finance Applies to Ethena Reserve Fund 
 
 Following Arbitrum DAO’s Stable Treasury Endowment Program Committee’s recommendation to diversify 6M ARB from the Arbitrum DAO Treasury into USDY, Ondo Finance has submitted applications for USDY and OUSG’s inclusion in the Ethena Reserve Fund .
