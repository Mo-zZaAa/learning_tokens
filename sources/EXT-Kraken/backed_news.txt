The Future Is xStocks: Upgrading bTokens
xStocks are the next evolution of bTokens — same underlying exposure, now with greater liquidity, adoption, and onchain utility.
Backed set out to bring traditional assets onchain, borderless, permissionless, open to everyone. That vision is now a reality. With over 100 tokenized stocks and ETFs, xStocks have become the leading tokenized stocks product in the market, with billions in combined exchange and onchain volume since launch.
As part of Payward, Backed is consolidating its product line. bTokens, the original product that started it all, are being upgraded to xStocks. The bTokens product line will be gradually wound down by the end of 2026.
Why Upgrade
bTokens and xStocks share the same issuer, prospectus, collateral structure, and investor protections. Both are fully collateralized 1:1 by the underlying asset. If you hold bTSLA, you can swap it to TSLAx. Same underlying, same exposure, same rights.
xStocks build on this foundation with greater utility and adoption: capital market liquidity during the week, onchain price discovery on weekends, DeFi integrations across SVM and EVM including lending and yield, and access through major exchanges. Over 100,000 unique onchain holders and $25 billion in total transaction volume in under eight months confirm the demand.
How to Upgrade
Onboarded clients
 can swap bTokens for the corresponding xStocks directly with the issuer, or redeem bTokens to stablecoin using the standard redemption process.
bToken holders
 can access onchain liquidity to swap from bTokens to xStocks on supported decentralized venues such as CoW Swap.
bTokens will remain redeemable throughout the wind-down period. We encourage all bToken holders to upgrade ahead of the end-of-2026 deadline. For support, contact 
[email protected]
ETF Mapping
For equities, the upgrade is 1:1. For example, bTSLA to TSLAx, bNVDA to NVDAx, bAAPL to AAPLx, and so on.
For ETFs, xStocks offers an updated selection with similar investment exposure. The exchange ratio will be determined by the issuer.
