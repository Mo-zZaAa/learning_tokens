xStocks Brings the DAX 40 Onchain, Opening Germany's Largest Listed Companies to 24/7 Global Trading
November 11, 2026
Tokenized shares of all 40 DAX constituents arrive as part of an xStocks expansion into UK and EU-listed equities, building on Payward's strategic partnership with Deutsche Börse Group
ST. HELIER, JERSEY – NOVEMBER 11, 2026 – xStocks, the tokenized equities framework developed by Payward, has deployed tokenized equities representing every constituent of the DAX 40, Germany's benchmark stock index. The launch puts the largest blue-chip companies on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, operated by Payward's strategic partner Deutsche Börse Group, onchain and within reach of investors across more than 110 markets.
The DAX40 tokenized equities will be immediately available on 360X, a company of Deutsche Börse Group, as well as Xetra-Gold as xStocks. Opening up onchain distribution for Europe’s most traded gold ETC.
Each xStock is backed 1:1 by the underlying share, held by a regulated custodian, and tradeable 24/7 across select venues. Tokenized versions of SAP (SAPx), Siemens (SIEx), Allianz (ALV.DEx) and Deutsche Telekom (DTE.DEx) and others are among the names now available from day one. For many investors around the world, this expansion is the first practical way to gain exposure to German capital markets. It also gives the companies themselves a new line of distribution into a global pool of onchain investors, whose demand feeds back to the underlying shares.
"German companies have a track record of building innovative products which the whole world relies on, yet there are investors on every continent who have been restricted from investing in these companies," said Mark Greenberg, Payward’s Chief Commercial Officer. "Bringing the DAX 40 fully onchain through our work with Deutsche Börse Group breaks that barrier, allowing someone in Southeast Asia or Latin America to finally hold tokenized Siemens or Allianz from the same wallet they already use, and trade whenever they like, including the hours when Frankfurt is asleep."
“Our partnership with Payward is exactly the kind of collaboration that accelerates the transformation of capital markets,” said Carlo Koelzer, Head of Digital Asset and FX of Deutsche Börse Group. “Our vision is to provide a unified hybrid exchange ecosystem - delivering seamless trading on- and off-chain across traditional and tokenized assets, bridging deep centralized liquidity with interoperable settlement.”
Deutsche Börse Group and Payward have strategically partnered on several initiatives dating back to December 2025 as part of a broader agreement to combine regulated infrastructure with crypto-native expertise to deliver frictionless institutional access. xStocks already trade on 360X, the Deutsche Börse-backed venue regulated under the EU's DLT Pilot Regime since February 2026.
xStocks launched in June 2025 initially as tokenized representations of US stocks and ETFs, proving that permissionless, onchain and 24/7 markets could unlock global distribution channels for assets to be held, transacted and deployed across DeFi from across the world. The framework has since grown to span more than 1,300 xStocks, representing tokenized stocks, ETFs and early access to IPOs, the widest range of assets offered by any tokenized equities framework. It has also since grown to include over $1 billion in assets under management, with more than $45 billion of total transaction volume, and more than 500,000 unique holders worldwide.
About xStocks
xStocks is the industry benchmark for tokenized real world assets, bringing publicly listed equities and other assets onchain through fully collateralized, 1:1-backed tokens. Powered by Payward's digital asset infrastructure, xStocks places traditional assets on blockchain rails, expanding access to global capital markets with extended availability, global reach, and digital-native settlement. Starting with tokenized US equities, xStocks now spans markets across the US, Europe and Asia.
Designed for interoperability, xStocks move seamlessly between centralized exchanges, self-custodied wallets, and onchain applications, unlocking new utility across trading, collateralization, and decentralized finance. Since launching in June 2025, xStocks has grown to power billions of dollars in transaction volume across multiple blockchain ecosystems, anchoring a rapidly expanding global network shaping the future of tokenized markets.

About Deutsche Börse Group
As an international exchange organization and innovative market infrastructure provider, Deutsche Börse Group ensures that capital markets are fair, transparent, reliable, and stable. With its wide range of products, services, and technologies, the Group organizes safe and efficient markets for sustainable economies.
Its business areas cover the entire financial market transaction process chain. This includes the provision of indices, data, software, SaaS, and analytical solutions, as well as admission, trading, and clearing. Additionally, it comprises services for funds, the settlement and custody of financial instruments, and the management of collateral and liquidity. As a technology company, the Group develops state-of-the-art IT solutions and offers IT systems worldwide.
With over 16,000 employees, the Group is headquartered in the financial center of Frankfurt/Rhine-Main and has a strong global presence in locations such as Luxembourg, Prague, Cork, London, Copenhagen, New York, Chicago, Hong Kong, Singapore, Beijing, Tokyo, and Sydney.
