CEX Volume Falls to the Lowest Level Since November 2023
In July, combined spot and derivatives trading volumes on centralized exchanges fell 23.9% to $3.76T, marking the lowest monthly volumes since November 2023.
Spot DEX trading volumes declined 9.82% in July to $176B, their lowest level since September 2024.
In July, spot trading volume on Binance fell 22.4% to $196B. Despite this decline, its spot market share rose to 26.9%, the highest level since January.
