Binance Withdraws MiCA Crypto License Application in Greece – Will Apply in Another EU Country

by Bill Giannopoulos
0 0 0

Binance has officially pulled its application for a Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) license in Greece, citing delays in the approval process.

The world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange announced on Wednesday that after months of working with the Hellenic Capital Market Commission (HCMC), it has decided to withdraw its application and seek licensing in another European Union member state.

Binance’s Statement

“We made this decision after careful consideration of the status and the timeline of the process in Greece, with our users’ interests at the center.”

The company emphasised that it had engaged with Greek authorities “in good faith,” but no formal decision was made despite the looming end of the MiCA transition period.

Binance reassured users that it remains committed to the European market long-term and will continue its efforts to obtain a MiCA license elsewhere in the EU. The next country of application has not yet been announced.
