Dinari raises US12.7M Series A to make it easy for anyone, anywhere, to access and invest in the U.S. stock market Markup 1177
Southeast Asia

2025.05.08

500 Global Team


Equities for Everyone

  • U.S. equities represent over $40 trillion in value and are among the most desired financial instruments worldwide. Yet for billions of investors outside the U.S., access remains limited. 
  • 500-backed tokenized asset company Dinari is solving that with their blockchain technology, letting non-U.S. investors directly access American equities, cutting out the friction of complex onboarding flows, high transaction and compliance costs, fragmented infrastructure, and inconsistent or unavailable brokerage services. 
  • The team just raised US$12.7M in funding to expand its tokenized asset offerings, deepen integrations with institutional partners, and scale infrastructure to support increased global demand.
  • Global access is already live in 60+ countries across Latin America, Middle East & North Africa, Southeast Asia, and beyond. The majority of new Dinari users have come from Latin America over the past year, especially Argentina and Brazil.
