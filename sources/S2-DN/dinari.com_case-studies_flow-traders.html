Always-On Markets With Dinari's dShares™ and Flow Traders

By combining Flow Traders' global liquidity expertise with Dinari's dShares™, investors can now trade select U.S. equities 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, bringing always-on access to some of the world's most actively traded stocks and ETFs.

Overview

Global markets don't stop when the closing bell rings.

Major news breaks after hours. Companies announce earnings after the market closes. Geopolitical events unfold overnight. Crypto markets continue trading through weekends and holidays.

Yet U.S. equity markets still largely operate on a schedule designed decades ago.

Dinari and Flow Traders are helping change that.

Together, the companies have introduced 24/7 trading for select dShares™, bringing continuous access to some of the world's most actively traded U.S. equities and ETFs. By combining Dinari's regulated tokenized securities infrastructure with Flow Traders' global market making capabilities, investors can now trade around the clock without waiting for traditional market hours.

The Opportunity

Investor expectations have changed.

Digital assets introduced a world where markets never close, allowing investors to react to news and manage portfolios at any hour, from anywhere in the world.

Traditional equity markets, however, remain constrained by exchange hours, leaving investors unable to respond to important events during evenings, weekends, and holidays.

Creating continuous equity markets requires more than extending trading hours. It requires reliable liquidity, efficient price discovery, and infrastructure capable of supporting trading across every hour of the week.

That's where Flow Traders and Dinari saw an opportunity.

The Solution

Dinari partnered with Flow Traders, one of the world's leading liquidity providers, to bring institutional-quality liquidity to a growing selection of tokenized U.S. equities.

The initial rollout includes:

  • SPY
  • QQQ
  • META
  • AAPL
  • NVDA
  • TSLA
  • AMZN
  • MSTR
  • SPCX

During U.S. market hours, Dinari aggregates liquidity from traditional exchanges and alternative trading venues to provide best execution. Outside of those hours, Flow Traders helps provide continuous liquidity, extending access through evenings, weekends, and holidays.

For investors, the experience remains seamless. They simply place an order through a Dinari-powered platform while Dinari intelligently routes that order to the best available source of liquidity, regardless of the time of day.

Why It Matters

Twenty-four-hour trading isn't simply about convenience.

It changes who can participate in U.S. public markets and when they can participate.

For investors outside the United States, it reduces the need to trade in the middle of the night. For digital asset users accustomed to always-on markets, it brings equities closer to the experience they've come to expect. And for fintechs and exchanges integrating dShares™, it creates a more compelling product by extending access well beyond traditional market hours.

More broadly, continuous trading reflects a shift in how capital markets are evolving. As financial infrastructure becomes increasingly digital, investors expect markets to operate on their schedule rather than the other way around.

Looking Ahead

Twenty-four-hour trading is an important step toward a more accessible and globally connected financial system.

The initial rollout focuses on a curated group of highly traded equities and ETFs, with additional dShares™ expected to become available over time.

As tokenized securities continue to mature, continuous trading will become an increasingly important part of how investors access U.S. public markets. Through their partnership, Dinari and Flow Traders are helping lay the foundation for markets that are more responsive, more global, and better aligned with how investors already interact with digital assets.
