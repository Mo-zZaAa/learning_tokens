Branded staking
Offer staking directly within your product under your brand.
Auto-earn
Enable yield generation on eligible assets with a single API call. On by default, users opt out rather than opt in, maximizing user participation.

Configure auto-earn settings per asset and read current allocations and full historical reward data for display in your product.
Geo-aware eligibility
Discover which assets are auto-earn eligible by user and by country, so you only surface what is available to each user.

Staking managed with the same risk standards applied across the Payward Services platform.

Frequently asked questions
Do my users' assets stay liquid when they earn through Auto Earn?
Yes. With Auto Earn, Payward absorbs any on-chain bonding or unbonding periods, so allocated assets stay liquid and remain at the user's disposal at all times. You turn a yield source on or off for the user, and Payward chooses the assets and routes eligible balances. For the technical detail, see the Auto Earn documentation.
What is the difference between Auto Earn and Bonded Earn?
Auto Earn is a single switch per yield source: we pick the assets and route eligible balances for the user. Bonded Earn lets you choose a specific strategy and amount, and shows the lock-up periods that apply. Requests to move funds in or out are accepted first and settle later. See Bonded Earn.
How do fees and revenue work on staking rewards?
A fee is taken from the rewards a strategy pays, not from the user's principal. The displayed APY is gross, and rewarded amounts are net of the fee. The fee can be tiered by how much an account has allocated. Your commercial terms are agreed with Payward, so contact sales for pricing specific to your product.
How does staking work, and what waiting periods can apply?
Proof-of-stake networks pay rewards for using tokens to help secure the network. Some strategies require funds to stay allocated through a bonding period before they earn, and for ETH an exit queue (the network's waiting list) and an unbonding period before funds return. Bonded Earn shows these stages for each allocation so you can display them to users. See the Bonded Earn documentation.
How do I show users only the assets available in their country?
You can check which Auto Earn assets are eligible by country before a user has an account, and then by account. The account view shows the APY and the allocation cap for each eligible asset, so your product only surfaces what is available to that user. Availability varies by jurisdiction. See the Auto Earn documentation.
Can I show users the rewards they have earned?
Yes. You can read each user's historical rewards, which are net of any reward fee, as well as the rewards expected at the next payout and the date of that payout. This lets you display earnings in your own product. See the Auto Earn documentation for details.
