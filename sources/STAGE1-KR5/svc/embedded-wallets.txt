Frequently asked questions
What is the difference between embedded wallets and custody?
With Payward Embedded Wallets, your users hold their own keys, which are generated and used inside trusted execution environments, so you carry no custody burden on your books. If you'd rather we hold assets for your users, Payward Services Custody provides segregated custody for every asset we support. See coverage.
Which assets and networks can I offer with embedded wallets?
Embedded Wallets connect to onchain features across supported networks. Across Payward Services, stablecoins such as USDC and USDT are supported on several networks, alongside major cryptocurrencies and tokenized equities (xStocks). Coverage varies by product and is subject to regulatory requirements in each jurisdiction, so confirm your countries and assets with the Payward team. Details are in the coverage overview.
Can my users earn yield on the assets in their wallets?
Yes, where the asset and the user's country are eligible. Wallet balances can earn through our staking and yield products. See Staking & Yield.
Can we test an integration before going live?
Yes. You get a sandbox from day one. It's fully separate from production and uses test balances instead of real funds, so your team can build and run end-to-end flows before launch. Ask us how to test wallet-specific flows. When you're ready, we enable production access. See Environments.
