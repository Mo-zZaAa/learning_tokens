Key capabilities
Regulated benchmarks
CF Benchmarks is a UK FCA-authorized Benchmark Administrator, audited by KPMG for compliance with the Benchmarks Regulation. Regulated financial products can reference these indices with confidence in their methodology.
Real-time and reference rates
Live tick-by-tick pricing across major crypto pairs and select traditional asset classes, plus once-daily settlement-quality reference rates for end-of-day pricing and structured products.
Custom index construction
Single-asset, multi-asset, and thematic indices built to partner specifications, supporting ETF issuers, structured note providers, and onchain index products.
Track record at scale
Over $500B in CFTC and FCA-regulated futures contracts settled against CF Benchmarks rates, with $80B+ in assets carried through investment funds, ETFs, and other financial products.

Frequently asked questions
What is a crypto reference rate, and is it a price I can trade at?
A reference rate is a benchmark price used to value or settle a product, not a price you trade at. CF Benchmarks provides real-time rates and once-daily settlement-quality rates. Payward Services asset prices are for display only. When you trade, our swaps lock a quote for about two minutes. See Assets.
Can I pull historical price charts for crypto assets through Payward Services?
Yes, for every asset in the Payward Services catalogue. We provide historical price candles (open, high, low, close and volume) for charts and trend views, and the public price data needs no API credentials. This is Payward Services market data, separate from CF Benchmarks indices. See Assets.
