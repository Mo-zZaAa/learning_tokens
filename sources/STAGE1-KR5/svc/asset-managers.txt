Frequently asked questions
What is Payward Services, and is it an exchange or infrastructure?
Payward Services is infrastructure for businesses such as fintechs, banks, exchanges and asset managers. We give you the infrastructure that has powered Kraken for 15 years through one integration for trading, payments, tokenized assets, staking, lending, compliance and settlement, replacing a stack of separate vendors.
Is CF Benchmarks a regulated index provider?
Yes. CF Benchmarks is a UK FCA-authorized Benchmark Administrator, and it is audited by KPMG for compliance with the Benchmarks Regulation. Its rates are used across CME Group's Bitcoin and Ether futures and major ETFs, which gives regulated products a defensible reference price. See the Indices & Data page for details.
What is a crypto index, and can I get a custom one for my fund?
A crypto index tracks a defined basket of digital assets under a published methodology. CF Benchmarks builds single-asset, multi-asset or thematic indices to your specifications, and CF DACS classifies coins and tokens by the role they play for end users, helping you apply a consistent taxonomy across a portfolio.
Can we add staking to a fund strategy without running validators?
Yes. Payward Services manages the validator infrastructure and slashing risk, and you can offer staking under your own brand. Eligibility is geo-aware, so you can see which assets qualify by user and country, and you can read current allocations and reward history to show in your product.
