Frequently asked questions
What is Payward Services and what can an exchange add through it?
Payward Services gives you the infrastructure that has powered Kraken for 15 years. Instead of assembling several vendors, you use one integration for trading, payments, tokenized assets, staking, lending, compliance and settlement. An exchange can add derivatives, tokenized equities or yield on top of what it already runs. See our developer documentation.
How long does an integration take, and how do we test before going live?
Most partners go from contract signature to first transactions within three months. You build and test in a sandbox that's fully separate from production and uses test balances instead of real funds. When you're ready to launch, we enable production access. See Environments.
Are there minimum commitments or setup fees?
No. There's no setup fee, no implementation fee and no minimum volume. Other terms depend on your volumes and product mix, so get in touch through the form below to discuss them.
Is Payward Services regulated?
Yes. Payward Digital Solutions Ltd. is licensed by the Bermuda Monetary Authority to conduct digital asset business. Other Payward and Kraken entities hold their own authorisations, listed in our regulatory disclosures. The entity and products available to you depend on your country, so confirm them with our team.
How does identity verification work for our users?
We can verify your users for you through a hosted web or embedded flow. Verification runs in the background, so your platform waits for the result before the user can trade. Approved partners in the EEA can instead submit users their own provider has already verified. See Hosted KYC.
Can we offer staking and yield to our users?
Yes. Partners can offer two kinds of yield. With Auto Earn, you enable or disable yield across eligible assets for a user and show their allocations and historical rewards. With Bonded Earn, users allocate into strategies and you can follow each allocation through bonding and unbonding. Eligible assets and strategies depend on the user's country, so availability varies by region. See the Auto Earn guide.
How do our users get a price when they trade?
Swaps work like institutional FX-style trading. Your platform requests a quote that locks the price for about 2 minutes, executes against that locked quote, and then checks the trade until it reaches a final state. This gives users a firm price before they confirm. The swaps guide explains the full flow.
