Frequently asked questions
What can a neobank offer with Payward Services?
Partners can offer in-app crypto buying, selling and holding, portfolio views, recurring purchases, staking and earn, and tokenized stocks (xStocks). The partner controls the interface and brand, while Payward Services provides execution, custody, settlement and portfolio data. See the use cases guide for the full list.
How does revenue work for partners?
Partners earn on every transaction their users execute, from the first trade, with no setup fee, implementation fee or minimum volume. Specific revenue-share terms are not published, so talk to the Payward Services team through the contact form below for details.
Which assets and regions are supported?
Supported assets include major cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin and Ethereum, USD-pegged stablecoins such as USDC and USDT, and tokenized stocks (xStocks). Coverage varies by product and by jurisdiction and is subject to regulatory requirements, so confirm availability for your markets with your integration contact. See the coverage overview.
How do users get verified (KYC)?
We can verify your users for you through a hosted web or embedded flow, and a user is onboarded once the result comes back. Approved partners in the EEA can instead submit users their own provider has already verified. See Hosted KYC.
Can we test before going live?
Yes. You get a sandbox from day one. It's fully separate from production and uses test balances instead of real funds, so your team can build and run end-to-end flows before launch. When you're ready, we enable production access. See Environments.
