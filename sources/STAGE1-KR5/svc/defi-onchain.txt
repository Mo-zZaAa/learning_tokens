Frequently asked questions
How do DeFi protocols connect to fiat rails?
Through one Payward Services integration, instead of separate bridges, custodians and banking partners. Your users fund in fiat and settle onchain in USDC or USDT, or cash out to a bank account, card or wallet. You can combine these payment flows with trading in the same integration. See use cases.
What compliance options does a DeFi protocol have for regulated markets?
You can have us verify your users, or bring your own verification. With hosted verification, we collect identity evidence through a managed web or embedded flow. Approved partners in the EEA can instead submit users their own provider has already verified. Either way, wait for the result before a new user trades or converts. See Hosted KYC.
Does Payward cover the banking and funding rails my protocol's users need?
Your users can fund in fiat and settle in stablecoins such as USDC and USDT, which we support on several networks, including Ethereum, Solana, Polygon and Arbitrum. Which funding rails are available depends on the product and your users' countries, so your integration engineer confirms coverage for your markets. See coverage.
How can we test the integration before launching?
Yes. You get a sandbox from day one. It's fully separate from production and uses test balances instead of real funds, so your team can build and run end-to-end flows before launch. The sandbox includes test networks, so you can check the onchain flows your protocol depends on. When you're ready, we enable production access. See Environments.
