Frequently asked questions
Which derivatives products can my platform offer?
You can offer spot margin, crypto perpetual futures across 16+ markets, dated futures and prediction markets, all listed on Bitnomial Exchange, our CFTC-regulated venue. Regulated crypto options are coming soon. Availability depends on geography and terms. See Payward Services for exchanges.
Is Payward Services regulated for derivatives?
Yes. Derivatives run through three CFTC-registered entities: Bitnomial Exchange (a designated contract market), Bitnomial Clearinghouse (a derivatives clearing organization) and NinjaTrader Clearing (a futures commission merchant and NFA member). See our regulatory disclosures.
How long does it take to launch, and can I test first?
Most partners go live in 6 to 12 weeks through one REST and WebSocket API. You build and test in a sandbox with test balances first, then we enable production access when you're ready to launch. See Environments.
Do I need my own licenses or a compliance team?
No licenses of your own are needed. They're included: our CFTC-regulated exchange, clearinghouse and futures commission merchant provide the regulated layers, so you can launch under your own brand. Geo restrictions, terms and eligibility apply, so talk to us about your model.
