Zero Hash alternatives for embedded crypto trading

Trading

November 12, 2026

4 min read

By Payward Services editorial team

Fintechs and consumer apps that want crypto trading inside their product commonly compare white-label crypto-as-a-service providers, such as Paxos, Coinbase, Alpaca, Bakkt, BitGo and Anchorage Digital, alongside Payward Services. Payward Services Crypto Trading offers execution, custody and settlement through one API, while the partner controls the interface and branding. This guide sets out what to compare, the main types of provider, and where Payward Services fits.

What to compare in a Zero Hash alternative

Decide your answer to each criterion before you talk to providers. Provider details change, so confirm every point in writing with the vendor rather than relying on a summary.

  1. Regulatory coverage and who holds licenses. Establish which entity holds which licenses, in which markets, and which obligations remain with you.

  2. Custody. Ask who holds customer assets, how they are segregated and who carries the operational risk.

  3. Execution and liquidity. Ask where orders are executed, how prices are set and whether a markup sits between you and the source.

  4. KYC and onboarding. Check who verifies your users, and how much of the onboarding flow you build yourself.

  5. API quality, sandbox and webhooks. Review the documentation, confirm there is a sandbox separate from production, and check which events are delivered by webhook.

  6. Monetization. Understand the spreads and fees that apply, and what you can earn on your users' trading.

  7. White-label control. Decide how much of the interface and customer experience you need to own, and what the API lets you configure.

Types of provider

Providers in this space generally fall into a few groups. Many span more than one, so treat these as a way to frame your shortlist, not as a ranking.

  • Brokerage and stablecoin APIs. Provide an API for buying, selling and moving crypto or stablecoins, with the provider handling the back end.

  • Custody-first providers. Start from safekeeping of assets and add trading or settlement on top. Check how much of the trading experience you still need to build.

  • Stablecoin payments specialists. Focus on moving value with stablecoins. Confirm whether consumer trading is in scope.

  • On-ramp widgets. Offer a hosted purchase flow that you embed. They are quicker to add but give you less control over the experience.
