Frequently asked questions
What are xStocks tokenized equities?
xStocks are tokenized stocks and ETFs that trade onchain. They are tokens, which means they can be held and used in the same onchain environments as other digital assets. Payward Services offers tokenized equities for businesses that want to bring stocks and ETFs to their customers. xStocks also has its own dedicated platform, xstocks.fi. Access may be subject to eligibility requirements and jurisdictional restrictions.
How are xStocks backed?
Each xStock is fully collateralized 1:1 by the underlying share, which is held in regulated custody. In other words, every token is matched by the real share it represents. The 1:1 ratio means each token corresponds to one underlying share. Eligibility and availability of tokenized equities can vary by jurisdiction.
Can tokenized stocks be traded 24/7?
Yes. The markets are always on, with 24/7 trading, no closing bell and fast settlement. Fractional ownership is built in, so users are not limited to buying whole shares. These features apply to xStocks, which are tokenized stocks and ETFs that trade onchain. Access to tokenized equities may be subject to eligibility requirements and jurisdictional restrictions.
Can xStocks be used in DeFi?
Yes. Because xStocks are tokens, they plug directly into lending markets, AMMs, perpetuals and structured products. This makes them suited to onchain protocols building on tokenized equities. xStocks are tokenized stocks and ETFs that trade onchain. Learn more on the DeFi and onchain page of Payward Services.
