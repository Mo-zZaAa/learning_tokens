# Accept Bitcoin payments and pay merchants in dollars

> For payment processors: receive Bitcoin and deliver US dollars to a merchant’s own bank account.

> [!NOTE]
>
> This guide is coming soon. Explore the core concepts and primary APIs below.

## Core concepts

* [Users, accounts, and ownership](https://docs.services.payward.com/tabs/concepts/users-and-accounts.md): distinguish the merchant's Payward account from its bank account.
* [Moving money and exchanging assets](https://docs.services.payward.com/tabs/concepts/money-movement.md): understand how an on-demand conversion combines exchange and delivery.
* [Transaction lifecycles and settlement](https://docs.services.payward.com/tabs/concepts/transaction-lifecycles.md): distinguish receipt, conversion, and completed delivery.

## Primary APIs

* **Receive Bitcoin:** [List deposit methods](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/funds/list-deposit-methods.md), [List deposit addresses](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/funds/list-deposit-addresses.md), and [Claim deposit address](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/funds/claim-deposit-address.md).
* **Link the merchant's bank account:** [Create bank link](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/bank-links/create-bank-link.md) and [Get bank link](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/bank-links/get-bank-link.md).
* **Convert and deliver funds:** [Execute on-demand conversion](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/on-demand-conversions/execute-on-demand-conversion.md) and [Get on-demand conversion](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/on-demand-conversions/get-on-demand-conversion.md).
* **Check balances and activity:** [Get portfolio](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-portfolio.md) and [List portfolio transactions](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/portfolio/list-portfolio-transactions.md).
