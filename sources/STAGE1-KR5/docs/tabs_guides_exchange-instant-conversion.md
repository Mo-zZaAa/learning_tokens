## Core concepts

* [Assets, networks, and payment routes](https://docs.services.payward.com/tabs/concepts/assets-and-payment-routes.md): distinguish a deposit's asset, network, and address.
* [Moving money and exchanging assets](https://docs.services.payward.com/tabs/concepts/money-movement.md): distinguish market prices from executable quotes and account-funded trades from wallet-funded trades.
* [Transaction lifecycles and settlement](https://docs.services.payward.com/tabs/concepts/transaction-lifecycles.md): distinguish quote execution, trade settlement, and account history.

## Primary APIs

* **Receive deposits:** [List deposit methods](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/funds/list-deposit-methods.md), [List deposit addresses](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/funds/list-deposit-addresses.md), and [Claim deposit address](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/funds/claim-deposit-address.md).
* **Check limits and request a quote:** [Get quote limits](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/swaps/get-quote-limits.md) and [Create quote](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/swaps/create-quote.md).
* **Execute and track the trade:** [Execute quote](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/swaps/execute-quote.md) and [Get quote](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/swaps/get-quote.md).
* **Read balances and activity:** [Get portfolio](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-portfolio.md) and [List portfolio transactions](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/portfolio/list-portfolio-transactions.md).
* **Retrieve settlement reports:** [List settlement reports](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/reports/list-settlement-reports.md) and [Get settlement report](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/reports/get-settlement-report.md).
