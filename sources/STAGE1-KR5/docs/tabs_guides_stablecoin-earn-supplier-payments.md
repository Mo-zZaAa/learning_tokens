# Earn between supplier payments

> For business payments platforms: earn on eligible stablecoin balances and pay suppliers within your program.

> [!NOTE]
>
> This guide is coming soon. Explore the core concepts and primary APIs below.

## Core concepts

* [Verification, permissions, and eligibility](https://docs.services.payward.com/tabs/concepts/verification-and-eligibility.md): understand why earning availability depends on the account.
* [Moving money and exchanging assets](https://docs.services.payward.com/tabs/concepts/money-movement.md): distinguish transfers within your program from external payouts.
* [Transaction lifecycles and settlement](https://docs.services.payward.com/tabs/concepts/transaction-lifecycles.md): distinguish an accepted change from a completed operation.

## Primary APIs

* **Discover eligible assets:** [Get account Auto Earn assets](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/sandbox/auto-earn/get-account-auto-earn-assets.md).
* **Manage Auto Earn preferences:** [Get Auto Earn preferences](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/sandbox/auto-earn/get-auto-earn-preferences.md) and [Set Auto Earn preferences](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/sandbox/auto-earn/set-auto-earn-preferences.md).
* **Read earning balances and rewards:** [Get Auto Earn allocations](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/sandbox/auto-earn/get-auto-earn-allocations.md) and [List Auto Earn rewards](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/sandbox/auto-earn/list-auto-earn-rewards.md).
* **Check available funds and pay a supplier:** [Get portfolio](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/portfolio/get-portfolio.md) and [Create transfer](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/funds/create-transfer.md).
* **Reconcile payments and rewards:** [List portfolio transactions](https://docs.services.payward.com/api-reference/portfolio/list-portfolio-transactions.md).
