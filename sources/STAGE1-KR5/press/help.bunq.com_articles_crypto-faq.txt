Crypto FAQ
Trade crypto securely and easily directly in your bunq app. Start with just €1 and keep your assets safe with integrated security features.
Crypto FAQ
Trade crypto securely and easily directly in your bunq app. Start with just €1 and keep your assets safe with integrated security features.
In this article
Hey bunqers 🌈,
Skip the hassle of new platforms or confusing processes. Buy and sell top cryptocurrencies directly in your bunq app. It’s beginner-friendly, fully integrated, and powered by Kraken for peace of mind.
What can I do with crypto at bunq?
Buy: Easily purchase popular cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and many others;
Track: Monitor the value and performance of your crypto portfolio in real time, right from your app;
Swap: Instantly exchange one cryptocurrency for another, such as swapping Bitcoin for Ethereum;
Sell: You can easily sell your crypto and have the money securely transferred to your Bank Account.
What coins can I buy?
You’ll find top coins like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and over 350 others available in the Crypto tab.
Is there any risk when investing in crypto?
Yes, just like with any investment, crypto values can go up or down. That’s why we show clear info before every trade, so you can make informed decisions. bunq doesn’t provide financial advice. All trades are securely handled by our licensed partner, Kraken. More details about the risks involved in investing in cryptocurrencies can be found here.
What are the fees?
You pay a small trading fee, always shown in the app before you buy, sell, or swap:
bunq Free: 1.99%
bunq Core: 0.99%
bunq Pro: 0.49%
bunq Elite: 0.25%
The price you see in your bunq app before you confirm your trade already includes this fee and the spread — the gap between the buy price and the sell price. Learn more about the fees and spread in bunq Crypto.
Are trades instant?
Yes, your crypto appears right after your order is completed.
How do I get started?
Getting started with crypto is easy, and you can begin with just €1. Simply set up your personal crypto wallet by following these steps:
Go to the ₿ Crypto tab
Tap on “Get Started”
Complete the verification flow for Kraken
Your wallet is now ready ✅
Do I need to verify anything?
Yes, a quick identity check is required when opening your crypto wallet. It helps keep your account secure and ready to use.
You won’t need to go through verification again if you already completed it when creating your account.
However, there are some cases where we may need extra information. For example, this can happen if your Identification Document has expired. If anything is missing from your record, we’ll ask you to complete the verification process again.
Are my cryptoinvestments protected?
Your cryptocurrencies are safeguarded by Safety Shield, an automatic feature that temporarily holds any money you receive from selling crypto for 24 hours before it’s released to your Bank Account. This security measure provides you with extra time to detect and report any suspicious activity, ensuring that even if someone gains access to your account, they cannot immediately withdraw your funds.
Safety Shield is enabled by default for all users, and you’ll always see a note in your payment details if it’s active during a crypto sale. You can turn off Safety Shield anytime in the Crypto tab, but there’s a 24-hour delay before it’s fully disabled for your protection.
You can read more about Safety Shield here.
Can I sell my crypto whenever I want?
Yes, you can sell your crypto whenever you want with bunq. However, thanks to Safety Shield, any money you receive from a crypto sale will be held securely for 24 hours before it’s released to your Bank Account.
Which Bank Accounts can I use to buy and sell cryptocurrencies?
Currently, you can only buy and sell cryptocurrencies from your euro Bank Accounts. You won’t be able to make crypto purchases from your Foreign Currency Accounts or Savings Accounts.
Is there a minimum to start investing?
Yes, you can start investing with as little as €1. This means you can buy and sell your crypto for any amount above €1, or swap your crypto for any amount above $1. 
Keep in mind: Crypto prices can change quickly. This means your crypto position might temporarily drop below €1 right after you buy it. If this happens, you’ll need to wait for its value to go back up to at least €1 before you can sell it, or $1 before you can swap it. 
How is my crypto stored?
Your crypto is stored securely by Kraken, a licensed and regulated partner.
Can I set price alerts?
Yes, you can create custom price alerts and get notified when a coin reaches your chosen value. It’s easy to set up. Here’s how:
Go to the ₿ Crypto tab
Choose the crypto currency you want to be alerted for
Tap on the 🔔 in the top right corner
Select the price hit you want to be alerted for
Tap on Create Alert
How can I delete or edit a price alert?
You can change or remove your price alerts whenever you like. Here’s how:
Go to the ₿ Crypto tab
Tap on ⚙️ Settings
Select 🔔 Alerts
Select the alert you want to edit or delete
Adjust the sum or delete the alert
Where do I track my crypto?
You can track your crypto in the Crypto tab. There, you’ll see your entire portfolio, including real-time prices, performance over time, and all the coins you own, all in one convenient place.
How can I export a crypto statement? 
Follow these steps to download a statement with all your crypto transactions, and the balance at the beginning and end of the selected period
Go to your Profile 
Tap on Accounting 
Select Crypto under Statements 
Choose the dates and format
Tap on Export statement 
How can I share my Crypto trades with friends?
Your trades are always private by default. You can share your Crypto trades with your contacts in the bunq App by enabling trade sharing. Trade sharing is a fun and secure way to learn about investing together with your friends — you can learn more about it here. 
Here’s how you can share your Crypto trading:  
Go to your Stocks or Crypto Tab
Tap on ⚙️ Settings in the top right corner 
Toggle on for See Friends' Trades
All done!
When you turn on trade sharing: 
You’ll share your Stocks and Crypto trades with your contacts
You’ll see trades from your friends who have also chosen to share their trade activity. 
You can change your mind whenever you want. To turn trade sharing off, simply:
Go to the Crypto or Stocks tab.
Tap on ⚙️ Settings.
Toggle off for See Friends' Trades to switch sharing off.  
How is crypto taxed?
Crypto taxes depend on the tax laws in your country of residence. In some countries, you may need to report gains, losses, or even just owning crypto as part of your annual tax filing. We recommend checking with your local tax authority or a financial advisor to understand your specific obligations. More details can be found here.
Can I send my crypto to an external wallet?
No. You can’t send crypto from bunq to an external wallet, and you can’t receive crypto from one. Kraken holds your coins and their private keys, so your crypto stays in your bunq Crypto Wallet.
If you want to move the value of your crypto out of bunq, you can sell it:
Go to the ₿ Crypto tab
Tap 🟠 Sell
Choose the cryptocurrency and the amount
Choose the Bank Account you want the money to go to
Slide to sell
The money from your sale arrives securely in the euro Bank Account of your choice.
Who is Kraken?
Kraken is one of the world’s most trusted crypto platforms. As our licensed partner, they bring secure storage, fast execution, and deep liquidity—so you get simplicity and safety with every trade.
Where can I find updates or tips?
You can find updates, tips, and helpful information on our blog, the Together knowledge platform, and our social channels. It’s a great way to stay informed and get the most out of your bunq experience.
