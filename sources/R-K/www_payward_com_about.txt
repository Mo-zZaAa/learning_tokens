The parent company behind a portfolio built for modern markets
Payward, Inc. is a unified financial infrastructure platform that powers a family of products advancing an open, global financial system. Built on a single shared architecture, Payward enables customers to hold, trade, earn, pay, and invest across asset classes without friction or fragmentation.
At its core, Payward provides the infrastructure layer behind Kraken and a growing set of purpose-built products, including NinjaTrader, Breakout, xStocks, and CF Benchmarks.
Payward separates infrastructure from product expression. Each product surface is designed for a specific customer segment, regulatory regime, and use case, while operating on the same global foundation:
One global liquidity pool
One unified risk and margin engine
One collateral and settlement system
One compliance and licensing framework
This shared architecture allows Payward to scale efficiently, launch new products at low marginal cost, and serve diverse global markets while maintaining consistent risk management, regulatory integrity, and operational resilience.

From a pro trader exchange to a multi-asset platform on crypto rails
Kraken was built for professional traders as a high-performance exchange. Over time, that foundation evolved into a vertically integrated platform supporting both digital and traditional assets.
The founding focus
Performance, precision, and reliability
The earliest focus was building a high-performance exchange for professional traders—solving core pain points with 24/7 markets, instant settlement, on-demand leverage, and direct access to global counterparties through precise, intuitive interfaces.
The evolution
Vertical integration, maintaining rigor
The platform expanded from trading into a unified system spanning custody, yield, payments, and lending. Customers can trade, stake, hedge, and custody both digital and traditional assets through interfaces built for different workflows.
Platform vision
Infrastructure that connects crypto and traditional markets
The architecture runs nodes and pools liquidity across assets, geographies, and time zones—supporting an interoperable platform that connects crypto, tokenized assets, and traditional markets.
Key milestones
A concise timeline of foundational events and platform evolution.
2008
Bitcoin is introduced to the world with the release of the Bitcoin whitepaper.
2011
Jesse Powell is brought in to help Mt. Gox recover from a security attack and later co-founds Kraken in July 2011.
2014
Kraken becomes the first exchange to announce a verifiable cryptographic Proof of Reserves audit, and the first to provide complete Bitcoin market data to the Bloomberg Terminal.
2015
Kraken becomes the first exchange to offer both crypto spot and futures on one platform, and the first platform to list Ethereum’s native token (ETH).
2019
Kraken receives the first SPDI digital asset bank charter in Wyoming.
2020
Kraken becomes the first crypto facility to receive a UK Multilateral Trading Facility (MTF) license.
Today
Kraken accelerates its vision for a borderless, always-on trading environment, including:
The acquisition of NinjaTrader  (described as the largest TradFi + crypto deal to date)
Doubling the number of interfaces and products from 2024
Launching 11,000+ stocks and ETFs and introducing xStocks
Closing an $800M funding round led by Citadel, Jane Street, Oppenheimer, and Tribe Capital
