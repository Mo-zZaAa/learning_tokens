Binance's bStocks Become the Fastest Tokenized Equity Product to Surpass $1 Billion in Assets Under Management
          USA - English
                APAC - English
                USA - English
News provided by
                  Binance
Oct 15, 2026, 07:15 ET
Share this article
Share this article
More than $40 billion in cumulative trading volume, nearly 100 bStocks listed and $6 billion in 1:1 conversions as tokenized equities become an enduring on-chain market
ABU DHABI, UAE
, 
Oct. 15, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- Binance today announced that bStocks, its tokenized 1:1 U.S. securities, have become the fastest tokenized equity product to surpass $1 billion in assets under management (AUM), reaching the milestone roughly four months after launching on June 11, 2026 (per on-chain data from 
Dune Research
 as of October 15).
bStocks are tokenized representations of U.S.-listed stocks and ETFs, each fully backed 1:1 by the underlying security. They trade around the clock, settle on-chain, transfer between exchange and wallets, and can be converted 1:1 into direct stock holdings, all within a single Binance account. Each token fully redeems into the underlying security itself, not merely its cash value, keeping the token anchored to the stock it represents.
Key figures since launch: 
More than $1 billion in assets under management
, the fastest tokenized equity product to reach the $1 billion milestone, roughly four months after launch
More than $40 billion in cumulative trading volume
 since June 11, averaging over $300 million per day across the period.
Nearly 100 bStocks assets listed
, up from five at launch in June
$6 billion converted between direct stock positions and bStocks
 since launch, at 1:1 parity with no broker transfer and no market-hours wait.
"The test of a tokenized stock isn't the launch-day chart; it's whether it still behaves like a market after the launch. bStocks trade around the clock, get used as collateral, move between self-custodial wallets, and convert 1:1 into direct stock holdings. Today, more than a billion dollars of that is held on Binance, and it's being used, not just held,"
 said 
Shunyet Jan, Head of Exchange and Trading at Binance
.
How holders are using bStocks
Deployment in decentralized finance: In early October, bStocks utilization, measured by total value locked (TVL) deployment in DeFi, 
rose to 11.2%
, around 3.8 percentage points above the rate across comparable tokenized equities
Increasing capital efficiency: Outstanding borrowing against deposited bStocks collateral 
increased to 46.2% by September 10
, from 5.5% at end-June, an increase of 8.4-fold in the period
Adoption is additive: Data from end-July showed that 58.5% of early bStocks users 
also traded perpetual futures and/or direct equities
 on Binance
Growing demand for tokenization and 24/7 trading
Only 38% of Binance bStocks volume occurred during regular U.S. market hours in July, 
versus 89.2% for conventional U.S. equities
On-chain transfers of tokenized stocks 
exceeded $100 billion in Q3 2026
, more than 16x Q1's $6 billion, with bStocks accounting for over 90% of weekly transfer volume at the early-August peak
Tokenized equities became the fastest-growing real-world asset (RWA) category of 2026, with market cap more than 
quadrupling to over $3 billion
 by late September
Last month, Binance extended 
bStocks collateral support to all eligible users
, so retail traders can also use supported bStocks tokens as margin and futures collateral: borrowing quote assets to build exposure with up to 5x leverage, or holding the same token as both collateral and the hedge for a futures position. Where traditional venues generally only accept cash or cash-equivalent collateral, tokenized equities on Binance are now doing double duty as both market exposure and collateral, and Binance will continue expanding their utility from trading to hedging, margin, and risk management.
