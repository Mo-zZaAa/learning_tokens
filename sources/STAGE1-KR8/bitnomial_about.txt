 Mission 
 Building the Future of Markets.
 We're building the next-generation exchange and clearinghouse—dedicated to cutting-edge derivatives, innovative market structures, and breakthrough trading technology.
 We foster innovation while upholding trust and integrity through US CFTC regulation, delivering tools that meet today's needs and shape the future of finance.
 2014
 Founding
 On the FX desk of Allston Trading, Luke witnessed first-hand as the markets sought a safe haven from global economic uncertainty. Investors piled into the Swiss Franc causing its value to soar and impair Swiss exports. The Franc-Euro peg and subsequent unpeg caused even further economic turmoil. Bitnomial was founded to solve market dynamics and access problems.
 2020
 DCM Approval
 Bitnomial Exchange, LLC was designated as a contract market (DCM) by the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) to operate an exchange for margined and deliverable digital asset futures and options, a first for new exchanges.
 2021
 DCM & Physical Futures Launch
 Bitnomial Exchange, LLC launched their margined, physically settled bitcoin futures market.
 2022
 FCM Registration
 Bitnomial Clearing, LLC becomes a registered member of the National Futures Association (NFA) as a Futures Commission Merchant (FCM) for margined and deliverable digital asset futures and options.
 2023
 DCO Approval
 Bitnomial Clearinghouse, LLC was registered as a derivatives clearing organization (DCO) by the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) to operate a clearinghouse for margined and deliverable digital asset futures and options, becoming the first crypto-native exchange to be granted the full set of CFTC derivatives licenses.
 2025
 DCO & Perpetual Futures Launch
 Bitnomial Clearinghouse, LLC launched immediately supporting the first ever U.S. perpetual futures contract, replicating the full economic mechanics of offshore perps within the existing regulatory framework.
 2026
 Acquired by Payward
 Bitnomial was acquired by Payward, the parent company of Kraken, and continues to operate within the Payward group. The combination brings CFTC-regulated derivatives to eligible US clients across Kraken and NinjaTrader, including spot margin and perpetuals for retail clients.

 product
 Why Crypto-Settled Futures Matter
 Bitnomial Exchange is the only U.S.-regulated exchange offering futures and options that settle in crypto or 'physical settlement'. While most U.S. crypto derivatives settle in fiat, Bitnomial's contracts deliver actual digital assets at expiration. This distinction opens up real ownership, digital assets as margin, and trading strategies unavailable with cash-only futures.

 Bitnomial, LLC , headquartered in Chicago, is a derivatives exchange company that owns and operates U.S. CFTC-regulated exchange (DCM) , clearinghouse (DCO) , and clearing brokerage (FCM) subsidiaries. Bitnomial offers leveraged spot , perpetuals , futures , options , and prediction markets on a single unified exchange and clearinghouse with digital asset margin and settlement capabilities.
