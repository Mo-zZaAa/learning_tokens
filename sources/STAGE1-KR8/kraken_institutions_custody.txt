 Custody designed for institutions 
 Built to help institutions enforce governance, deploy capital efficiently and participate on-chain – all from secure, regulated custody.
 Qualified digital asset custody, with a difference
 Kraken Custody supports complex organizations with vault-level permissions, role-based approvals and policy enforcement. Scale securely with architecture that eliminates single points of failure and aligns custody operations with internal governance.
 Earn rewards without giving up control
 Put capital to work directly from custody through secure access to staking and off chain reward programs. Unlock value from held assets while maintaining full control, compliance and custody integrity.
 Seamless access to Kraken Prime
 Kraken Custody is fully integrated with Kraken Prime , enabling institutions to access deep liquidity, high-performance trading and flexible financing – directly from secure, regulated storage. Capital can be allocated and settled immediately from custody to support portfolio objectives, whether executing trades or accessing leverage all without compromising governance, speed or security.
