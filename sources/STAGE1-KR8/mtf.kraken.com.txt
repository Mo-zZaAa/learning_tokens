 Kraken MTF:
FCA-authorised crypto derivatives venue 
 One of the first firms to receive FCA authorisation, Kraken MTF has served institutional participants since 2019 with transparent, fair, and non-discriminatory access to crypto futures markets. 
 What’s available
 Perpetual and fixed-expiry futures contracts across over
250 crypto assets, available in two contract types:
 Linear (USD-margined) perpetuals
 Collateralised and settled in USD, with PnL denominated in dollars for straightforward portfolio management.
 Inverse (crypto-margined) perpetuals
 Inverse (crypto-margined) perpetuals — collateralised and settled in crypto, with no fiat conversion required.
 All instruments are traded against CF Benchmarks reference rates with transparent pricing and real-time mark-to-market.
 View contract specifications 
 Key features
 Low-latency API
 High-performance FIX and REST API connectivity designed for algorithmic and systematic trading strategies.
 Real-time margining
 Continuous portfolio margining with transparent risk calculations and automated liquidation processes.
 Competitive fees
 Volume-based fee schedule with maker rebates.
 Institutional custody
 Segregated collateral arrangements with robust safeguarding of client assets.
 Why Kraken MTF
 Regulated infrastructure Authorised and regulated by the FCA as a Multilateral Trading Facility. Transparent rulebook. Real oversight. No guesswork on counterparty risk.
 Institutional-grade execution Low-latency API connectivity, real-time margining, and deep order book liquidity. The engine behind Kraken’s derivatives markets, purpose-built for professional trading firms.
 Part of Kraken Backed by one of the world’s longest-standing and most trusted crypto platforms. Over a decade of operational track record, institutional partnerships, and a relentless focus on security.
 Who is it for
 Kraken MTF is designed for institutional and professional participants across the crypto derivatives ecosystem.
 Financial institutions
 Banks, asset managers, and investment firms seeking regulated crypto derivatives exposure.
 Distributors
 Retail brokers, CFD firms, and other intermediaries connecting their end clients to MTF-listed instruments.
 Proprietary trading firms 
 Systematic and discretionary desks trading their own capital.
 High-frequency and algorithmic traders
 Firms requiring low-latency API access and co-location options.
 Hedge funds
 Crypto-native and traditional funds executing derivatives strategies.
 Market makers
 Liquidity providers quoting continuous two-way pricing on the venue.
