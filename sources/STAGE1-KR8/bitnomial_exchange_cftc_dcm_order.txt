                                  ORDER OF DESIGNATION

       Bitnomial Exchange, LLC (“Bitnomial”) has submitted to the Commodity Futures

Trading Commission (“Commission”), pursuant to Section 5(a) of the Commodity Exchange Act

(“Act”), 7 U.S.C. § 7(a), and Commission Regulation 38.3(a), 17 C.F.R. § 38.3(a), an application

for designation as a contract market, which includes submissions and amendments filed

September 30, 2016 through February 20, 2020 and representations by Bitnomial in connection

thereto (the “Application”). Having reviewed the Application, the Commission makes the

following findings and rulings:

       WHEREAS the Commission staff reviewed the Application and conducted an onsite

technical evaluation of Bitnomial’s operational capabilities in order to evaluate whether

Bitnomial would be in a position, if the Application is granted, to comply with the core

principles and corresponding regulations in accordance with Section 5(d) of the Act, 7 U.S.C

7(d)(1).

       WHEREAS based on the review, Commission staff concludes that Bitnomial has

demonstrated in the Application and evaluation its ability to comply with the applicable

requirements of the Act and the Commission’s regulations for designation as a contract market.
       The Commission FINDS that Bitnomial has demonstrated, as required by Section 6(a) of

the Act, 7 U.S.C. § 8(a), and Regulation 38.3(a), 17 C.F.R. § 38.3(a), that Bitnomial (i) is able to

comply with the provisions set forth in the Act and the Commission’s regulations thereunder

applicable to designation as a contract market; and (ii) has provided sufficient assurance that it

will continue to comply with the requirements of the Act and the Commission’s regulations.

       Therefore:

       IT IS HEREBY ORDERED, pursuant to Sections 5 and 6(a) of the Act, that the

application of Bitnomial for designation as a contract market is approved, subject to the terms

and conditions specified herein:

       (1)     Bitnomial shall comply with all representations and submissions made by

Bitnomial in support of its Application for designation as a contract market, as shown in the

Application record; and

       (2)     Bitnomial shall comply with all provisions of the Act and all requirements set

forth in the Commission’s regulations, as may be amended or adopted from time to time, that are

applicable to designated contract markets.
