                1.0 Introduction
                London Stock Exchange 24 (LSE 24) is a new trading venue designed to extend access to London markets
                beyond traditional UK market hours. As investment activity becomes increasingly global, LSE 24 will give clients
                greater flexibility to respond to events across time zones and trade global exposures when relevant underlying
                markets are active. It will complement the core London Stock Exchange market, whose trading hours and closing
                process will remain unchanged.
                LSE 24 is being introduced through a phased Early Access programme ahead of production launch. Participants
                can test new functionality as it becomes available, assess their technical and operational readiness, and provide
                feedback to help shape subsequent releases. This gives clients the opportunity both to influence the development
                of the service and to prepare for live trading.
                The Appendix sets out the planned build up to the production go live.

                1.1 Purpose
                The purpose of this document is to provide participants with:

                •    A service overview of LSE 24
                •    A description of the LSE 24 functionality available in CDS from 19 October 2026


                LSE 24 runs on Millennium Exchange and, unless stated otherwise in this guide, behaves in the same way as the
                core London Stock Exchange market described in MIT201, the Business Parameters document and the
                associated technical specifications. This guide concentrates on where LSE 24 differs.
                This guide covers the CDS early access service only. Details of the production service – including final trading
                hours, market model, eligible instruments, membership and post-trade arrangements – will be communicated
                ahead of launch through London Stock Exchange notices and service announcements.

                2.2        Overview
                London Stock Exchange 24 (LSE 24) is a new trading venue designed to complement the London Stock Exchange
                core market which will operate outside London Stock Exchange core market hours.
                The venue has been developed to meet growing global demand for more flexible, real-time access to markets,
                enabling clients to trade London-listed products outside traditional UK market hours, respond to events across
                time zones and access liquidity when global markets and investor demand are active.
                Designed for a more connected, automated and digital market landscape, LSE 24 combines trusted exchange
                infrastructure with next-generation trading and connectivity capabilities, while preserving the integrity and
                operation of the core London Stock Exchange market.
                Direct trading access will be available to LSE’s eligible existing network of member firms, including brokers, banks,
                liquidity providers, market makers and specialist trading firms with technical and operational arrangements in place
                for trading directly on LSE. Investors would typically access LSE 24 through their member firm rather than
                connecting directly to the venue.



                2.3 Offered Securities1
                At launch, LSE 24 will support Exchange Traded Products (that have already been admitted to London Stock
                Exchange), including ETFs, ETCs and ETNs. ETPs are a natural starting point given their global investor base,
                broad underlying exposures and role in helping clients access international markets. While ETPs will be the first
                asset class available, the venue is being built to support broader participation across securities markets over time,
                with equities representing a natural next step, subject to client demand, operational readiness and regulatory
                approval.
                Participants can identify the instruments available on LSE 24 from the Instrument Directory messages published
                on the LSE 24 Group Ticker Plant channels.

                2.4 Timeline
                LSE 24 will be available for customer testing from the 19 th of October 2026. Subject to regulatory approval,

                2.5 Customer Testing Phase
                2.5.1 Interfaces
                In addition to the usual native and FIX trading gateways, customers will be able to use agentic artificial intelligence
                to connect to the LSE 24 system.

                2.5.1.1       Agentic AI
                Agentic AI refers to AI-enabled systems that can act on user-defined instructions to achieve specific outcomes,
                including interacting with market data, order management and execution workflows.
                Customers (Member firms) will be able to connect to LSE 24 via Claude or ChatGPT Large Language Models via
                the provided connectors.
                A new trader group must be requested for the AI agent which can then be used to submit RFQs or RFQ quotes.
                Further details on onboarding the AI agent will be provided via a separate guide.


                2.5.2 Orderbooks
                LSE 24 will only support the Request for Quote functionality2 for manual RFQs and Auto RFQs.

                2.5.2.1       Request for Quote (RFQ)
                The Private RFQ functionality will be available for client testing via all 3 interfaces. Please refer the LSE guides for
                further details on how RFQs work.
                The following types of RFQs will be available:
                          1) Named Manual RFQs
                             a) Select and Match
                             b) Best Execution
                          2) Named Auto RFQs
                             a) Winner Takes All


                2.5.3 Trading Sessions

                2.5.5 Bank Holidays and Early Close Days
                On the trading day preceding a UK Bank Holiday, LSE 24 CDS Phase 1 will close as normal at 14:20, it will then
                not re-open until 17:10 on the next trading day following the UK Bank Holiday.
                On an early close day, LSE 24 CDS Phase 1 will close as normal at 14:20, it will then not re-open until 17:10 on
                the next trading day.

                3.0 LSE 24 Market data
                3.1 Overview
                LSE 24 market data is published by a dedicated LSE 24 Group Ticker Plant using the MITCH protocol.
                Instruments are published across three channels – B, D and J. Each channel provides the Full Depth Incremental
                (FDI), Level 1 (TOB), Market By Order (MBO), Market By Price (MBP) and Post Trade (PT – MiFID post-trade)
                services, with Replay and Recovery services.


                3.2 LSE 24 identifiers in market data
                Table 4: LSE 24 identifiers in Group Ticker Plant messages

                  Trade                                   Venue of Execution            26 (XLOE)

                  MiFID Trade                             Venue of Execution            XLOE

                  MiFID Trade                             Trading Mode                  4 – Out of Main Session Trading


                The same values are used in messages sent in response to Replay, Recovery, Force Snapshot and order book
                republish requests.

                GTP Analytics will not be supported by LSE 24.

                4.2 Readiness checklist
                1.   Customers should differentiate LSE 24 activity from LSE core market activity. Note that both markets have the
                     same Instrument ID, ISIN and TIDM.
                2.   Customers should request for separate (from core LSE market) trader groups and compIDs.
                3.   Customers should be able to
                     a)      receive XLOE as a MIC in the LastMkt (30), MarketID (1301) and the Native Execution Venue fields.
                     b)      access the LSE 24 trading system from 17:00 to 15:00.
                     c)      send and receive an RFQ request and/or RFQ Quote.
                     d)      receive Group Ticker Plant messages with
                           i.    Source Venue 17 (LSE 24),
                          ii.    Venue of Execution 26 (XLOE)
                          iii.   MiFID Trading Mode 4 (Out of Main Session Trading)
