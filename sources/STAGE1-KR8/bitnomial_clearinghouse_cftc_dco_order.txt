                         AMENDED ORDER OF REGISTRATION

       On December 15, 2023, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (the

“Commission”) issued an order pursuant to Section 5b of the Commodity Exchange Act (the

“Act”), 7 U.S.C. § 7a-1, granting Bitnomial Clearinghouse, LLC (“Bitnomial”), registration as a

derivatives clearing organization (“DCO”) for the clearing of futures contracts and options on

futures contracts traded on or subject to the rules of a designated contract market (“DCM”).

       On August 27, 2025, Bitnomial requested that the Commission amend its order to remove

the language that provides Bitnomial may clear futures contracts and options on futures contracts

“traded on or subject to the rules of a [DCM]” and to permit Bitnomial to additionally clear fully

collateralized swaps. Having reviewed the complete record in this matter, including Bitnomial’s

application, all amendments thereto, and representations made by Bitnomial, the Commission

makes the following findings and rulings:

       WHEREAS Bitnomial represents that it meets all the requirements for registration as a

DCO under Section 5b of the Act, that it will comply with all core principles applicable to a

DCO under Section 5b of the Act (“Core Principles”) and the Commission’s regulations

thereunder, and that it will remain subject to and compliant with all provisions of the Act and the

Commission’s regulations applicable to a DCO;

       WHEREAS Bitnomial represents that it will ensure the performance of all self-regulatory

functions required of it as a registered DCO under the Act and Commission regulations,

including, without limitation: monitoring and enforcing clearing member compliance with

Bitnomial admission and continuing eligibility standards; and enforcing clearing member

compliance with the terms of all other Bitnomial rules, regulations, and procedures;

       WHEREAS Bitnomial represents that all funds held in any clearing member account (as

defined in Bitnomial’s rulebook) will be considered member property, as that term is defined in

the U.S. Bankruptcy Code, and it will at all times maintain funds of its clearing members

separate and distinct from its own funds;

       WHEREAS Commission staff reviewed and analyzed Bitnomial’s application for an

amendment to its registration as a DCO, including Bitnomial’s rules and all amendments thereto

and representations made by Bitnomial, and conducted a technical evaluation of Bitnomial’s

proposed operations to evaluate Bitnomial’s compliance with each of the relevant Core

Principles and corresponding regulations in accordance with Section 5b(c)(2)(A)(i) of the Act, 7

U.S.C. § 7a-1(c)(2)(A)(i);

       WHEREAS based on that review, the Commission concludes that Bitnomial’s

application, including all amendments thereto and representations made by Bitnomial,

demonstrates compliance with the applicable requirements of the Act and the Commission’s

regulations for registration as a DCO;

       WHEREAS this order is based upon the representations made and supporting material

provided to the Commission by Bitnomial and any changes to or omissions in the material facts

or circumstances pursuant to which this order is issued may require a new or amended order;

       The Commission FINDS that Bitnomial has demonstrated, as required by Section 5b of

the Act, 7 U.S.C. § 7a-1, and Commission Regulation 39.3(d), 17 C.F.R. § 39.3(d), that it

complies with the provisions set forth in the Act and the Commission’s regulations thereunder
