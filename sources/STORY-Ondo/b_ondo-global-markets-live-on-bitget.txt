Ondo Tokenized Stocks Are Now Live on Bitget’s CEX
Ondo Finance
Official Blog
Ondo Stocks
, the largest tokenized stock platform globally, is now integrated with 
Bitget
, one of the world's largest crypto exchanges. Ondo’s tokenized U.S. equities, index ETFs, and commodity-linked products are accessible alongside crypto assets for hundreds of millions of Bitget users worldwide. This marks the first time these tokenized securities are available for trading on Bitget’s CEX.
This first batch of Ondo’s tokenized securities available on Bitget include:
Stocks:
 Tesla (TSLAon), NVIDIA (NVDAon), Apple (AAPLon), Alphabet (GOOGLon), Microsoft (MSFTon), Amazon (AMZNon), Meta (METAon), and AMD (AMDon)
ETFs:
 SPDR S&P 500 ETF (SPYon), iShares Core S&P 500 ETF (IVVon), Invesco QQQ Trust (QQQon), iShares Russell 2000 ETF (IWMon), and iShares Core S&P Total U.S. Stock Market ETF (ITOTon)
Commodities:
 iShares Gold Trust (IAUon) and iShares Silver Trust (SLVon)
This integration builds on an existing relationship between Bitget and Ondo that has steadily expanded over the past several months. In September 2025, Bitget Wallet introduced access to more than 100 Ondo tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs, and Bitget then deepened that collaboration by introducing 98 additional assets.
“Bitget is now offering tokenized equities alongside crypto and becoming the everything app, powered by Ondo,” said Ian De Bode, President of Ondo Finance. “Ondo tokenized stocks, ETFs, and commodities are available for trading on every Bitget platform, in size.”
“Market movements are no longer bound by date and time, user expectations no longer stop at the boundary between crypto and traditional finance. This expansion with Ondo brings some of the world’s most watched equities, index products, and precious metals into our spot market in a way that feels native to how modern users already trade,” said Gracy Chen, CEO at Bitget.
Since launching in September 2025, Ondo Stocks has surpassed $600 million in TVL, driven over $12 billion in cumulative trading volume, and accumulated more than 30,000 holders. It holds more than 50% of the tokenized stock market by total value locked, exceeding every other platform combined and is supported by leading wallets, exchanges, custodians, and protocols worldwide.
