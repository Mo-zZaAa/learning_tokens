Welcome to the Monthly Spotlight from Ondo Finance
September marked a transformative month for Ondo and the digital asset space. Major milestones included launching Ondo Stocks with 100+ tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs, expanding the Ondo Stocks Alliance with partners like WisdomTree, appointing Peter Curley as Head of Global Regulatory Affairs, launching USDY on Stellar, and publishing an open letter to the World Federation of Exchanges on the future of tokenized securities.

Ondo Stocks Launches with 100+ Tokenized Stocks and ETFs
Ondo Finance launched Ondo Stocks, bringing 100+ tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs onchain starting on Ethereum, with hundreds more planned. The platform inherits liquidity from traditional finance while enabling DeFi composability. 
Read more
OUSG Becomes Anchor Investor in Fidelity's FDIT Tokenized Fund
Ondo Finance's OUSG fund became the sole anchor and largest investor in Fidelity's tokenized fund, Fidelity Digital Interest Token (FDIT), with diversified holdings across major asset managers including BlackRock, Franklin Templeton, and WisdomTree. 
Read more
Peter Curley Appointed Head of Global Regulatory Affairs
Ondo Finance appointed Peter Curley as Head of Global Regulatory Affairs. With experience at Coinbase, the SEC, U.S. Treasury, and Hong Kong Exchanges, Curley strengthens Ondo's regulatory leadership in tokenized assets. 
Read more
WisdomTree Joins Ondo Stocks Alliance
WisdomTree, a global asset manager with $131B in AUM, joined the Ondo Stocks Alliance to advance standards for tokenized securities and interoperability. 
Read more
USDY Launches on Stellar Network
Ondo's flagship yieldcoin, USDY, launched on the Stellar network, expanding global access to yield-bearing tokenized assets through Stellar's onchain finance infrastructure. 
Read more
Open Letter to the World Federation of Exchanges
Ondo Finance published an open letter to the World Federation of Exchanges advocating for technology-neutral regulation of tokenized securities. The letter distinguishes well-designed tokenized products from poorly conceived alternatives and calls for standards that enable integration with traditional market infrastructure.
