Ondo and Binance Bring Tokenized Securities to Hundreds of Millions of Users
Ondo Finance
Official Blog
We’re excited to announce that 
Ondo Stocks
, the largest tokenized stock platform globally, is now live on 
Binance
, the world’s largest crypto exchange. Ondo tokenized U.S. securities are now accessible via Binance Alpha, making them seamlessly accessible to hundreds of millions of Binance users. This marks one of the largest expansions to date in global access to tokenized stocks and a major step in bringing traditional securities onchain.
To start, ten Ondo tokenized securities are now available through Binance Alpha, including: AAPLon, GOOGLon, TSLAon, NVDAon, QQQon, CRCLon, METAon, AMZNon, SPYon, and MSFTon.
Hundreds of Millions of Binance Users Can Now Access Ondo Tokenized Securities
Already accessible to millions of users on 
BNB Chain
 and Binance Wallet, expanding access to Binance Alpha makes Ondo tokenized securities directly available to hundreds of millions of 
Binance.com
 users. Ondo Stocks’ securities preserve close alignment with TradFi markets while unlocking the accessibility and composability of crypto markets.
Bringing Together the Largest Platforms for Crypto Trading & Tokenized Stocks
This integration brings together two industry leaders: Binance, the largest global crypto exchange, and Ondo Stocks, the world’s largest platform for tokenized stocks. Since launching in September 2025, Ondo Stocks has surpassed $550 million in total value locked and driven over $11 billion in cumulative trading volume across tens of thousands of users.
Binance VP of Product, Jeff Li:
“Binance is committed to maintaining the highest infrastructure and regulatory standards as we bring tokenized securities into our ecosystem, which is why we’re excited to integrate Ondo Stocks. With Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs available through Binance Alpha, hundreds of millions of users can now gain exposure to U.S. markets onchain.”
Ondo Finance President Ian De Bode:
“We’re excited to bring Ondo Stocks to hundreds of millions of Binance users, providing seamless access to leading tokenized U.S. equities and ETFs. By featuring Ondo on the world’s leading crypto exchange, the stage is now set for the mainstream global adoption of tokenized stocks.”
Neither Binance nor Ondo tokenized stocks and ETFs are available in the U.S.
About Ondo Stocks
Ondo Stocks is an issuance and redemption platform for tokenized publicly traded U.S. stocks and ETFs. It enables investors outside the United States to gain economic exposure to these assets by minting, transferring, and redeeming securities-backed tokens. Each token is fully backed by the corresponding stock or ETF (together with cash in transit).
About Ondo Finance
Ondo Finance is a blockchain-based platform focused on tokenizing real-world assets and bringing institutional-quality financial products onchain. By bridging traditional finance and decentralized infrastructure, Ondo aims to make capital markets more accessible, transparent, and efficient.
