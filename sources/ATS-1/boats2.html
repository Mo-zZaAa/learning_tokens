Revolutionizing Equities Trading

Our Vision: Empowering global investors to better manage their holdings in US stocks by providing unique data and technology solutions to provide investors extended trading hours via our Blue Ocean ATS Session service. Global clients can trade US National Market System stocks between 8:00 PM and 4:00 AM ET, (Sunday – Thursday).

Our Mission: To expand trading, enabling investors to trade stocks 24-hours, moving away from legacy New York Eastern Time market hours. Blue Ocean ATS is the first alternative trading system to expand market hours, filling the gap to trade equities continuously throughout US evening hours.

Unlock Extended Trading Hours

Access U.S. markets beyond traditional market hours, tapping into a 24-hour trading cycle that aligns with global market movements. Our innovative platform empowers investors to seize opportunities, manage risk, and react to news in real-time, regardless of time zones.

Redefining US Trading Hours

Blue Ocean ATS is a Blue Ocean Technologies subsidiary and operator of the alternative trading system. Blue Ocean ATS (“BOATS”) uses electronic order-delivery and live data to provide a unique market center trading experience for investors, bridging the eight-hour time gap in US equities trading.
