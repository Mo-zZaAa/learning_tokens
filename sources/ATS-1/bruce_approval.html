Redefining Overnight Liquidity

Bruce Markets operates Bruce ATS, a U.S. equities Alternative Trading System designed for the overnight session. Built on exchange-grade technology and guided by experienced market operators, Bruce Markets delivers the structure, transparency and resilience typically reserved for core market hours. Backed by leading firms across the trading ecosystem, the platform provides brokers and investors with a competitive source of after-hours liquidity and a seamless bridge between the U.S. after-hours and pre-market sessions. As trading continues to evolve, Bruce Markets is helping raise the standard for trading beyond traditional market hours.
