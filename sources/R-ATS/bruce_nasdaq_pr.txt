The partnership delivers a seamless data integration solution that enables brokers and traders to effortlessly access Bruce ATS™’ data through Nasdaq’s proven data delivery connections. By leveraging Nasdaq’s established distribution network, market participants can now incorporate Bruce’s market data into their platforms—minimizing complex implementation requirements while gaining immediate access to comprehensive after-hours market insights. Bruce ATS™ is redefining after-hours trading by providing secure and reliable access from 8:00 p.m. to 4:00 a.m. ET, Sunday through Friday morning. This partnership empowers financial institutions and investors with added visibility into non-traditional trading hours activity – helping to drive greater confidence and clarity in decision-making. New Features Include: Bruce Last Sale: Real-time data feed providing complete and up-to-date trade information for all securities traded on Bruce ATS™, delivering actionable insights from 8:00 p.m. to 4:00 a.m. ET. Bruce Best Bid and Offer: Real-time snapshots of liquidity with proprietary data, tracking the best bid and offer on the Bruce ATS™ throughout the overnight trading session. Bruce Depth of Book: Extends complete order book visibility by displaying every order at every price level in the Bruce order book. Seamless Global Distribution – Market data clients will be able to consume Bruce ATS™’ market data through Nasdaq data distribution services, data vendors, or via a direct connection to the Bruce ATS™ matching engine. “After-hours trading requires an unparalleled level of reliability, transparency, and accessibility. Our partnership with Nasdaq underscores Bruce ATS™’ commitment to delivering that standard,” said Jason Wallach, CEO of Bruce Markets. “With Nasdaq as our exclusive global data administrator, we’re making it incredibly simple for every broker and investment firm worldwide who already uses Nasdaq data to seamlessly integrate Bruce data into their existing platforms. This provides firms and investors with immediate access to optimize their overnight trading performance without complex implementation hurdles.” “As markets continue to evolve toward 24-hour activity, access to reliable and accurate data is paramount,” said Garrick Stavrovich, VP and Head of Product at Nasdaq. “This collaboration highlights Nasdaq’s commitment to empowering innovative platforms like Bruce ATS™ by providing robust data technology that instills trust and helps reduce uncertainty and enable smarter trading decisions. Together, we remain at the forefront of the markets’ evolutions towards overnight trading and excellence in data-driven insights.” Bruce Markets is proud to continue its mission of offering enterprise-grade overnight trading solutions supported by leading technology from Nasdaq. By aligning with Nasdaq’s data capabilities, Bruce ATS™ sets a new benchmark for operational excellence and transparency in the overnight market. For more information, visit: https://data.nasdaq.com/bruceats About Bruce ATS ™ Bruce ATS™, operated by SEC and FINRA-registered broker-dealer Bruce Markets™, is an Alternative Trading System offering secure overnight access to U.S. markets. Bruce ATS™ provides financial institutions with seamless integration and scalable solutions. Designed for reliability and performance at enterprise scale, Bruce ATS™ redefines after-hours trading by bridging the gap between investors and U.S. markets, empowering firms to extend trading opportunities beyond traditional hours with confidence and ease.
